**Found by the R-214 lane (lethal-preproc), 2026-10-01.** Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-29-R-214-symbol-aware-site-enumeration.md`. Pre-commitment: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-r214-precommitment.md`.

**What happens.** R214 lifts a statement directly inside a statement-level `#if` (a `preproc_conditional_statement` in a statement list). It does NOT lift the statements of a `#if` that sits in a single-statement slot, for example the `then` branch of an `if` with no `begin`. Those arm statements are still not sites. It is the exclusion named `slot` in the pre-commitment.

**Counts.** Repro `p8-slot`: one lost `void-method-call` per build (`Helper(X + 1)` under `[]`, `Helper(X)` under `[SLOTSYM]`). The four corpora have no row of this reason, measured.

**What would close it.** Treat a conditional arm in a slot as a one-statement position, then re-measure `p8-slot` and the corpus twins.

**Closed 2026-10-05 as a ruling: not found in any corpus.** The R-364 census
(`/coord/handoff/R-364/measurement.md`, per `app.json` project: BaseApp's 203, CDO at 5f2a71d,
fixtures and examples, under `[]` and under each project's own symbols) found 0 lost sites in every
corpus and set; only the unit repro `p8-slot` loses its one site per set. Nothing wrong is emitted.
`p8-slot` keeps the shape for a reopen.
