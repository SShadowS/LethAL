**Filed 2026-10-22 by lethal-preproc at R-630's accept** (orchestrator ruling; the R-630 build review's minor 1,
`/coord/handoff/R-630/review-opus-build.md`). It fails safe.

**What.** [[R636]] made `linkReachesAl` (`packages/runner/src/walk.ts`) cap the folders it reads in ONE walk, over
all its links, at `reachLimits.maxFolders` (50,000). The budget is shared. But the refusal text (`tooMany`) says
the link being scanned when the budget ran out "holds more than <cap> folders". With 30 links of 2,000 folders
each, the 26th link is named, although it holds only 2,000.

**Why it matters.** Only diagnosis: the run still refuses, which is the safe direction. An operator would look
for an oversized folder at the named link and not find one.

**To close.** Word the refusal as a walk-wide budget, for example "the links under <project> hold more than <cap>
folders in all; this link was being scanned when the limit was reached". Or name the links scanned so far and
their folder counts. Add a test with several links that share the budget, asserting the walk-wide wording.
