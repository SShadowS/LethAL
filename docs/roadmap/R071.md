**`SwapRecXRec`, scoped to `OnValidate`/`OnRename` — the signal is measured, the operator is not built.** R33's experiment answered only the `Modify(true)` shape (`differ=No`) and the conclusion drawn was a blanket no-go. Follow-up probes on Cronus281 (fenced path, `fixtures/sandbox-probes/src/Tier2Phase2Probe.Codeunit.al`) measured the two shapes it never touched: field `OnValidate` driven by `Validate(Amount, 250)` gives `rec=250 | xrec=100 | differ=YES`, and `OnRename` driven by `Rename('R2')` gives `rec.No=R2 | xrec.No=R1 | differ=YES`. Those are exactly the triggers where AL reads `xRec` (`if F <> xRec.F then` change detection; old-vs-new key), so swapping them is observable and killable. What the operator needs beyond the site predicate: it must NOT claim `OnModify`-shaped sites (measured equivalent — a survivor there means nothing), which makes it the first Tier-2 operator whose targeting depends on the enclosing TRIGGER KIND rather than on the receiver.
