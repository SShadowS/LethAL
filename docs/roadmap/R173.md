## What breaks, and for whom

A mutant that never returns strands its tier and blocks every mutant behind it, on the default path
where `--stop-hung-sessions` is off because it ends a session on the user's own server. [[R164]]
fixed that for the shape it measured, `negate-conditional` at a `repeat` exit condition, and left the
rest standing on purpose.

## The residual, measured on `do-rel2/Cloud`

`loop-truncate` now owns the `repeat` exit condition and cannot hang there. Everything below still
claims a loop condition and can in principle produce a non-terminating mutant:

| where | operator | sites |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `repeat` exit condition | `conditional-boundary` | 8 |
| `repeat` exit condition | `remove-not` | 8 |
| `repeat` exit condition | `toggle-blank-string` | 3 |
| nested inside a `repeat` condition | `negate-conditional` | 1 |
| `while` condition | `conditional-boundary` | 16 |
| `while` condition | `negate-conditional` | 11 |
| `while` condition | `remove-not` | 8 |
| `while` condition | `toggle-blank-string` | 2 |

**57 sites**, against the 326 R164 removed. Evidence:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-26-r164-loop-truncate-design.md` §2 and §4.

## Why R164 stopped where it did, so this is not read as an oversight

Two reasons, both of which still hold and either of which could change with measurement.

1. **A boundary shift is not a negation.** `until A >= B` becoming `until A > B` usually runs a loop
   one EXTRA iteration and then finishes; the negation of an exhaustion test runs forever. The
   hazard is plausible for these three, not measured, and R164's cession was justified by a
   measurement (`itest:hang` scored the negated mutant `timeout-killed` in stage 1).
2. **Ceding costs real mutants.** `itest:hang`'s M0005 is `conditional-boundary` on
   `until Counter >= Limit`. It terminates, it is killed, and it is the CONTROL that proves R164's
   cession was not too broad. Deleting a working mutant to prevent a hypothetical hang is a cost
   against no measured benefit.

## `while` is the larger half and has no operator to cede to

37 of the 57 sites are `while` conditions, and `loop-truncate` deliberately does not claim them:
`while false` runs the body ZERO times, which is a different mutation from "run it once" and has its
own overlap question against `empty-block`. So ceding at `while` today would leave those sites with
NO mutant at all, which is strictly worse than a hang risk nobody has measured.

## What to do, in order

1. **Measure before ceding anything**, the way R164 did. Build a `sandbox-hang` arm per claimant and
   see which of the three actually strands. Three arms is cheap and the gate is built for it.
2. **Decide `while` on its own terms.** Either a `loop-skip` operator (`while false`), which gives
   the `while` sites somewhere to cede TO, or a ruling that they stay claimed and the risk is
   accepted and documented.
3. Only then widen the cession, and only for claimants a run showed stranding.

None of this is started. What exists is the count, and the reason R164 did not act on it.

## MEASURED 2026-08-27, and the row's own plan was the weaker one

This row said to build a `sandbox-hang` arm per claimant and see which strands. That was started and
ABANDONED on purpose: the three loops had to be written so that they WOULD strand, which
demonstrates a hang is constructible rather than that the residual risk is real. Those are different
claims and only the second matters.

Censusing the REAL corpus instead answers it, and the answer is much sharper than the row assumed.

**First correction: the count.** 57 counted claims ANYWHERE inside a loop condition, including
nested ones. What a cession would actually move is the EXACT-SPAN claim, and there are **34**:

| operator | at `while` | at `repeat` |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `negate-conditional` | 11 | 0 (R164 ceded these) |
| `conditional-boundary` | 10 | 2 |
| `remove-not` | 8 | 3 |
| `toggle-blank-string` | **0** | **0** |

**`toggle-blank-string` drops out entirely.** Its three claims from the earlier census were all
nested, so it never claims a whole loop condition and cannot be ceded at one.

**`remove-not` is dominated by one safe shape.** Eight of its eleven are `while not ReadStream.EOS`.
Mutated to `while ReadStream.EOS` the loop body never runs at all (EOS is false at the start), so it
terminates immediately. That is an ordinary behaviour change and a good mutant, not a hang.

## The real risk: 7 sites, one operator, one idiom

`conditional-boundary` flips `>` to `>=`. Seven of its twelve exact-span claims are
`while <position> > 0`:

```
while StrPos(String, Find) > 0        (x2)
while StrPos(ArrayAsText, ',') > 0
while Pos > 0                         (x2)
while BodyPos > 0
while HtmlPos > 0
```

`StrPos` and friends return **0** when there is nothing left to find. Mutated to `>= 0` the test is
true forever and the loop never ends. This is the SAME root pattern as [[R164]]'s canonical shape,
an exhaustion sentinel of 0 that the mutation makes permanently satisfied, reached through a
different operator. It is also the most ordinary string-scanning idiom in AL, so the count will be
higher on a text-heavy app than on this one.

The remaining five are safe by inspection and by shape: `StrPos(...) > 1` becomes `>= 1`, which is
false at 0; the two date comparisons and `BatchCounter < CommitBatchSize` all still terminate.

## Why nothing can be ceded, which is the hard part

All seven are at `while`. `loop-truncate` is scoped to `repeat`, deliberately, because `while false`
runs the body ZERO times and is a different mutation with its own overlap question against
`empty-block`. So there is no operator to cede these to today, and ceding them to nothing would
delete seven mutants on live string-processing logic and give back nothing.

## What to do, revised

1. **Decide `while` on its own terms first.** A `loop-skip` operator (`while <cond>` -> `while
   false`) would give these somewhere to cede TO, and it needs its own sizing and overlap
   measurement against `empty-block`. That is the blocking question, not the cession.
2. **Or refuse the shape rather than the position.** `conditional-boundary` could decline `> 0` ->
   `>= 0` specifically, where the left operand is a call or variable that an exhaustion idiom drives
   to 0. That is narrow and needs no new operator, but it is a heuristic about VALUES and this
   codebase has been repeatedly burned by exactly that kind of inference (R175 most recently), so it
   would need a measured false-refusal rate before shipping.
3. Only then widen anything.
