## What breaks, and for whom

A mutant that never returns strands its tier and blocks every mutant behind it, on the default path
where `--stop-hung-sessions` is off because it ends a session on the user's own server. [[R164]]
fixed that for the shape it measured, `negate-conditional` at a `repeat` exit condition, and left the
rest standing on purpose.

## The residual, measured on `do-rel2/Cloud`

`loop-truncate` now owns the `repeat` exit condition and cannot hang there. Everything below still
claims a loop condition and can in principle produce a non-terminating mutant:

| where | operator | sites |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `repeat` exit condition | `conditional-boundary` | 8 |
| `repeat` exit condition | `remove-not` | 8 |
| `repeat` exit condition | `toggle-blank-string` | 3 |
| nested inside a `repeat` condition | `negate-conditional` | 1 |
| `while` condition | `conditional-boundary` | 16 |
| `while` condition | `negate-conditional` | 11 |
| `while` condition | `remove-not` | 8 |
| `while` condition | `toggle-blank-string` | 2 |

**57 sites**, against the 326 R164 removed. Evidence:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-26-r164-loop-truncate-design.md` §2 and §4.

## Why R164 stopped where it did, so this is not read as an oversight

Two reasons, both of which still hold and either of which could change with measurement.

1. **A boundary shift is not a negation.** `until A >= B` becoming `until A > B` usually runs a loop
   one EXTRA iteration and then finishes; the negation of an exhaustion test runs forever. The
   hazard is plausible for these three, not measured, and R164's cession was justified by a
   measurement (`itest:hang` scored the negated mutant `timeout-killed` in stage 1).
2. **Ceding costs real mutants.** `itest:hang`'s M0005 is `conditional-boundary` on
   `until Counter >= Limit`. It terminates, it is killed, and it is the CONTROL that proves R164's
   cession was not too broad. Deleting a working mutant to prevent a hypothetical hang is a cost
   against no measured benefit.

## `while` is the larger half and has no operator to cede to

37 of the 57 sites are `while` conditions, and `loop-truncate` deliberately does not claim them:
`while false` runs the body ZERO times, which is a different mutation from "run it once" and has its
own overlap question against `empty-block`. So ceding at `while` today would leave those sites with
NO mutant at all, which is strictly worse than a hang risk nobody has measured.

## What to do, in order

1. **Measure before ceding anything**, the way R164 did. Build a `sandbox-hang` arm per claimant and
   see which of the three actually strands. Three arms is cheap and the gate is built for it.
2. **Decide `while` on its own terms.** Either a `loop-skip` operator (`while false`), which gives
   the `while` sites somewhere to cede TO, or a ruling that they stay claimed and the risk is
   accepted and documented.
3. Only then widen the cession, and only for claimants a run showed stranding.

None of this is started. What exists is the count, and the reason R164 did not act on it.
