**Found by the R-214 audit (lethal-preproc, 2026-09-29).** Evidence:
`H:/lethal-coord/tasks/R-214/audit.md`, new finding 1. The probe is
`H:/lethal-scratch/R-214/a/probe.ts`: it runs `generateMutationSet`, then an offline
`writeInstrumentedProject`, and reads `reachGrain` from the manifest.

**What happens.** `packages/runner/tests/reach-grain-fixtures.test.ts` fails on any `unplaced`
entry. Its reason is that an unplaced grain is a shape no placement rule covers. But its `PROJECTS`
list names only `sandbox-app`, `sandbox-data`, `sandbox-hang`, `gift-card` and `credit-limit`.
`fixtures/sandbox-symbols`, the R321 fixture, yields `M0008` to `M0013` (lines 13, 15 and 17) all at
`reachGrain=unplaced`. They are statements directly inside a `preproc_conditional_statement`, and
`placeReach` (in `packages/schemata/src/dispatch.ts`) has no rule for them. A survivor there reads
`not-decided` rather than `covered-but-unreached` (R306 case 3 predicted statement grain; that
prediction is wrong).

**Fix.** Add `fixtures/sandbox-symbols` to `PROJECTS`. The test then goes red until `placeReach`
gains a rule for a statement in `preproc_conditional_statement`. This belongs with the R214 plan,
because the same parent kind is the under-claim in `isStatementPosition`.
