Filed by lethal-bugs on the orchestrator's word (2026-11-21), from the maintainer's update relayed by kraken.

- **Upstream's promise:** "the daemon is clean after ANY notRun refusal" becomes a documented promise of al-runner once their
  state-leaking refusal fix and a CI comparison test land. Timeouts, panics and protocol errors are excluded; #8334 covers timeouts.
- **LethAL today:** LethAL closes a serve daemon after a refusal unless the refusal is on its per-class keep list
  (`V3_SERVE_SAFE_REFUSALS`). PR-244 declined option 4 (keep the daemon after any refusal) for lack of that promise.
- **When the promise is on edge:**
  - Replace the per-class keep list with one rule: keep the daemon after any notRun refusal.
  - Timeouts, panics and protocol errors still close it.
  - Prove it on DO with our own kept-vs-fresh check on `current`, in the run's isolation mode (PR-195/PR-214's tool), before the
    rule ships.
  - Revisit PR-244's option 4 on that evidence.
- **Decision recorded (orchestrator, 2026-11-21):** do NOT add PR-245's six clean classes to the keep list now, although
  alrunner's daemon clean check found the daemon clean after each (c03-c07 and c09, posted on #8334).
  - The gain now is small: the combined edge run had only 25 refusal closes.
  - Per-class keep entries have a history of never matching (the 12 dead entries).
  - So wait for #8180: one rule for every refusal kind, proved on DO.
- **Watch:** lethal-bugs' daily al-runner update reads the fixed-issue scan's line for #8180 and tells the orchestrator when a build
  named there is on edge or `current`.
