**What happens.** `laneMayStart` (al-runner-v3-backend.ts, PR-98's lane gate) lets lane k start only while the lanes it charges fit
under the declared heavy-slot peak: each charged lane at the per-mode sampled peak, plus 4.0 GiB for LethAL. When it refuses, it logs
`al-runner-v3-lane-memory-hold` once and "this session stays at the lanes already running".

**Measured on the DO cross-build windows** (edge-4ebfe7913, `--workers 3`, declared `--peak 15 baseapp`; `/coord/handoff/R-984/`):

| window | hold | needed (charged) | declared | real tree peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| x1 | lane 1 does not start | 15.3 GiB | 15 GiB | 12.37 GiB |
| x2 | lane 1 does not start | 15.8 GiB | 15 GiB | 11.85 GiB |
| x3 | lane 1 does not start (2,276 s into the mutant phase) | 16.2 GiB (3 x 4.1 + 4.0) | 15 GiB | 12.59 GiB |

t14 (847b1fa5a, same 15 GiB) logged no hold. In x3, PR-121's offline `gaps.ts` over the events shows lanes 1 and 2 idle after their
last request for 10,605 s and 10,589 s, **85% of the 12,448 s mutant phase**, while lane 0 (busy 98.7%) carried 110 distinct
codeunits after the hold (splittable work, not one giant codeunit). No lane restarted after the hold for the rest of the session.
x1 and x2's events carry no `atMs`, so their idle time cannot be measured, but they logged the same hold. x3 scored 4.25 s per covered
mutant against x2's 3.28 s, and the idle lanes are the second-largest cause after heavier mutants (result.md, x3's breakdown).

**Two defects:**
1. **The charge over-estimates.** The sampled peak per lane times the lanes, plus 4.0 GiB, came to 16.2 GiB where the whole tree
   peaked at 12.6 GiB, about 3.6 GiB too high. A declared peak that matches the measured tree therefore holds lanes back.
2. **A hold is permanent in practice.** After the one notice the session runs on the remaining lanes for the rest of the window;
   nothing reports that the window is now one-lane (R1119: TIMING has no lane-idle figure).

**Workaround in use:** DO window x4 declares `--peak 18 baseapp` (watchdog at 20 GiB), pre-committed to log 0 lane-memory-hold lines.

**To close.** A charge that a measured tree peak bears out (or a re-check that re-admits a lane once the charge fits), and a
pre-committed DO window at the old declaration (15 GiB) that keeps 3 lanes busy, measured per lane with `gaps.ts` or R1119's figure.

**Correction (coord PR-140, lethal-code, 2026-11-18, after an Opus spec-adversary).** Defect 1 does not hold. The charge is honest:
- **Per part, in x3:** LethAL's own process reached 3.86 GiB (x1: 3.91), and single daemons reached 4.06 GiB in the mutant phase
  (the baseline daemon reached 4.80). The parts' worst case is about 16 GiB, against the model's 16.2.
- **Why the tree was lower:** its 12.6 GiB peak came in under that only because the parts did not peak at the same moment.
- **So:** 15 GiB is too low for 3 lanes of this DO work, and x4's 18 GiB is the honest declaration. The charge stays (no crash before
  speed).

**The real defect is defect 2, and its cause:**
- **The cause:** at a window start, the daemons of lanes 1 and 2 are still resident from the previous window. Lane 2's is charged
  on top of lanes 0 and 1, and lane 1 is refused ("3 lanes charged").
- **Why it never lifts:** `tryLaunch` stops at the refusal, so lane 2 is never asked. The charge never falls: the resident lane stays
  charged, and the peak only rises.
- **What the idle daemons cost:** they hold memory, or swap (x3: their RSS fell to about 0.15 GiB after 23:50), and do no work for
  the rest of the window.
- **The fix (PR-140, spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-pr140-idle-lane-close.md`):** a hold closes the charged daemons of lanes
  that are not running (close reason `lane-idle`), then asks again. At 15 GiB, x3's numbers then give 2 working lanes instead of
  1; 3 lanes need the honest declaration.
- **The "To close" bar above changes:** the DO window at 15 GiB must keep at least 2 lanes busy after a hold, not 3.
