**Found by** coord PR-349 (lethal-code, 2026-11-22), re-measuring R1099, from c2's `serve-request-timed` events (full DO,
main-ccd0f823d; `/coord/handoff/PR-349/floor.txt`).

**The numbers (c2, summed over lanes):**

| kind | requests | wall | inside tests | outside tests | its largest part |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| warm-up | 521 | 7,784 s | 1,588 s | 6,196 s (79.6%) | compile 5,971 s |
| live | 3,262 | 7,466 s | 7,028 s | 439 s (5.9%) | setup 152 s |
| prefetch | 873 | 40,479 s | 40,230 s | 249 s (0.6%) | setup 164 s |

- **So outside the tests, the warm-ups' compile is 5,971 s of 7,353 s.** That is 10.6% of the 56,248 s of serve wall.
- **c2's daemons closed 168 times:** 146 refusals, 21 timeouts and 1 unreadable answer. Each close means a new daemon
  and its warm-up. R1147 covers only the timeout restarts (y3: 23, 1,107.6 s).
- **Not attributed yet:** 521 warm-ups against 168 closes plus the first daemon of each lane. Some daemons may warm up
  more than once (a mode change, or a window's union warm-up). That is the first thing to measure.
- **t1** (PR-302, the same build with `refusedCutTail` on): 522 warm-ups, compile 4,908 s. Its live requests also compiled
  5,430 s over 33,616 requests (the tail path), so that cost is the flag's, not the warm-ups'.

**The levers, each to be sized:**
- fewer refusal restarts: the keep list, R1152 and R1153;
- keeping the daemon after a timeout: R1147;
- a cheaper warm-up compile (upstream: R1029's one compile per daemon);
- not warming up twice per daemon, if that happens.

**Related:** R1099 (closed), R1147, R1152, R1153, R1029, R1182.
