**Found by** PR-443's spec-adversary (S8; lethal-code, 2026-11-25). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-443/` (`premise-v2.md`,
`probe443b.ts`).

- **The door.** On a mutant daemon with replay on:
  - NONE always replays the image recorded at warm-up.
  - A mutant's first entry logs in for real (its key, the resource texts, is new). Its later entries replay its own
    image.
  - So any login input that is NOT in the key can split a mutant from its NONE confirm. Example (the fact cannot cross
    midnight): the warm-up runs on the last day of a month and records a Today-derived WorkDate in NONE's image. A
    mutant then logs in after midnight. A date-window test fails for the mutant and passes in the NONE confirm, which
    replays the old date. A kill is scored that a replay-off run would refuse, because its confirm would also run on
    the new day.
  - No leg-C fact can close this. The fact (PR-443) only shows that replay keeps answers equal on its corpus.
- **The fix shape** (the orchestrator's): every kill already gets a kill pair (R653: the M re-run plus the NONE twin, one
  request). Run those pairs on a replay-OFF daemon, so a kill only ever stands on `=0` evidence. Replay can then only
  lose kills (the safe direction): the prefetch's kill is just a candidate, and the pair confirms or refutes it at
  `=0`.
- **Sized on c5** (PR-422; 6,178 kill pairs, 6,013 batched by R778; prices from PR-438/PR-439: a test entry ~611 ms, an
  echo ~294 ms, a real login ~250 ms):
  - **Form A, the pairs on a dedicated `=0` daemon per lane** (the orchestrator's first choice):
    - 0 extra entries. Each pair's 4 entries (2 test, 2 echo) log in for real instead of possibly replaying: at most
      4 × ~0.25 s ≈ 1.0 s per pair of replay saving given up, ≤ ~6,200 lane-s (~6% of the mutants phase) on c5.
    - Memory: one more daemon per lane. c5's lanes measured ~3.8 GiB each, and the memory hold already capped c5 at 5
      of 8 lanes, so a second daemon per lane likely costs a lane.
    - Variant: one `=0` pair daemon shared by all lanes, serialised: ~3.8 GiB once. Its throughput limit: 6,178 pairs
      × ~1.8 s ≈ 11,100 s on one daemon, against the 19,758 s mutants phase.
  - **Form B, the pairs on the recheck path** (the withheld-kill recheck already forces replay off, PR-402 F1):
    - It runs under METHOD isolation (each test alone), not the kill's own context. A kill that depends on its codeunit's
      prefix (R1141's order dependence) would fail to confirm: a lost kill, a changed context.
    - It also moves the pairs out of the batch into the nested session (R1190's costs: its own generate, deploy and
      baseline, about 125 s on c5).
    - Not recommended unless Form A's memory cannot be found.
  - **Form C, a second `=0` re-confirm on top of today's pair** (the reviewer's first shape): +2 test entries and
    +2 echoes per kill, ~1.8 s × 6,185 ≈ 11,100 lane-s (~11%), plus a `=0` daemon anyway. Dominated by Form A.
- **Next:** pre-commit Form A (shared or per lane, by measured memory). Red tests: a kill whose pair would pass at `=1`
  but fail at `=0` is not scored. PR-402's guard then requires this item's flag and the fact. Measure on DO with
  PR-443's step 2.
