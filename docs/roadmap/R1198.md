Found by coord PR-347 (lethal-preproc, 2026-11-23). Evidence: PR-344's DO re-check (`/coord/handoff/PR-344/recheck/result.md`:
arm A sent 0 tail packs, with 1 stable tail test), and TC's first record attempt (`/coord/handoff/PR-347/tc-record/v3-gate.log`:
`TC recorded: TC-on pack stats packs 0`). Related: [[R1156]], [[R1160]], PR-344.

**What happens.** A pack's mates are the walked mutant's OTHER covering tail tests (`tailPackMates`). TC's mutants are each
covered by one stable tail test (Double by T1, IsBig by T2, IsSmall by T5), and so is DO's 68874 after PR-365. So no pack is
ever sent. Packing stays covered by 24 unit tests (`pr344-pack-tail.test.ts`) and changed no verdict on DO (175/175), but it
has never run live.

**To do.** A v3-only fixture leg (or a TC variant with its own baseline) where one mutant is covered by two or more stable
tail tests after a refused one. The judge then asserts `tail packs sent > 0` and TC-on equal to the alone truth. TC's own
table is not changed for this; it would need its own pre-commitment.
