Measured by coord PR-106 (lethal-preproc, 2026-11-17, measure only; `/coord/handoff/PR-106/result.md`), on DO run 13 of
R-984 (window t12, 481 error verdicts) and a 60-mutant slice of it.

**What the errors are.** All 481 are al-runner `NotRun` refusals; none is a LethAL transport or guard text.
- **268 (56%) are kills al-runner withholds.**
  - The covering test fails under the mutant. An earlier, non-covering test of its codeunit was refused (it did not run),
    and al-runner reports a later failure as `NotRun` ("this failure is not trusted") but keeps a later pass.
  - The earlier test was refused at baseline too, and the covering test passed there. So the kill can never be scored
    under isolation "test".
  - The roots of the earlier refusals: #7775 (FindSet on Company, 81), #8002 (number sequences, 63), a GoToKey on a
    descending list (96), and others.
- **213 are direct refusals** in the covering test; their filing state is in the result file. Three new ones are
  reduced in upstream #8213 and added to the refusal ledger.

**The LethAL side.** DO's config forces `"isolation": "test"` (R-942 amendment 3), because the auto re-baseline was
killed by the memory watchdog at 20 GB on the one-shot transport then (R970).
- The slice (60 mutants: 39 of the withheld kills, 21 survivors) at N = 1 under "method": 39 of 39 errors became kills (33
  of the 35 joined by `ast_hash` with exactly the withheld assertion message), and the 21 survivors stayed survivors.
- **Method is not a global answer.** On the whole DO baseline it moved 156 tests (149 error to pass, 3 fail to pass,
  3 error to fail, 1 pass to fail: `CDO Variant Setup UI Tests.U_CopyCriteriaFromLine_...` needs an earlier test's state).
- **Under R921's per-codeunit auto rule**, applied to the two measured baselines, 31 of 110 test codeunits move to method.
  They hold **163 of the 268** withheld kills. The other 105 stay on test: 97 in `CDO Variant Setup UI Tests` (it would
  lose a pass), 5 in `CDO Get Report Params Tests`, 3 in `CDO Variant Matching Tests`.
- **The cost.** The method baseline took 3,668 s against 318 s on serve (peak 4.55 GiB; the 20 GB was the one-shot path).
  The slice's mutant phase took 214 s against 47 s. Per [[R1099]], most of a session's cost is the per-session company
  login (#8211), so auto's re-baseline gets cheaper if #8211 is fixed.

**To close.** Either:
- run DO under isolation "auto" on serve and measure the re-baseline's time and peak on the whole app, a pre-committed
  window: the 163 should score, and every other verdict should be identical outside the 31 codeunits; or
- rule that the time is not worth it until #8211 lands.

The 105 left need al-runner to treat a pass and a failure after a refused test the same way: upstream #8218 (a follow-up
to #5610 and #5961).
