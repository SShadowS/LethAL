**Filed 2026-10-08 by the [[R500]] build** (survey `/coord/handoff/R-500/survey.md`, shape 8; plan r3 ruling on shape
8). Product-wide, not only in reports. A plausible hang that is NOT refused.

**What.** A `while`/`repeat` whose condition is a `Find`/`FindFirst`/`IsEmpty` on a record and which ends only
because its body consumes that set (`Delete`, `DeleteAll`, or a `Modify` of a filtered field) loops forever when a
mutant removes or weakens the consuming call. R196/R446/R480 read the condition and its guards, not progress made
through a call ([[R196]]'s "progress through a call" exclusion), so these are emitted everywhere. In R500's sample
of loops inside bounded report items, **1 of 12 was a certain hang** of this shape (`FA Posting Group - Net
Change`: a loop that runs while a buffer's first row can be found and ends only because its body deletes the rows;
removing the `DeleteAll` hangs on any non-empty buffer).

R500 ruled the bounded report item adds nothing here: the inner loop is an ordinary AL loop, the same as in any
codeunit, so the fix belongs in the loop rules, not in the report rules.

**To close.** Census the shape product-wide (the cursor-method condition plus a consuming call on the same
receiver in the body), read a sample, then refuse the consuming call's removal in such loops or close it with a
ruling.
