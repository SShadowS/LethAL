Filed by coord PR-125 (lethal-preproc, 2026-11-17), measure only. Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-125/result.md`.
- **Why:**
  - On DO 735fb42's whole project, batched, the 9 cross-check one-shots summed 199-250 s, against 132-189 s of daemon
    requests. The baseline phase was 231-288 s.
  - [[R1107]] allows at most one sample in flight, and the fill settles it before starting the next. Each one-shot (12-14 s
    quiet: a 7 s workspace compile, 4.4 s of coverage, 0.2 s of tests) outlasts the daemon requests between two samples, so
    the daemon waits.
- **The idea:** allow k = 2 in flight. Admission as R1107's, with the in-flight sizes summed:
  `used + held x E + sum(S_i) + E <= bound`. No admission means today's behaviour.
  - **Expected:** the baseline becomes bounded by the daemon's requests, about 231 s -> 150-160 s on the whole DO. About 0 on
    small scopes, where the samples already fit.
  - **Memory:** one more one-shot, about 2.1 GiB resident after compile on the whole DO.
- **Which samples run does not change,** but R1107's invariants are reopened:
  - the settle order and "the first drop wins";
  - a backward pick never runs beside other one-shots;
  - no new one-shot after a stop (R955);
  - the `send()` pre-check counting every in-flight reservation;
  - the stats.
- **Before building:** a spec, a spec-adversary, red checks both ways, then the v3 gate (DBM) and the DO pair (sequential
  against k = 2, 0 per-mutant moves) as a pre-commitment.
- **Related:**
  - upstream #8143 (keep the compiled workspace in the disk cache). It would cut about 55% of each one-shot, and so shrink
    this lever.
  - [[R1092]].
