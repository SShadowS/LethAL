Found by the orchestrator, 2026-11-14, on coord task R-974 (bugs). Related: [[R953]] / [[R972]] (admission by memory), the R934 queue.

**What happened.** R-974's DO measurement (`heavy-slot.sh --wait --peak 20`) queued at 04:10Z as ticket 211. The memory check refused it on
every poll (held peaks 24-28 GiB + 20 + 4 GiB reserve over 32 GiB), while the cgroup used about 4 GiB. Meanwhile two later `--peak serve`
(8 GiB) jobs started (25 s and 5 min before the orchestrator looked): the held peaks never fell to the 8 GiB ticket 211 needed.

**Why: it is the queue's rule.** `scripts/heavy-slot.sh` (R934): only the oldest live ticket tries for a slot, but a waiter the memory
check refused marks its ticket `.refused` and from then on retries out of turn, and later tickets may overtake it while it stays refused
(chosen so one large job cannot block every small one). With a steady stream of smaller jobs, a large one can be overtaken without end.

**Wanted.** A refused head ticket must eventually stop later admissions: either at once (strict head of line), or after an age limit
(for example 15 min refused), after which no later ticket is admitted until it is. Test with injected readings and a stream of smaller
waiters; red both ways (the large job is admitted once the slots drain; a small job still passes a young refused ticket, if the age
rule is chosen).

**Meanwhile.** The orchestrator asked preproc to start no new project until ticket 211 holds its slot.
