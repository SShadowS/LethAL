also opened and closed), each in its own test codeunit, on `current` edge-a184cfc79. Results: 59 cells, 3 pass, 29 fail, 27 refused
(`/coord/handoff/PR-01/untouched-current.json`). Related: [[R995]], upstream #7760.

- `CopyStream between two streams that were never created` (1 cell).

**Known classes, more methods:** `RecordRef.* on a RecordRef that is not open` (11 cells, untouched and closed), `FieldRef.* on a
FieldRef that was never assigned` (4), `KeyRef.* on a KeyRef that was never assigned` (4), and the uncreated streams (2).

refused (11 cells each way, untouched and closed); on an untouched FieldRef 8 fail with `NavFieldRef variable not initialized.` while

while 5 are refused; on untouched streams ResetPosition and Write are refused while EOS, ReadText, Read, Length, Position and
WriteText fail with `NavInStream`/`NavOutStream variable not initialized.` (EOS, which #7760's body lists as refused, now fails). A
refused call costs a mutant its verdict; if the failing siblings give BC's error, the refused ones could too.
