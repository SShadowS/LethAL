Found by coord task R-953 (2026-11-13), deciding R-950's eight Cryptography Management survivors (`/coord/handoff/R-953/survivors.md`).
Related: [[R175]] (attribution lost), [[R964]], [[R963]].

**What happened.** Mutant M1200 (empty-block) sits in `SignedXml.SetSigningKey(SignatureKey: Codeunit "Signature Key")` (lines 80-83 of
`Xml/SignedXml.Codeunit.al`; the codeunit declares five `SetSigningKey` overloads). R-937's run on al-runner edge-42af5d384 recorded
`coverageAttribution: "exact"`, `reachGrain: statement`, covering tests `SignXmlDocumentWithSigningKeyAsSecretText` and
`...AndRsaAlgorithm`, and scored it SURVIVED. Those tests declare `SigningKey: SecretText` and call the SecretText overload (line 61),
never line 80. The two tests that do reach the `Signature Key` overload were refused at baseline on that build. So the mutant was
executed by no test, and "survived" is not a measured result; the honest verdict is no-coverage. Same shape for M1201, M1322, M1323
(`SignedXmlImpl.SetSigningKey`) and, by the survivors table, M1190, M1191, M1259, M1262 (an `InitializeSignedXml` overload).
On edge-c8fe56e05 these eight are refused instead (an error), so today's figure is not false, but the attribution is.

**Not known yet.** Whether al-runner's per-test coverage marks the lines of every same-named overload (a coverage bug upstream),
or LethAL's mapping from coverage lines to statements resolves a procedure by name (a LethAL bug); and how many survivors in other
stores rest on such an attribution (any codeunit with overloads).

**To find out, then fix.** Read one covering test's raw coverage for M1200's lines on a current build (al-runner `--coverage-json`),
and LethAL's line-to-statement mapping for overloaded procedures. Then count, across the stores, survived mutants in an overloaded
procedure whose covering tests call only other overloads. A false survivor is a correctness risk: fix at the source and re-check
the counted mutants.
