**Found by R-504's plan review** (`/coord/handoff/R-504/review-r1-opus-adversary.md`, M5). Kept
as it was by [[R504]], which pins the refusal's text in its test L6 and deliberately not the label.

`postLeaseAction` (`lease.ts`) wraps every error its `fetch` throws into
`LeaseUnavailableError("LethALControl_<action> unreachable: <error>")`. That includes R-496's
`UnfilteredExtensionsQueryError`, which `refuseRedirects` (`bc-fetch.ts`) throws when BC answers
with a redirect to the unfiltered `extensions` list (the request that hung BC 28.4, R433), and its
sibling `BcRedirectRefusedError`.

Two problems:

- **The type is lost.** A caller that would treat R-496's refusal differently from an outage
  cannot tell them apart on the lease path; it sees only `LeaseUnavailableError`. The refusal's
  text survives inside the message.
- **"unreachable" is the wrong diagnosis.** The request was dispatched and BC answered; LethAL
  refused to follow the redirect. An operator reading "unreachable" looks for a network or
  container fault that is not there.

Nothing that propagates today stops propagating: every lease caller already handles a thrown
`LeaseUnavailableError`, fail-closed.

Proposed fix: rethrow `UnfilteredExtensionsQueryError` and `BcRedirectRefusedError` unchanged from
`postLeaseAction` (or wrap them with a label that says BC answered with a refused redirect), after
checking every lease caller's `catch` for what an unwrapped refusal would do there.
