**Upstream grammar note, not filed upstream.** tree-sitter-al 4.4.1: `preproc_not_expression` is `seq('not', _preproc_expression)` with no precedence, so `#if not A and B` parses as `not (A and B)` and `#if A and not B or C` as `A and not (B or C)`. alc 18.0.41 binds `not` to the next operand only. Suggested fix: `prec(3, seq('not', _preproc_expression))`, above `and` (2) and `or` (1). There are zero occurrences in the four corpora.

LethAL no longer depends on it: R214 reads condition text with its own parser. File it upstream when convenient; no LethAL work is open.
