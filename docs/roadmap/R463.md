**Filed 2026-10-05 at R254's build (plan r4, sections 3.4 and 4).** R254 made a `reportextension`
instrumentable and gave its members a variable scope (`ExtensionKind` and `parseExtensionHeader` in
`packages/engine/src/semantic/symbol-table.ts`), so Tier-1 and the typed operators now mutate inside
one. Tier 2 does not: `packages/engine/src/semantic/receiver.ts` resolves a record receiver through
its `OBJECT_KINDS` list, the scope-owner switch and the implicit-table branches, and all of them
name `tableextension` and `pageextension` only. So a `Rec.SetRange(...)`, `TestField`, `CalcFields`,
`Modify`/`Insert`/`Delete` flag or filter literal written inside a `reportextension` resolves to no
receiver, and every Tier-2 operator refuses the site.

**Why not built with R254.** Refusing is the safe direction: a missed claim loses mutants, a wrong
claim emits one that cannot compile or that mutates the wrong record. The tables fixture's
`DataBandExt` arm has no record call, so a change here would have no gate measuring it: the arm's
mutant list is identical with or without a `receiver.ts` change (measured in R254's scratch copy).

**To build it.** Add `reportextension` to the receiver's object kinds and scope-owner switch, decide
what the implicit record is inside a `modify(<dataitem>)` trigger (the dataitem's table, as R294's
`implicitRecordShadowsGlobals` already assumes), red-test each direction, and add a fixture arm with
a record call whose verdicts are pre-committed. Count the newly claimed sites on BaseApp first.
