**Found by** PR-435 (lethal-code, 2026-11-25), measured on c5's events. Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-435/findings.md`,
`split.py`. The figures are shares, because c5 ran under load.

- **What was measured:** over the mutants phase (5 lanes × 19,758.5 s = 98,792 lane-s):
  - **Request wall outside al-runner's own timings:** prefetch 1,900 s over 897 requests (about 2.1 s each, carrying
    126k entries) and live 755 s over 2,780 requests. Together 2,655 s (2.7%).
  - **Lane time in no request and not idle:** 2,059 s (2.1%). This is the lanes' capacity less 95,273 s of request
    wall, less 1,460 s of end and window idle.
- **What is not known:** how that splits between sending and encoding a request, reading and decoding its answer (large
  prefetch answers), store writes, and the scheduler. The first step is a profile of one DO window (PR-252's
  sampling), or per-request LethAL-side timings added to `serve-request-timed`.
- **Bound:** at most 4.8% of the mutants phase (about 4% of the run). Some of it is irreducible, for example a
  request's answer must be read.
- **Related:** R1216, R1217, PR-113 (the request timing events).
