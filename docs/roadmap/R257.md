**Found 2026-09-26, in GH-24b's first review round.** The demo runbook (`docs/directions-emea-2026-runbook.md`,
the beat-by-beat table) says the planted `GetBalance` survivor "ranks sixth of nine" under
`lethal explain --top 10`, and its narration told the presenter that `executionProven: true` means a
test ran the mutated line. Both came from the frozen `rehearsal.report.json` in
`docs/campaign/2026-08-16-gift-card/`, written before GH-24.

GH-24b (explain v5) ranks survivors by each mutant's own reach first (`survivorActionabilityRank`),
so on a report from a current build the planted survivor's rank can move, possibly to the top, which
would also change what the agent-loop prompt's "take the top survivor" picks. The `executionProven`
wording was simply wrong at any version: it is procedure-level, and was corrected in the runbook on
2026-09-26 with a dated note.

What is missing is the measurement: re-run the gift-card demo on a build with GH-24 (a live run,
owner-only), read the planted survivor's rank and its `reach`/`reachedBy` from the new explain output,
then update the rank, the `--top` advice and, if `reach` is `reached-unnoticed`, the 1:20 narration.
Until then the runbook's note says not to quote the rank.
