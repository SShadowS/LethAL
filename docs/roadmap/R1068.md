Coord PR-52 (2026-11-16). Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-pr52-format-evaluate-family.md` (addendum 1). Evidence:
`/coord/handoff/PR-52/` (`joined-prelim.tsv`, `joined2.tsv`, `v3.json`; BC side lethal-bugs on bc2, `bc/`).

**The method.** `scripts/refusal-family.ts` family `format-evaluate`: one call per cell, its answer carried as the test's failure
message (`Error('V:[%1]', ...)`), and `--join` compares v3 and BC per cell after normalizing both sides. The family covers:
- `Format` with length, number and picture over 12 value types;
- `Evaluate` into 10 types, starting from a non-default sentinel;
- text-function edges;
- an `Error` control cell and a time-zone probe;
- 29 cells under `GlobalLanguage(1030)`.

**Measured** (BC 28.4 W1, en-US region, UTC):
- First run, 295 cells: **295 equal**.
- Second run, 326 cells: **325 equal**.
- Equal includes the 15 cells where the call raises (same error text), Boolean words and Evaluate under Danish, the Evaluate
  sentinel kept on failure, and `Error`'s own `%` handling.
- **The one difference:** the closing-date letter under Danish (v3 `C`, BC `U`): #8178, R586 row 21, regression entry
  `u8178-closing-date`.
- Region-dependent formats (decimal, date, time) follow the region, which is en-US on both sides. A non-en-US region cannot be set
  from a test without a client.

**What it suggests.** al-runner's text conversions are close to BC's in en-US. Language-dependent words are where differences sit,
as the SetFilter refusal under 1030 (PR-53) also shows.
