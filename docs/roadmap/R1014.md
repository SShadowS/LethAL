Found by R-998's DO window (lethal-bugs, 2026-11-15; evidence `/coord/handoff/R-998/do/result.md`, events and process samples
beside it). Head 30150ce3, al-runner edge-847b1fa5a serve, `--time-budget 14400` under `timeout 15300`. Related: [[R955]]
(budget and baseline; SIGTERM spends the budget), [[R897]] (one hanging mutant's cost on v3), [[R958]].

**What happened.** The budget ran out at 01:47:36 and `timeout` sent SIGTERM at about 02:02:36. LethAL went on scoring until
02:41, when a second SIGTERM ended it by the default action: no report, so the window's serve summary (warm-up time) was lost.
From 01:27 on, mutants of one codeunit hung: each hang cost the whole `--test-timeout` (1,762 s here), the daemon closed
(`serve-daemon-closed` reason `timeout`), and the rest of the request was re-sent to a new daemon. About 32 mutants were scored
in those 70 minutes.

**Why the budget did not stop it.** R955's stop only spends the budget; the run stops "at its next check". On serve that check is
not reached between the re-sends of a request after a timeout, so a run of hanging mutants overruns the budget without bound.

**A second effect, in the drivers, not LethAL.** GNU `timeout` signals its whole process group, so the al-runner daemon got the
SIGTERM too, died, and LethAL started a new daemon, as for any daemon loss. Corpus drivers should signal LethAL's pid only, and use
`timeout -k <grace>` so the run still ends.

**To close.** The spent budget (clock or signal) is checked before each re-send of a request after a daemon close; the rest of
the request's mutants are then left unscored (resumable), and the run writes its report. A unit test with a fake daemon that times
out on every entry shows the run stopping at the first check after the budget is spent.

**Done (coord PR-07, 2026-11-15).**
- A parallel lane now checks the spent budget (clock or signal) before each mutant, not only between windows. It stops
  the commit the way the memory guard does: what finished is recorded, and what never started is left out.
- A mutant whose calls ended while a stop signal arrived is not recorded. This is R955's baseline rule; the signal can
  have killed al-runner. The memory guard's stop is excluded, because it kills nothing.
- No prefetch chunk, re-sent segment or kill-pair batch starts once the budget is spent.
- A request still running when a signal arrives ends at once: its daemon closes, with the new routine reason
  `stop-signal`.
- The "run as live requests" and "pair-batch-skipped" warnings are no longer printed after a stop.

Tests (each red with its fix reverted):
- `r766-parallel-v3.test.ts`, "R1014": a signal mid-window with 1 and 2 workers; a clock budget mid-window; the other
  direction.
- `al-runner-v3-entries.test.ts`, "R1014": no chunk or pair batch once spent; a hang ended by a signal, and run to its
  answer without one.

**Limits, kept.**
- With a CLOCK-only budget, the mutant already in flight finishes its own covering runs. That includes R897's
  alone-calls after a cut, each of which can wait out the test stop. It is bounded by its covering tests times the stop,
  and ending it would leave a partial verdict.
- Daemon spawns are not gated by a signal. The watcher ends one within 250 ms, and the transport error ends the mutant's
  loop.
- When a memory-guard stop and a real signal both happen, the memory exception applies, as before.

Why one hang costs 1,762 s, and whether a per-test stop should replace R954's single stop, is filed as [[R1015]].

**The clock-budget limit closed (coord PR-108, lethal-code, 2026-11-17).**
- **What it was.** The limit kept above, that "with a CLOCK-only budget, the mutant already in flight finishes its own covering
  runs", cost DO t13 797 s past `--time-budget 12600`. One hanging mutant's walk ran 785 s after the budget was spent.
- **The check.** The clock budget (and a signal) is now also checked between the steps of a mutant's walk (`feed`), but not
  during the held replay, and never on a memory-guard stop.
- **On a spent budget:**
  - the walk ends before the after-walk code;
  - the mutant is left UNSCORED and unrecorded, counted in `time-budget-reached`'s `unscheduledCount`, so `--resume` scores
    it;
  - a lane exits through the sink's stop.
- **Tests.** `pr108-walk-cap.test.ts` (B1-B4) and `r766-parallel-v3.test.ts`, including the last mutant on a lane, are each
  red with their rule reverted.
