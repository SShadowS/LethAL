**Filed 2026-10-22 by lethal-orchestrator**, from the owner's handoff for the Directions EMEA 2026 talk (Tue 27 Oct
15:00). Due: usable by 23 Oct midday. The owner picks the talk commit on 23 Oct, after [[R654]] lands.

**What.** One command, `scripts/talk-cut.ts <sha> [--version 0.1.0-alpha.4] [--dry-run]`, that:
1. refuses unless CI is green on that sha;
2. bumps `package.json` to the version and adds its CHANGELOG section;
3. pushes the tag `v<version>`;
4. waits for `release.yml` to create the DRAFT release;
5. builds `lethal-control.app` with alc in the container and attaches it to the draft, stating its version
   (at least 1.0.0.23);
6. prints the draft URL.

It never publishes the release; publishing is the owner's click. It follows `docs/releasing.md` and leaves the
existing v0.1.0-alpha.3 draft alone. `--dry-run` does every check and prints every step without pushing
anything. The owner tried release.yml by workflow_dispatch on master c67a89fc on 22 Oct (run 39837034032).
