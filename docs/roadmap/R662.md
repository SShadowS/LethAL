**Done 2026-10-22 (R-662, lethal-bugs), code head 03448305.**
- **`scripts/talk-cut.ts <sha> [--version] [--notes <file>] [--package-cache <dir>] [--dry-run]`.** Every check runs
  before any push:
  - CI green on `<sha>`, both jobs;
  - the tag and its release are free;
  - the control app is built with alc and is at least 1.0.0.23;
  - the bump is prepared, and the release body is checked (more than 80 characters, under GitHub's 125,000 limit
    with margin).

  Then it:
  - runs a `release.yml` trial on `<sha>` through a throwaway `release-trial/<sha12>` branch, and refuses unless it
    is green;
  - pushes the bump to `release/v<version>` (never master; the owner merges it);
  - pushes an annotated tag;
  - requires a DRAFT for that tag;
  - attaches `lethal-control.app` and appends its version and sha256 to the body;
  - prints the URL.

  It never publishes. The v0.1.0-alpha.3 draft is untouched. Every push and dispatch target is guarded and
  red-checked.
- **The release body.** `scripts/release-notes.ts` (called by release.yml) uses `release-notes/<tag>.md` when the
  tagged commit has one, and otherwise the CHANGELOG section. `--notes` commits that file, and CHANGELOG.md stays
  whole. A dry-run on master's `[Unreleased]` (156,625 characters) refuses, so the owner either passes `--notes` or
  shortens `[Unreleased]` on master (the orchestrator's ruling: option (c), shaped to serve (a)).
- **The release.yml trial fixes:**
  - The parse and dry-run expectations moved to `scripts/native-parse-expect.txt` and
    `scripts/release-dry-run-expect.txt` (`files 34 nodes 7707 errors 0`, and 30 / 428 / 406). A unit test runs both
    on every CI push, so a fixture change now fails ordinary CI.
  - Two release-only bugs that the failed parse step had hidden: talk-cut's paths on Windows, and the unit-test step
    missing ci.yml's R449 `TEMP`/`TMP` (the R631 walker test failed there).
