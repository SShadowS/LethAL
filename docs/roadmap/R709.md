**Filed 2026-10-24 by lethal-code during R-708.** Evidence: `/coord/handoff/R-708/duration-trace.md`.

**What happened.** The hang gate on bc3 at 845e6886 failed with `[SINGLE] M0035: duration 18520ms is outside
[20000, 50000)`, every verdict matching (log: `/coord/handoff/R-271/gates/hang-bc3.run1-duration-fail.log`).

**Root cause (inferred from the code, the step itself not observed).** `test_results.duration_ms` is the transport's
`durationMs`, a difference of two `Date.now()` readings in packages/runner/src/run-mutant-transport.ts. The budget
timer is a `setTimeout` (monotonic), armed after `started`, so a stop-confirmed row is at least `timeoutMs` plus the
stop round trip (about 150 ms in the baseline leg's rows) on a monotonic clock. 18520 ms is only reachable if the wall
clock stepped back by about 1.6 s during the call (this container is WSL2, whose clock the host corrects). R272
already guarded a negative duration for the same reason, but not the measure.

**Fix.** `monoNow = () => Math.floor(performance.now())` replaces `Date.now()` for every elapsed reading in the
file: `dispatch`, `runMany`, `execute`'s `stopDeadline` and `settleStop`'s `left`. `Date.now()` stays for the trace
`at:` field and the poll `sentAt` (wall timestamps). The hang gate's bound `[BUDGET_MS, BUDGET_MS + STOP_GRACE_MS)` is
unchanged: it holds on a monotonic measure and a tolerance would weaken it.

**Tests.** `R709: a wall-clock step back ...` in packages/runner/tests/run-mutant-transport.test.ts; a 5 s
too-early row in packages/runner/tests/hang-stop-rows.test.ts. Not covered: a `runMany` stopped row under a clock
step (same helper, same shape).
