**Found by the build review of coord task R-736 (2026-10-24).** [[R736]] made every gate pin the `warning` EVENTS
that `runSession` emits. Two kinds of warning are still outside that pin:

1. **`console.warn` lines that never become events** (file-level names; see the review notes in the R-736 submit):
   - orchestrator.ts: the lease path (`noteRefusal`, the failed store correction in `onLost`, `closeLeaseScope`'s
     extra refusals, `surfaceLateRefusal`, `invalidateLostBatch`) and the resource-rebase collision;
   - al-runner-v3-backend.ts: the pinned-defect and `v3DefectWarnings` lines, `al-runner-v3-dotnet-helper-missing`,
     `entries-prefetch-dropped`, `pair-skipped`;
   - bcdev-backend.ts: the leftover-copy and dangling-package warnings, the fenced-coverage "no rows for this
     artifact" and "no line in a known procedure" warnings;
   - harness.ts's tenant line and app-package.ts's unmapped object type.
2. **Warning events outside `runSession`:** `runVerify` (src/verify.ts) and `runNamedMutants` emit warning events,
   but the verify, testapp and agreement gates call them (`verifyFromCli`, `runVerify`, `runNamedMutants`) without
   collecting them.

**To close.** Route the user-facing `console.warn` lines through the emitter where a session has one (a warning
event with a code), and collect `runVerify` and `runNamedMutants` events in their gates under R736's keys and rules:
observe, explain each code, pre-commit, pin, prove.
