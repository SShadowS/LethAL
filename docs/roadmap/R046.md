**A survivor no instrumented guard fired for is not a finding, and until now nothing distinguished one.** `LC Control State.IsActive` is a bare string compare, so an unactivated mutant behaves byte-identically to baseline — "the test ran and passed" proves nothing about whether the mutation was ever in play. R32 had to establish that by hand, one mutant at a time, after R29 had already produced 10 false survivors out of 20. `RunMutant` already attested `observedAny` per run, but `runSession` folded it into a session-wide artifact ledger and discarded it per mutant. Now surfaced as `MutantOutcome.guardObserved`, OR-ed across a mutant's covering runs, with the asymmetry documented: **`false` is decisive** (no guarded site executed, so the mutant was never given a chance to fail — it belongs with `no-coverage`, not with findings), **`true` is weak** (some selector fired somewhere in the artifact, not necessarily THIS mutant's guard), and **absent means not measured** (al-runner cannot attest; collapsing absent to false would accuse every al-runner survivor). The console names unexercised survivors so they do not read as suite gaps. First measurement on Document Output: **86 of 86 survivors `true`, 0 `false`**, with `no-coverage`/`error` correctly absent — so the artifact was live throughout and the "10 of 12 procedures have zero kills because activation is broken there" hypothesis is ruled out at artifact level. It does NOT individually verify the 86; that still wants driving each against its intended killer.
