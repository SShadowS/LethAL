**`SessionConfig.emit` accepting a single subscriber, plus a separate `fanOutEmit` helper to combine several, is one layer of indirection more than the design needs.** `runSession` (`orchestrator.ts`) already fans events out to multiple listeners via `createEmitter([collector, ...])` internally; `cli.ts`'s `fanOutEmit` (`packages/runner/src/cli.ts:1714`) exists ONLY to pre-combine the stderr progress renderer (Task 5) and the `--progress-out` NDJSON sink (Task 6) into the ONE subscriber slot `SessionConfig.emit` currently allows, then unwraps that combination again inside `runSession`. Widening `SessionConfig.emit` to `readonly EventSubscriber[]` would let `runSession` fan out to all of them directly and delete `fanOutEmit` (and its dedicated test suite, `cli.test.ts`'s `describe("fanOutEmit (Task 6 review round 1)")`) entirely — one fan-out implementation instead of two. Found during the whole-branch review of the event-stream branch (2026-08-05); not fixed there since it touches Task 5/6's already-landed, already-tested surface rather than Task 4's. Evidence: `packages/runner/src/cli.ts:1699-1916`, `packages/runner/tests/cli.test.ts:987-1030`.
