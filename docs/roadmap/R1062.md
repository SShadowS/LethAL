Filed and closed by coord PR-44 (2026-11-16), PR-41's step 2. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-pr44-t5-hang-shapes.md`
(addenda 1-2). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/R-984/t5-timeouts.md`, `/coord/handoff/PR-44/measure.md`. Related: [[R196]], [[R1050]],
[[R1061]].

**What.** `until X = ''` with X refilled in the body, and `while X <> ''` with X cut down in it. Toggling the `''` makes the exit
unreachable once X has emptied: the toggled `=` is never true, and the toggled `<>` is always true. In DO's t5 and t6 these
mutants timed out (60 s floor, 2-3 daemon closes and 2 single re-runs each).

**Rule** (`loop-hazard.ts`, `lethal.toggle-blank-string` only). The literal is a direct operand of `=` or `<>`, the other operand
is a name the loop body writes, and the comparison's value after the toggle reaches the loop's exit unchanged (R572's polarity
walk):
- `not` flips the value;
- the walk continues through `and` while the value is false, and through `or` while it is true;
- `repeat ... until` is refused when the exit stays false, and `while` when it stays true.

**Measured.** On DO it removes 5 sites, of which 3 were timeout-killed, 1 killed and 1 no-coverage in t6: 3 hangs per 4 scored
sites lost (PR-37's accepted rate: 1 per 8). No fixture site moves and no golden moves. One DO site with the opposite polarity
(`... <> '') or (... Next() = 0)`) stays mutated. Out of scope: an operand wrapped in a call (`DelChr(X) = ''`). Rule B
(a loop bound set before the loop) was dropped by ruling: R1061.

**To confirm.** From DO window t8 on, no timeout close from this shape.
