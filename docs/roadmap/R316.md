**Found by R-309's plan (2026-09-28)**, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-28-R-309-split-preamble.md`
on branch `lethal/r309`, section "What else touches this node". Filed by the orchestrator ahead of
R-309 so no other session re-finds it. Sibling of [[R309]] (the whole-run throw for this shape),
[[R301]] (the shared-`var` split procedure) and [[R302]] (the same site blindness for
`preproc_split_procedure`).

**What breaks.** A `preproc_split_procedure_preamble` member (each `#if` arm holds a header and its
own `var` section, before one shared body) is not a member anywhere in the pipeline: its
`procedureName` is `""`, it has no scope, and the line map finds no member span for it (the gap
block climbs to the file root). Two measured consequences, on hand-written repros under al-runner
v2.11.0:

- **False `no-coverage`.** With al-runner coverage ON, every mutant in such a member reads
  `no-coverage`, although its tests execute it: with coverage OFF the same mutants are scored
  (3 of 4 killed on the plan's repro). This drops live sites from the score. It never makes a kill
  or a survivor.
- **Lost sites.** The same body gets 7 mutants in a plain procedure and 4 in a preamble
  (`empty-block`, `return-value` and one `swap-additive` are missing), measured on the twin-body
  repro `q1-twin-body`.

**Size.** 0 corpus files hold this shape (R-297 Task 0's census and R-309's re-check). The trigger
form does not parse as a preamble at all (tree-sitter-al issue #32) and is refused as [[R313]].

**What would close it.** Make the preamble a member in the walks [[R301]] extended
(`isProcedureLike` or a sibling predicate for the manifest, scope, line map and gap block), so its
mutants get a name, a span and coverage attribution, and the missing operators see its body. Then
consider a per-arm reach latch (the latch declared after each arm's header), whose `alc` evidence
(`pa-p1`, `pa-p2`, `pa-p4`, `pa-p8`, every subset PASS; latch in the first arm only fails with
AL0118) is in the R-309 plan. Until then R309's named refusal keeps such a member's mutants at
`reachGrain: "unplaced"`.
