**What was measured.**
- **Targets:** the 30 distinct texts DO windows t10 (edge-847b1fa5a) and t11 (edge-4ebfe7913) closed a serve daemon on in
  the mutant phase, none on master's keep-list.
- **The family:** `t10-refusals` in `scripts/refusal-family.ts`, 27 invented refusers plus one non-refuser cell. 27 of the
  30 texts came out exactly.
- **The census:** PR-54's strict `scripts/keep-census.ts`, with a new opt-in `--refusers <n>` (default 3, as before). With
  `--refusers 7` every distinct text of a class was a measured refuser; a first run at 3 left four SetFilter texts unmeasured.
- **Result:** 15 classes, all CLEAN:
  - every reader equal to the control;
  - `engineKept` on every kept reader;
  - every refuser refused under serve;
  - no panic text.

**Predictions:**
- P1 held: every measured class was CLEAN.
- P2 held: 14 of the 16 families were driven, against a floor of 12.
