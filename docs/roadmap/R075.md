**BLOCKS WIRING `routedTransport`: routed verdicts discard `coverageAttribution`.** `orchestrator.ts` calls `coverageFilter(...)` and keeps only `.covered`, dropping `.attribution`; the routed `record(...)` passes `undefined`. `report.ts`'s `coverageAttribution` field documents absent as "nothing ran (`no-coverage`)". An `object`-attributed routed survivor — the exact R29 shape that produced 10 false survivors out of 20 — becomes indistinguishable from an `exact` one. Bounded to `exact` vs `object` (FALLBACK 2 / `all-green` cannot reach this path; the table-trigger gate excludes it). Filed, not fixed — do not implement without a design pass; blocks any production code constructing `routedTransport`.
