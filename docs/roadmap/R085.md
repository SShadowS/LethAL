**The RATE at which a real suite notices a real argument swap — unmeasured, and it is the only R82 question left.** R82 proved the MECHANISM live (30/30 predicted verdicts, `f9e055c`), on a fixture written for it. A fixture cannot produce a rate: six arms chosen to be readable say nothing about how often Continia Document Output's 1,290 tests would catch a swap at one of its real sites. **The population is 390, not 340, and sampling the wrong one would have described something LethAL does not emit.** 340 is the CENSUS's number, and the census pairs over all arguments and only then asks whether the chosen pair happened to be two identifiers; the shipped operator filters to identifiers FIRST and then pairs, so it finds pairs the census skipped. Running the actual `swapCallArguments.targets` over `do-rel2/Cloud` claims **390** sites. Sample from 390. **Two instruments, and the cheap one does not need the operator:** (a) hand-swap a SEEDED random sample of the 390, run DO's own suite per swap, count what it notices — zero self-construction and it exercises none of LethAL, which is the point; (b) a post-ship LethAL-on-DO run, which also measures the pipeline at scale but costs a real run. **Pre-commit the bar BEFORE any rate is seen** (R13's discipline at `349901a`) and pre-commit the sampling rule with its seed, or the sample becomes the argument. **Both instruments are exposed to arm-E-shaped platform kills, and an earlier version of this row wrongly granted (a) immunity.** Of the 390 claimed sites, 233 have a project-local callee that resolves; **12 of those (5.2%) declare DIFFERENT parameter types at the two swapped positions, 6 of them same-base-different-length** — the arm E shape exactly. A real one: `Al\Codeunit\Codeunit 6175293 CDO Payment Link Handler.al:187` swaps a 17-character label into a `Text[50]` and a 72-character label into it. At 5.2%, a 30-swap sample should expect roughly one, so **the sampling rule must require recording the FAILURE TEXT of every noticed swap, not just the count** — that is what makes (a)'s immunity a discipline someone actually performs rather than a property of the instrument. (b) additionally needs **R121**, not R86: R86 is closed and a LethAL run now RECORDS every kill's failure text (`MutantOutcome.killingTestFailure`), which is what makes instrument (a)'s "record the failure text of every noticed swap" performable on a LethAL run at all — but nothing classifies that text, so the kill-rate split still has to be done by a reader.

**Instrument (b) was run 2026-08-07. Read `docs/campaign/2026-08-07-r85-swap-rate/rung1.result.md`
before planning another.** Four things it settled, none of them the rate:

- **The population is 437, not 390.** R87 (`ac9ce94`) fixed `resolveIdentifierType`; re-measured with
  the same instrument and set-diffed, 0 lost / 47 gained. Any future sampling frame is the 437.
- **All three scored swaps were FALSE KILLS**, and identically so: `AddFeature(..., NameLbl,
  DescriptionLbl, ...)` swapped, the description overflowing the 80-character field the name is
  assigned to, BC rejecting the data before any assertion ran. Raw rate 3/3 = 100%;
  false-kill-adjusted 0/3 = 0%. This row predicted the shape at 5.2% of resolvable sites; in this
  sample it was 100% of what scored.
- **R86 is what made that visible**, in its first use on a real project. Without the recorded failure
  text the run reports 100% and the reader concludes DO's suite catches every argument swap.
- **A coverage-blind scope rule does not work for this question.** 80% of the chosen scope had no
  covering test (181 covered / 713 no-coverage), so 894 deployed mutants scored 3 swaps. That is a
  finding about instrument (b), not bad luck.

**What a next attempt needs**, and it must be a NEW pre-commitment rather than a retune of that one —
re-scoping after seeing coverage is selecting on a variable correlated with the outcome:

1. A **coverage-aware** selection rule. Files with swap sites AND covering tests, decided from a
   prior run's coverage split rather than from swap density.
2. A larger scored n. 3 is not a sample; even 30 gives ±18 points.
3. A plan for the environment failing mid-run — this one 502'd and quarantined the tier at M0487.

Instrument (a), the hand-swap one, is looking better than when this row was written: it needs no
environment, cannot be truncated by a 502, and its exposure to false kills is now something R86 makes
checkable rather than something the instrument had to be trusted about.
