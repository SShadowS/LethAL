**Found by the R229 audit (2026-09-29).** The script's "procedure" grain is
`` `${m.file}:${m.procedureName ?? ""}` `` (`scripts/r181-discrimination-census.ts`). A trigger
row's `procedureName` is `""`, not absent, the same trap as R229, so all trigger mutants in one
file land in one group and are compared with each other as if they were one member.

**Size, measured on its own corpus:** `2026-08-07-r85-swap-rate/rung1` has 21 trigger rows over 7
distinct triggers in 2 files, and `2026-08-16-gift-card/rehearsal` 7 rows over 2 triggers in 1
file; the other three reports have at most 6 rows and at most one trigger per file. So the grain
experiment's procedure row merged 9 triggers into 3 groups.

**Fix shape:** `m.procedureName || m.triggerName || ""` (and `triggerName` in the row type), then
re-run the script and restate R181's procedure-grain figures if they move. Not done in R229
because it changes a committed measurement's output, which needs its own re-read.

A cosmetic sibling in `scripts/r175-rung1-rerun-compare.ts` (lines 146 and 171) prints a trigger
mutant's member as blank in its diff lines; no count depends on it.

**Done (R-359b).** Both scripts now name a row's member through `memberOf`
(`scripts/lib/member-key.ts`: `procedureName || triggerName || ""`). Re-run on the same six
reports: the procedure grain goes from 492 to 498 groups (the measured 9 triggers in 3 groups are
now 9 groups); the solo-mutant count (510) and the marginal share (25.5%) do not move, so R181's
conclusion stands. [[R181]]'s table is restated with a dated note. Pinned by
`scripts/lib/member-key.test.ts` (two triggers in one file are two members). Red-checked: the old
`procedureName ?? ""` turns it red.
