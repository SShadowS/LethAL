**Filed 2026-11-06 by lethal-bugs, from the orchestrator's reading of the process list.** Related: [[R814]], [[R633]].

**What.** `heavyJobs()` in `scripts/al-runner-v3-update.ts` counted every process whose command line matched `HEAVY_JOB`. One job is a wrapper
shell, `flock`, `bun run`, a bun child and workers, so one corpus job could read as many. The update skips for the day at 3 or more. On
2026-11-06 at 10:47 the retry logged 6 and skipped `v3.0.0-alpha.13`, while `heavy.1` was the only lock held. A process list taken a few minutes later
showed that sampling work in two process groups, so the true figure was about 2 at most; there is no list from the minute itself.

**Fix.** `countHeavyJobs`: distinct process groups, `--worker` left out, or the number of held `heavy.<n>` locks, whichever is higher. A job under a
lock is in both counts, so they are not added. Real jobs still stop the update: three groups count as three, and so do three held locks.
