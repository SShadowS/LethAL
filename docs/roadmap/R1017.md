Found in PR-11's review of [[R1016]] (2026-11-15). al-runner finds a `dotnet-sidecar/` folder next to its own binary by itself.
Since [[R886]], a build that passed keeps its sidecar in its folder, so `--regate` (which gates `current/al-runner`) and a refresh
(which gates a row's folder) ran WITH the sidecar, while a candidate's gate ran without one. The frozen figures were measured with
no sidecar (al-runner-v3-gate's `dotnetSidecar: false`: "could turn a refusal into a run. Off, explicitly"). Regates passed, so
the readings were probably insensitive, but the two paths did not measure the same thing.

Orchestrator ruling (2026-11-15): consistency over a re-measure. The gate stays sidecar-less on every path.

**To close.** A binary with a sidecar beside it is gated as a plain copy that cannot see it, with its `.gated` (a non-candidate
gate reads it beside the binary); a test shows a regate of a `current` with a sidecar sees none, and the sidecar stays in
`current`.
