**Found by R-214's pre-commitment (2026-09-30, ruling T1-a).** When an `if/else` is split by
`#if` so that its head and the `end else begin` sit in an arm (node `preproc_split_if_statement`
or `preproc_split_if_else_statement`), master emits neither the `empty-block` of the then-block
nor the `negate-guard` of the condition. The compiled build has both when that arm is active. The
else-tail itself is R287-C7 and the `if C then begin` split is R304; this is the remaining part.

**Rule:** smallest directive node `preproc_split_if_statement` or `preproc_split_if_else_statement`,
and `lethal.empty-block` or `lethal.negate-guard`.

**Lost.** Repro `packages/runner/tests/fixtures/r214/p13-exclusions`, `ElseTail.Codeunit.al` L6
`empty-block` and `negate-guard`, under `[]`. Corpora: baseapp.0 2; all others 0.

**Out of R-214's scope.** A split construct is not a statement-level `#if`, which is the only
container R-214 lifts.

**Closed 2026-10-05 as a ruling: negligible.** The R-364 census (`/coord/handoff/R-364/measurement.md`,
per `app.json` project: BaseApp's 203, CDO at 5f2a71d, fixtures and examples, under `[]` and under
each project's own symbols) found 2 lost sites in 2 BaseApp files under `[]` (`ProdOrderComponent`
`empty-block`, `XMLBufferWriter` `negate-guard`), 0 under the project symbols, 0 in CDO and 0 in the
fixtures. Nothing wrong is emitted; sites are missing. Repro `p13` keeps the shape for a reopen.
