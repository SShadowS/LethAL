**Filed 2026-10-24 by R-522 (lethal-bugs)**, evidence `/coord/handoff/R-522/` (measure.md). A plausible hang that is
NOT refused.

**What.** A report data item's own triggers rename the record it walks (`Rec.Rename`), or write a sort-key or filter
field of it and then call `Modify`. A mutant can move the record ahead of the cursor, and the walk revisits it.

**Why nothing covers it.** [[R531]] refuses rename and modify-filtered consumers only inside `while`/`repeat` loops,
and a data item is not one. [[R523]] measured no Rename in fired triggers. [[R529]] and [[R530]] are the Insert family.
[[R484]] makes Integer items loops, by their Break.

**To close.** Count the shape by parse on every corpus, read a sample, then refuse or close it with a ruling.
