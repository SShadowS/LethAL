**Filed 2026-10-24 by R-522 (lethal-bugs)**, evidence `/coord/handoff/R-522/` (measure.md). A plausible hang that is
NOT refused.

**What.** A report data item's own triggers rename the record it walks (`Rec.Rename`), or write a sort-key or filter
field of it and then call `Modify`. A mutant can move the record ahead of the cursor, and the walk revisits it.

**Why nothing covers it.** [[R531]] refuses rename and modify-filtered consumers only inside `while`/`repeat` loops,
and a data item is not one. [[R523]] measured no Rename in fired triggers. [[R529]] and [[R530]] are the Insert family.
[[R484]] makes Integer items loops, by their Break.

**To close.** Count the shape by parse on every corpus, read a sample, then refuse or close it with a ruling.

## Done

R-696 measured it and landed a refusal (`inMovedWalk` in `loop-hazard.ts`, tests in `r696-moved-walks.test.ts`).
Evidence: `/coord/handoff/R-696/numbers.md` (the census), `probe/results.md` (the bc2 probe, pre-commitment a67f292f).

**The hazard.** `Next` sees a row that a SECOND variable of the walked table moved ahead of the cursor, or that an
insert put ahead of it (under `FindSet`, `FindSet(true)` and `Find('-')`, and in a report data item). A move by the
walked variable's own Modify never revisits a row. So such a walk ends only because an exit works, and a mutant that
removes the exit never ends. 5 forever stops are refused; at 3 of them no current operator deletes the exit (no
operator removes a bare `exit;`). The live ones are Resend Attachments' `CurrReport.Skip` and Update Contract Prices'
OnPreDataItem `SetFilter`, both through `void-method-call`.

**The rule, for every operator.**
- Shape B: a `repeat .. until R.Next()` or `while R.Next() ..` loop over a non-temporary R, whose body renames, inserts
  into, `ModifyAll`s a key field of, or (after a key or whole-record write) `Modify`s another non-temporary variable of
  R's table. R's own Rename, ModifyAll and Insert count too (no Rename leg was probed); R's own Modify does not.
  Refused: the exits and their guards, the mover and its writes, the pre-loop filter calls on R and their guards, and
  the assignments feeding them.
- Shape A: a report or XMLport item over an ordinary table that [[R530]] does not take, with the same movers through
  another variable, or a one-hop callee (or `V.P()`) that inserts into its table. Refused: its whole scope, its filter
  calls anywhere in the object, and their feeds. Resend Attachments is covered by the mover clause (see [[R529]]).
- Sort key: the primary key plus the union of every pre-loop `SetCurrentKey`; every field after `SetView`/`Copy`, for
  a table the project lacks, and when R is a parameter, a global or the implicit Rec with no `SetCurrentKey` (the
  caller set it, so it fails closed; the census puts this at about 35 more BaseApp rows).

**Cost** (measured on the final code: `generateMutationSet` with the rule off and on, specs removed over specs with the
rule off): BaseApp 6,495 of 743,985 (0.87%), DC 0.63%, DO 0.29%, CDO 0.26%. The prototype figures (0.68, 0.46, 0.21,
0.19%) rose because the production rule is wider. Details: `/coord/handoff/R-696/cost-final.md`.

**Accepted limits** (filed as [[R702]] where measurable): a guard in a helper; a bound set by a caller; temporary
walks, including R's own insert into a walked temporary table (not probed); XMLport self-insert items (outside
[[R530]] and this rule, since an import element does not walk its table); shape B follows no callee.

Fixtures: the production predicate refuses 0 nodes over the 21 `fixtures/*` projects with an `app.json`.
