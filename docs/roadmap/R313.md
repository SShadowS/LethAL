**Found by the R-303 Task 2 review (2026-09-28)**, outside [[R303]]'s diff. Two ways of splitting a
member's `var` section by `#if` are valid AL (alc compiles both, un-instrumented, under every
symbol subset) but tree-sitter-al does not parse them as a `preproc_conditional_var_block`:

- two `#if` var blocks in a row (`#if A` / `var K` / `#endif` / `#if not A` / `var N` / `#endif`);
- a nested `#if` wrapping the whole section (`#if A` / `#if B` / `var K` / `#endif` / `#endif`).

The grammar leaves ERROR nodes as direct children of the member, between the header and `begin`,
so R303's hoist never sees a block. Before this fix LethAL then emitted invalid AL without any
warning: for two blocks the latch was appended to the second block's section (alc `AL0118`, the
latch does not exist, under `[A]`), and for the nested wrap a new `var` was written before `begin`
(alc `AL0104` under `[A,B]`).

**Now (R-303 Task 2 fix round 1):** `varSectionUnparsed` (`packages/schemata/src/dispatch.ts`)
reports an ERROR or missing node anywhere from the header's end to the member's `begin`, and
`reachLatchRefusedOwner` refuses such a member. It gets no latch and no marker: its mutants are
`reachGrain "unplaced"`, reach `not-decided`, never unreached. It is still instrumented and scored.
`generateMutationSet` names it in a `reach-latch-refused` warning that says its var section did
not parse cleanly. Both shapes are pinned in `packages/schemata/tests/compile.test.ts` (the R303
describe) and the two-blocks shape in `packages/runner/tests/preproc-instrumentation.test.ts`.

**What stays open:** reach is not measured for these members. The fix belongs in the grammar: the
two shapes are filed upstream as SShadowS/tree-sitter-al issue 31
(<https://github.com/SShadowS/tree-sitter-al/issues/31>). When a grammar release parses them as blocks,
check whether R303's hoist anchor covers them (alc-prove every symbol subset) before removing this
refusal. The refusal also catches any other parse failure inside a member's header-to-`begin`
region, which is the intended fail-safe direction. It does not catch [[R309]]'s
`preproc_split_procedure_preamble`, which parses cleanly but has no procedure-like owner; [[R309]]
refuses it by name with its own sentence. The TRIGGER form of that shape (each `#if` arm holding
its own trigger header and `var` section) does not parse as a preamble in tree-sitter-al 4.4.1,
leaves an ERROR, and IS caught by this item's refusal instead (measured on `p5-trigger` in the
R-309 plan; filed upstream as SShadowS/tree-sitter-al #32).
