Coord PR-109 (lethal-code, 2026-11-17). Found by PR-108's analysis: DO t13's M36641 (`ReadAsText`) hung in 19 of 22 covering
tests, about 1,981 s. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-pr109-eos-pre-read-hang-rule.md`, with Addendum 1. Method: PR-44's.

**The shape.**
- S is an InStream (a local, parameter or global) read by `S.Read`/`S.ReadText`, as a statement or as an assignment's right
  side.
- That read is in a block that also holds `while not S.EOS do` (or `not (S.EOS)`), in a procedure or a trigger.
- Removing the `not` makes the loop run while S is at its end. After the pre-read of a short stream it is, so the loop never
  exits.

**Not refused:**
- no pre-read;
- a read only in a branch or loop the `while` is not inside;
- S written or EOS-tested between the last read and the loop;
- `not` combined with `and`/`or`.

**Accepted over-refusal:** a `break`/`exit` in the loop body. No measured site has one.

**Measured** (`/coord/handoff/PR-109/measure.md`):
- **Corpus:** the 72 BC.History modules show 0 sites removed.
- **Fixtures:** 15 targets, 0 sites moved.
- **DO:** 45021 -> 45014. The 7 refused sites, with their t13 verdicts:
  - M25653 `GetEMail` and M36641 `ReadAsText`: timeout-killed;
  - M36508 `ReadStreamToString`: killed;
  - 4 siblings of `GetEMail`: no-coverage.
- **Rate:** 2 hangs per 3 scored lost, far above PR-37's 1 per 8.

**P4 (pre-committed):** the first DO window on a head with PR-109 shows these 7 sites absent and no timeout close from them. Its
baseline is re-run (the mutation set changed). lethal-bugs has the list.
