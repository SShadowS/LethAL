**Found by R-214's pre-commitment (2026-09-30, ruling T1-g).** A `#if` that opens in a `var`
section parses as `preproc_conditional_var`. When its `#endif` sits after several procedures, the
tree makes those procedures children of that node. Declarations inside it are not seen by the
symbol table or type layer, so operators that need them decline there, and the Tier-1 mutant the
compiled build would give to Tier 2 stays on master.

**Rule:** smallest directive node `preproc_conditional_var` and an operator whose
`requiresSemantic` is non-empty.

**Lost.** Corpora: dc.0 3 (`swap-call-arguments`, `.dependencies/DC/Codeunit/CDCCaptureRTCLibrary.Codeunit.al`
L90, L100, L210); baseapp.0 72 (+14 displaced); all others 0. Repro t2 of R364 (a `var` line in an
arm) shows the operator declining.

**Out of R-214's scope.** A typing change; part of R364.

**Closed 2026-10-05 as a ruling, together with R364** (see R364 for the shared paragraph). The
R-364 census (`/coord/handoff/R-364/measurement.md`, per `app.json` project: BaseApp's 203, CDO at
5f2a71d, fixtures and examples) found this item in BaseApp under `[]` only: 92 sites lost in 12
files and 14 wrong-tier rows (`void-method-call` 11, `flip-boolean-literal` 3), and 0 everywhere
else. The dominant shape is a member-level `#if` placed directly after the global var section,
which the grammar stretches into `preproc_conditional_var`; the second is conditional global
labels. The wrong-tier rows compile (an alc probe of both shapes compiled every master mutant) and
are misattributed, not wrong. With R364 that is 0.013% of BaseApp's typed mutants. The fix for a
reopen is recorded on R364: read var-section `#if` declarations by arm, and treat a stretched var
`#if` as member-level.
