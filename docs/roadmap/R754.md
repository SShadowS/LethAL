**Filed and done 2026-10-25 by lethal-preproc (coord task R-754).** Related: [[R752]], [[R731]], R402.

**Cause.** `scripts/r402-shape-sweep.ts` checks, for every shape under `scripts/fixtures/r402` and every build, the
refusal reason or the spec count from `expected.json`, and compiles source and artifact with alc. It ran only by hand,
because it needs alc. R731 (69478059) re-ran only its own new shapes, so `w6_var_swallowed_table` went from 29 specs to
28 on master unseen, until R-737 ran the whole sweep ([[R752]]).

**Done.**
- **One shared half:** `scripts/r402-shapes.ts` holds the generate-and-judge code. `writeShapeProject`, `runShape` and
  `judgeShape` keep the sweep's exact rules, and `R402_BUILDS`, `r402Expected` and `r402ShapeNames` give the builds,
  the expectations and the shape list. The sweep calls them and keeps only the two alc compiles. Its output is
  byte-identical to before (`/coord/handoff/R-754/sweep-before.txt`, `sweep-after.txt`, also with `--repo`).
- **The unit pin:** `scripts/r402-shapes.test.ts` runs every shape under every build (47 × 4 = 188 cases) through the
  real `generateMutationSet`, with no alc, no symbols, no /coord and no server, and requires `judgeShape` to report
  nothing.
  - It also pins the shape count (47), the build count (4), one spec count per build in every entry, and that every
    shape has an entry and every entry a shape.
  - It runs without `.alpackages`: measured equal, 0 of 188 cases differ with and without them.
- **Known drift:** `KNOWN_DRIFT` holds w6 at R752's current 28/15/28/15. The test asserts those exact figures and that
  `expected.json` still disagrees on exactly builds 0 and 2. When R-752 lands (29 again), it goes red until the entry is
  removed. `expected.json` was not edited.
- **Red-checked per direction** (`/coord/handoff/R-754/red-checks.md`):
  - a dropped spec, or an added spec: all 144 admitted cases red;
  - an unexpected refusal on an admitted shape: red;
  - a refusal reason changed: all 44 refused cases red;
  - the drift resolved (29) or moved further (27): red;
  - a shape with no entry, or an entry with no shape: red;
  - a shape-count pin or a build-count pin off: red.

**Limits.**
- The compile half (the source and the instrumented artifact) stays in the manual sweep, which needs alc.
- Only spec COUNTS and refusal reasons are pinned. A swap (one spec lost and another gained), a changed operator or a
  flipped hang tag passes both the test and the sweep.
- "Reads no symbols" rests on that one measurement. A future symbol-dependent change would most likely LOWER the
  test's counts, which goes red.
