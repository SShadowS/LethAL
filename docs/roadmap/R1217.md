**Found by** PR-435 (lethal-code, 2026-11-25), measured on c5's events. Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-435/findings.md`.
The figures are shares, because c5 ran under load.

- **What was measured:**
  - c5's baseline phase took 1,627.8 s of a 23,860.6 s run (6.8%).
  - All 104 baseline serve requests ran on lane 0, one after another (1,403.0 s; al-runner inside them: compile
    413.8 s, coverage 431.0 s, tests 97.1 s).
  - The 12 R951 cross-check one-shots (997.1 s) overlapped them (PR-119). The other lanes were idle for the whole
    phase.
- **The lever:** spread `fillDaemonBaseline`'s requests over the lanes' daemons. At 5 lanes the bound is about 4-5% of
  the run's wall. Each extra lane pays a first daemon start, which the bound does not yet subtract.
- **Design to start from:** R1190's parked r2 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-pr434-per-test-baseline-spread.md`)
  was written for the per-test fill, and most of it transfers:
  - one tree for every baseline daemon;
  - kept-form outcomes only;
  - PR-98's admission after lane 0's first answer;
  - in-flight-safe closes and ordered throws;
  - per-lane samples and a laned gate leg.
  The whole-codeunit fill adds PR-115's batching and PR-119's concurrent samples, which the spread must keep.
- **Why this is new:** R1190 was parked because DO's MAIN baseline is session-isolated and never takes the per-test
  path. This item is that same main baseline's own one-lane cost.
- **Related:** R1190, PR-434, PR-115, PR-119, R951, R1216.

**Closed by measurement, PR-437 (lethal-preproc, 2026-11-25).** Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-pr437-r1217-baseline-spread.md` (v4, amendments 1 and 2). Evidence:
`/coord/handoff/PR-437/` (`premise.md`, `arms/RESULT.md`, the reviews, `redchecks.md`).
- **What was built:**
  - the requests AND the cross-check samples spread over the lanes;
  - today's loop stays the only decision maker in `ids` order;
  - one tree (lane 0's);
  - lanes join under `decideLanes`' two checks, with two one-shot slots reserved;
  - per-daemon warm samples.
  - The samples had to be spread too, because they are 61-76% of the baseline phase in every full DO run on record.
  - Three spec reviews and three build reviews; 13 red checks; VERIFY PASS 16095/0.
  - **Parked unmerged:** branch `lethal/pr-437` at ff7d94cfb.
- **The paired DO arms** (scoped full-suite baseline, isolation test, 5 workers, CPUs 0-11, heavy-slot peak 25, fresh
  stores):
  - **Correctness held:** for 786 mutants, verdicts, killing tests and covering sets were equal across all four arms, and
    the 1979 baseline tests were equal.
  - **The cost bar (65% of the paired one-lane arm) was MISSED:**
    - A1 858 s (cold cache, load 270);
    - B1 321 s (load 210; 60% of the A mean, confounded by A1's cold cache);
    - A2 205 s (load 66);
    - B2 189 s (load 43): **92%** of A2.
- **The cause, measured (A2 against B2, both warm, low load):**
  - with 4 daemons at once on the same 12 CPUs, each request got about 3x slower (request wall sum 187 s -> 529 s);
  - one-shots got about 2x slower (92 s -> 203 s).
  - An al-runner baseline request is already multi-core (compile, coverage), so LethAL's idle lanes are not idle CPUs. The
    phase is bound by CPU on the run's taskset, not by one daemon's sequencing.
- **Not shown, and not re-measured toward the bar:** whether the spread gains when other jobs leave CPUs idle on the run's
  set.
