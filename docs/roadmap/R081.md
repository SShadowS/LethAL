**`buildCallerIndex` keys call sites on the BARE owner name, so two same-named objects merge their callers — the sibling of R70.** `siteKey(owner, proc)` (`packages/engine/src/semantic/callers.ts`) built `"<owner>::<proc>"` from `obj.name`, so `table 50000 "CDO Setup".Configure` and `page 50000 "CDO Setup".Configure` landed in ONE bucket and each was reported as a caller of the other. The row asked for a MEASUREMENT before a fix — *what reads `callersOf`, and can a merged bucket reach a claim or a verdict?*
