**Stay on the newest tree-sitter-al.** Policy, not a defect: a grammar bump silently changed node shapes once (v2.5.0 → v3.0.1 inserted container nodes and zeroed `statementCalls` 703,239 → 0). The bump procedure — corpus site counts plus a **per-site** baseline proof, not a multiset signature — is in `packages/engine/vendor/README.md`. Re-check on every upstream release.
