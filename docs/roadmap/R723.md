**Filed 2026-10-24 by R-586d (lethal-bugs), owner design.** The daily run gated alpha.3 and alpha.4 (both predicted fails, about
75 minutes) before alpha.5.

**Done.** `scripts/al-runner-v3-update.ts` now gates the newest candidate first. A pass becomes `current` and ends the run. Anything
else (`gate-fail`, `held`, `infra-fail`, or a skip because `failed/<tag>@<commit>` exists) falls back to the next older candidate,
down to but not including `current`. `--edge` is unchanged. Versions compare by the alpha number as a number (alpha.10 after alpha.9).
Tests in `scripts/al-runner-v3-update.test.ts`; red checks in `/coord/handoff/R-586d/red/`. The gate page
(`docs/gates/alrunner-v3.md`, "The pin and the update gate") states the order and what is no longer measured: older releases skipped
after a newer one passes get no daily verdict, and their last record stays in `failed/`.
