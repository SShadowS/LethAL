**Filed 2026-11-06 by preproc.** Related: [[R830]], [[R829]].

**What.** [[R830]] refuses by name, the safe direction: a wrong refusal loses one mutant, a miss can end a run. Tracing 5 large BC.History
files (213 sites) showed two groups of refusals that cannot hang:
- `TestField` and `FieldError`: 17 of 60 receiver sites. Neither moves its receiver. They are refused when a helper in the `until` steps
  the record, because the rule does not see that step.
- A by-value argument of a simple type to a same-object procedure: 40 of 213 sites. Reference types such as `List` must stay refused, and
  temporary records need a check.

**Next.** Measure both exemptions with the production dump. Each re-adds sites, so ordinals move and it needs a scheme bump.
