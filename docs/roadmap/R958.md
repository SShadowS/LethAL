Found by the Opus delta review of coord task R-944 (R955), 2026-11-12. Related: [[R955]], [[R866]], [[R790]].

**What.** `fillIsolatedBaseline` (al-runner-v3-backend.ts, R866) starts at the first baseline call under `--isolate-tests` and runs
every suite test's process, `ISOLATED_BASELINE_PROCESSES` at a time, from a shared queue. A SIGTERM/SIGINT kills the processes running
at that moment (their results are rejected as non-clean answers), but the workers keep taking tests from the queue until it is empty;
only then does `scoreBatch` see the signal and stop. So a stop can take as long as a full isolated baseline. `speculateBaseline` (R790)
has the same per-codeunit tail. Cost only: nothing is scored after the stop, and no killed row is kept (R955).

**Wanted.** The fill's workers check the session's stop (the `TimeBudget`, passed in) before taking the next test, as the baseline
loop does.

**Done (R-956).** Found on build: R948 had already put the `spent()` check (budget, signal, memory) in the one-shot fill's workers; it
had no test, now it has (stop after 2 with one worker, after 4 with four, never stopped). `speculateBaseline` lacked it: added, with a
test. `baselineStopped` is counted by the orchestrator's baseline loop and was not changed.
