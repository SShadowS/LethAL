**Found by the R-214 lane (lethal-preproc), 2026-10-01.** Evidence: the A/B measurement in
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-29-R-214-symbol-aware-site-enumeration/flake-report.md` on that lane's
branch, with raw outputs on the lane's scratch disk (`H:/lethal-scratch/R-214/flake/`).

**What happens.** Now and then a full `bun test` run ends with two failures, always the same pair.
First, the `afterAll` of `scratchDirs()` (`packages/runner/tests/helpers/scratch.ts`) cannot delete a
`lethal-run-verify-*` folder: `R358: could not remove ...: EBUSY`. Second, the preload guard (R358)
then reports the folder as left behind. The folder is made by `runThenArtifact` in
`packages/runner/tests/cli.test.ts`, which the R358 and R360 run-then-verify tests use.

**It is not caused by recent work, and it is not proven to be only load.** The suite was run at
the R-214 branch and at its base commit, alternating one run at a time under the same load. The
branch failed 2 of 6 full runs and the base failed 3 of 6. Two of those runs failed in both trees
back to back. Running `cli.test.ts` alone failed 1 of 10 on the branch and 2 of 10 on the base. One
base run also had two 5-second timeouts in other tests, which suggests the machine was busy.

**Likely cause, not proven.** No unclosed database, child process or watcher was found in the
code: `cli.ts` closes its stores in `finally` blocks. The best fit is a short Windows lock on the
run's `lethal.sqlite` (or its WAL file) that outlasts the cleanup's retry: three tries, 100 ms
apart. R166 was an earlier EBUSY with a different cause (an unclosed store), and it is fixed.

**Why it matters.** An intermittent red in the full suite costs every lane time. It also tempts
people to "retry until green", which hides a real leak if one ever appears here.

**Fix direction.** First find the holder. One way: on a failure, list what has the folder open,
or log the WAL checkpoint state at close time. Then fix that at its source. If it is the
OS-level delay after closing SQLite, give the cleanup a bounded retry with backoff that is long
enough, and make it name the file it could not delete. Never weaken the R358 guard.

**Second occurrence (2026-10-01).** `itest:chunked` printed `chunked itest: PASS`, then exited 1 with `EBUSY: resource busy or locked, rm '...\lethal-itest-chunked-...'`. It came from the temp-directory cleanup at the end of `main` in `packages/runner/itest/chunked.itest.ts`. This is the same class: a Windows lock on a run's temp folder after the run. A cleanup EBUSY can turn a passing gate red.
