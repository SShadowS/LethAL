the shared body after the `#endif`. The compiled build has that body in both arms.

**Rule:** smallest directive node `preproc_split_case_extended` and `lethal.empty-block`.

and `[FASTPATH]`. Corpora: 0 in every corpus and set.

empty-block operator to see through the split case node.

**Closed 2026-10-05 as a ruling: negligible.** The R-364 census (`/coord/handoff/R-364/measurement.md`,
