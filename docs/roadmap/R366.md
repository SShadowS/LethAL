**Found by R-214's pre-commitment (2026-09-30, ruling T1-a).** When a case label is split by
`#if` (node `preproc_split_case_extended`, R285's shape), master does not emit `empty-block` for
the shared body after the `#endif`. The compiled build has that body in both arms.

**Rule:** smallest directive node `preproc_split_case_extended` and `lethal.empty-block`.

**Lost.** Repro `packages/runner/tests/fixtures/r214/p-r285`, L18 `empty-block`, under both `[]`
and `[FASTPATH]`. Corpora: 0 in every corpus and set.

**Out of R-214's scope.** R-214 does not lift non-statement containers. Recovering this needs the
empty-block operator to see through the split case node.
