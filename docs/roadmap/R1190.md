**Filed 2026-11-23 by lethal-code (coord task PR-370), from R1189's measurement.** Evidence: the PR-370 pre-commitment
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-pr370-recheck-phase.md` §"The daemon-reuse lever", and `/coord/handoff/PR-370/`
(`gap.py` over read-only copies of PR-357's c3 and c3off events and stderr).

**What costs time.** The recheck (`runWithheldRecheck`, `cli.ts`'s recheck runner) closes the main session's daemons and
runs a nested session under method isolation. That session regenerates an instrumented workspace holding only the targets,
deploys it and starts its own daemons. On DO (c3, c3off) its serve-entries line shows 28 daemons and a warm-up of 420-499
lane-seconds: about 84-100 s of wall at 5 lanes. Its generate and deploy were not recorded before PR-370; the `recheck`
phase's detail records them from now on.

**What reuse would need.** The nested session would have to run against the MAIN instrumented build (a superset of its
targets) on the main daemons, switched to method isolation per request. That changes the recheck's narrowing
(`exactSites`) and its build identity checks; it is a design change, not a flag.

**How to judge it.** The first DO run with PR-370's `recheck` phase gives the nested generate, deploy and mutants times.
If the reusable part (warm-ups plus generate plus deploy) is a small share of the 15-17 min gap, close this by ruling.

**Measured by PR-434 (lethal-code, 2026-11-25; `/coord/handoff/PR-434/findings.md`).**
- **c5's recheck** (2,120.8 s of a 23,860.6 s run): generate 23.9 s, deploy 47.3 s, baseline 757.3 s, mutants
  1,278.0 s.
  - The reuse this item names saves at most generate + deploy + the first warm-ups, about 124 s (5.8% of the recheck).
  - The baseline ran on ONE al-runner for its whole 757 s.
- **Why one.** A method-isolated (or `--isolate-tests`) baseline goes through `fillIsolatedViaDaemon`: one request per
  test, on one lane's daemon, in sequence.
- **m1** (a method-isolation DO run, `measure.sh`): the fill took 1,788.0 s.
  - Daemon requests: 1,979, taking 1,695.1 s (94.8%).
  - Cross-check one-shots: 16, taking 91.0 s.
- **The larger lever, designed, not built:** spread that fill over the lanes' daemons.
  - Pre-commitment: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-pr434-per-test-baseline-spread.md`. r2 answers a spec-adversary
    pass with 3 blockers.
  - Its prize is about 400 s of c5's recheck (about 2% of the run). It would be about 1,700 s per run for a main
    session that is method-isolated or `--isolate-tests`.
  - DO's reference runs (c4, c5) are session-isolated, so on DO only the recheck gains.
  - r2's cost is a new gate leg (DBI-W) and about 10 red tests. Parked at the orchestrator's ruling.
- **Unpark when** a method-isolated or `--isolate-tests` main run becomes a reference configuration, or when the recheck
  grows to a larger share of a run.
- **Part 1 (the per-test fill receipt and the nested recheck's own TIMING)** is built and red-checked on branch
  `lethal/pr-434`, but not landed.
