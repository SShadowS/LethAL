**Filed 2026-10-22 by lethal-bugs during R-658** (the plan's deep review, M10; orchestrator ruling 4).

**What.** R658 wires LethAL Control's stop for every unmutated run that goes through `BcDevMcpBackend.runViaTransport`
and `runMany`. Measured on bc3 (`docs/measurements/r658/` on `lethal/r658`):
- a stopped baseline test (busy loop or `Sleep`) gets BC's AL-stop 408 within about 75 ms;
- an abandoned one strands the op marker (row D2).

A bcdev session with `coverageMode` `"procedure"` (the legacy hub) or `"line"` collects its baseline through the
bc-dev-mcp hub, not through the fenced RunMutant transport. So the stop hook R658 adds never reaches that baseline.

**Who hits it.** A user who sets one of those modes, and has a baseline test that hangs or runs past the budget.
`"procedure"` is due to be deleted (see `coverageMode`'s doc comment in `bcdev-backend.ts`).

**To close.** First measure what a hub-mode baseline overrun leaves on the server: is the marker stranded, and is the
session alive? Then one of:
- refuse an overrun by name in those modes;
- wire an equivalent stop;
- or close this by ruling, once `"procedure"` is deleted and `"line"` is shown not to strand.

Evidence: R658 (`docs/roadmap/R658.md`); R-658's plan review (`/coord/handoff/R-658/review-plan-r1-deep.md`, M10).
