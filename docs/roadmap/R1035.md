Coord task PR-24. Pre-commitment `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-pr24-l2-lazy-warmup.md`; results
`docs/campaign/2026-11-15-pr24-l2-lazy-warmup/results.md`. Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-24/`. Related: [[R1008]] (PR-21's L5
re-check), R635 (the warm-up), R612 (the cuts that restart a daemon), R952 (recycles).

**What.** Every new serve daemon, first or restarted, sent one NONE warm-up over the session's whole union of codeunits (R635 §3).
On DO that is the whole suite. In t4 so far there have been at least 18 restarts, all R612 cuts.

**The change.**
- **The first mutant daemon** of each lane and mode still warms the whole union, with R786/R960's fallback.
- **A restarted daemon** proves, in `ensureDaemon`, exactly the codeunits the caller is about to run, before they run. It uses a
  NONE warm-up judged by the same `judgeWarmup`: echo, agreement with the baseline without `leftOutNames`' tests, and R612 clean.
- **Every entry path passes it:** the proof sits in `ensureDaemon` (not in `request()`/`send()`, as the spec put it). Every entries
  request (`requestOne`: prefetch, live calls, kill pairs, PR-20's widening live call) and the §5 coverage window calls it with the
  codeunits it is about to run.
- **Rules:**
  - A lazy warm-up does not count toward R952's request bound, so a restarted daemon serves `daemonMaxRequests` requests, not one
    fewer. (The spec's reason, a loop, was wrong: the bound is checked only on an existing daemon.)
  - It counts in `stats.warmups`, and R987's `noteEntryCost` does not sample it.
  - It has NO spent-budget check of its own, as master's whole-union restart warm-up has none. The spec's rule 2 was wrong, and
    lethal-code's review found it (B1, below).
  - Any failure, including no baseline rows, closes the daemon.

**Review B1 (lethal-code's Opus review of d9b547b3), fixed.**
- **The defect:** the first build refused a lazy warm-up once the budget was spent, with a "deadline" error. On a live call, a
  kill's confirm or a coverage window, `liveFailure` turns that into a `deadline-exceeded` verdict. So in a `--time-budget` run, a
  mutant whose test 2 met a restarted daemon just after the budget ran out was recorded `deadline-exceeded`, where master gives a
  real verdict.
- **The fix:** remove the check. Prefetch and pair batches already check `spent()` before calling `request()`.
- **Red test:** a live call on a spent budget still warms up and returns its real verdict, and with the check put back it is red.

**Departure from the pre-commitment.**
- **The spec said:** a failed lazy warm-up is "today's later-daemon refusal by name, never a fallback".
- **The build:** a lazy warm-up shares `judgeWarmup` with the whole-union warm-up, so a dirty row is judged exactly as today's
  restarted daemons judge it. That includes R786's one-shot fallback on the default transport, when every unkept row is a named
  refusal.
- **Why:** it is the same judgement on a smaller set and adds no new rule. lethal-code agreed (2026-11-15).
- **The other path:** the whole-request refusal (`entries-batch-refused`) still falls back only at the session's first warm-up,
  as before.
- **On any failure:** the daemon is closed and no entry of the codeunit is sent on it.

**A spec claim withdrawn.** The spec called it a gain that a restarted daemon warms a codeunit that joined the union after start.
Master already warms such a codeunit on every restart, through the whole union. What stays open, as on master, is the FIRST daemon:
a codeunit that joins after it started is never proven there. Not closed here.

**Why no verdict changes.** Every mutant entry still runs on a daemon where its codeunits' NONE rows were just compared. Entries
are independent: measured by the carry-over probe (24 of 24 readings, positive controls), and by lethal-bugs' probes. Fixture legs
with a restart after every request: IDENTICAL per mutant on sandbox-app (22) and the table fixture (418), with equal daemons.

**Tests.** Eight, each red on its revert:
- no loop, with equal verdicts;
- exact codeunits proven;
- a drifting codeunit refused before its entry;
- a live call on a spent budget keeps its verdict;
- a kill pair preceded by its codeunit's warm-up;
- the request bound;
- the first mutant daemon after a daemon-filled baseline warms the whole union (rule 4);
- `leftOutNames`. This one passes on a full revert as well, since master's whole-union warm-up uses the same names, so it pins the
  argument rather than the laziness.

The coverage window and PR-20's widening call `ensureDaemon` with their codeunit but have no test of their own.

**Saving.** None on the fixtures, whose requests span the whole union. On DO (PR-18's trace), restarted daemons used 24, 4 and 3 of
110 codeunits: about 39 s of tests saved per restart.

**To close:** the DO figure per window, pre-committed in an amendment from t4's restart count once t4 is scored, then measured by
the next DO window.

**Pre-committed (amendment 1 of the spec, 2026-11-15, before any DO window runs L2).** t4 (before L2):
- 86 mutant daemons;
- 83 warm-ups taking 7,322 s, which is 58% of its 12,600 s window and about 88 s each.

Prediction for the next DO window with L2:

The range is wide, and the spec's amendment says why. What remains open: the next DO window measures it.
