**Built.**
- After a `lethal run` narrowed by `--lines` or `--changed-since`, the console ends with **SURVIVORS ON THE CHANGED LINES**.
  It lists every `survived` mutant (no accept list yet, so every survivor counts as new). Each survivor whose site carries an
  al-runner-v3 caveat is listed apart, with its reasons (`changed-line-survivors.ts`). The caveats:
  - a covering test's baseline flagged, or its flag unknown;
  - the file's coverage off;
  - the report's restrictive-default at-risk survivor list.
  On any backend other than al-runner-v3, nothing is listed apart.
- `--fail-on-survivors` exits **9** when the counted list is not empty. Exits 3, 4 and 7 win over 9. Without the flag the run is
  advisory (exit 0). The flag is refused without a line filter, and refused on `--dry-run`.
- The CLI usage, the agent reference's exit-code table, `docs/ci/` (README, both templates map 9 to a failure, and the flag is
  named in a comment as advisory) and `threshold-options.md` ("Ruling and first step").
