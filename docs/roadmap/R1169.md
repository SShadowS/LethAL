**Found by PR-307's sizing** (`/coord/handoff/PR-307/sizing.txt`, approximate): of the 60 v3-vs-bcdev DO differences that
PR-299's GuiAllowed scan predicted, the static rule (H, `HandlerFunctions`; T, TestPage) flags 55.

**The 5 it misses** all fail with a client and pass headless. App code raises a Confirm that no handler answers, and
headless BC answers it by default. Nothing in the test's own source shows it.

**What closing it needs:**
- a post-baseline join: the test's per-test coverage against app (and dependency) procedures that hold `GuiAllowed`,
  `CurrentClientType` or UI calls;
- its precision measured on DO before it may label or refuse.
