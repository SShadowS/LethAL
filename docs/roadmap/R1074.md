Filed and closed by coord PR-60 (2026-11-16). Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-pr60-update-gate-small-allowance.md`
(addendum 1). Related: [[R992]] (the small-job allowance), [[R952]] (memory admission).

**What.** On 2026-11-16 at 07:47Z the daily update was skipped: "used 8.2, held slots' peaks 28.0 (3), this job's peak 2.0,
reserve 4.0 -> REFUSED". `scripts/al-runner-v3-update.ts`'s `gateMemory` built its own inputs for `memoryVerdict`. It passed no
`smallHeld`, so R992's allowance never applied, and it left out the unslotted heavy processes the guard counts.

**Fix.** One helper, `heldInputs` in `scripts/heavy-slot-guard.ts`, used by the guard and the update alike. It returns the held
peaks (unslotted heavy jobs included, an unclassed slot at the largest default) and `smallHeld`, which is undefined and so fails
closed when a held class is unreadable. A small admission writes the ledger line naming the update. `free -g` (the host) is
consulted only where no cgroup limit exists, and its log says so.

**Accepted ceiling.** The update holds no slot, so during its gate a third small job may be admitted: at most about 2 GiB above
the declared sum, once a day.
