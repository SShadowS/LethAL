Filed 2026-11-16 by PR-88 (lethal-code). Spec of what PR-88 did instead: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-pr88-entry-cost-per-codeunit.md`.

**Why PR-88 did not close R1058.** R987 charges each request's average growth per test entry to every call (codeunit) in it, and
keeps each call's highest value. Costs never fall. That is safe, and it is also why a light codeunit first measured beside a heavy
one keeps the heavy figure (R1058: DO windows printed caps of 38 to 230 for the same project). Two spec-adversary passes on PR-88
showed that any rule which LOWERS a cost from a mixed reading can run the container out of memory:
- entries of one codeunit differ in cost per mutant;
- a per-call count limit bounds entries, not bytes;
- readings clean enough to lower from may be rare, since most prefetch entries carry L4 cuts or R998 left-out names.
PR-88 fixed only the sensor (the true peak through VmHWM) and added the `serve-request-sized` event.

**Possible fix.** Before a codeunit's first prefetch request, send one calibration request: a few whole-codeunit entries of that
codeunit alone. Each codeunit then has a cost of its own, independent of co-packing. Keep it max-only.

**Probe first.** From the `serve-request-sized` events of a DO window after PR-88, measure:
- how many codeunits a window prefetches;
- what a calibration request would cost;
- how often entries are cut (L4, R998);
- how spread one codeunit's per-entry costs are across mutants.

**To close.** Caps that do not depend on co-packing, with a test, and a DO window's events showing it.

**Closed by measurement (coord PR-168, 2026-11-18).** The probe asked for above, on DO y1's events
(`/coord/handoff/PR-168/result.md`):
- the window prefetched 110 codeunits in 5,066 requests;
- 829 requests (16%) were at a lowered cap.
Even with no cap at all, those requests would save only 0.74-1.69% of the mutant phase (see [[R1058]]). Calibration
requests, which add requests of their own, can only save less. Not built (orchestrator ruling: about 3% or more to build).
