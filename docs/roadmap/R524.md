**Filed 2026-10-08 by the [[R500]] build** (plan r3 `/coord/reviews/R-500-plan/plan.r3.md`, shape 2). A plausible
hang that is NOT refused.

**What.** R500 refuses the subscribers of an event that open report-item code raises, and of an event a refused
one-hop callee raises (the PEPPOL Management `OnFindNext*` shape). An event raised from a subscriber, or from
anything two or more hops away, is not followed, so its subscribers are still mutated. A subscriber that sets a
`var` result the item's exit reads (the r1 hang) is the same risk there.

## Closed by ruling

R-522 measurement, 2026-10-23 (`/coord/handoff/R-522/measure.md`, section R524). Events-only fixpoint: 0 rows beyond
master. With hop-2 callees added: 276 rows, all in BaseApp (level 1: 34, level 2: 242). Consumption read: nothing
208, other 60, same-rec 8; the 8 same-rec rows are Package Management subscribers that only narrow a filter. 185
subscribers write a var parameter (65 distinct events). The real cover is R569 (`exitFedHop2`): the 276 rows are the
hop-2 raises that do NOT feed the exit. Stated limit: R569's own entry-var gap (a hop-1 callee entered through a var
argument from the exit's scope) stays; the raising procedure's own callers are not followed. Pairs with R520.

**To close (original plan, closed by the ruling above).** Count the second-level events with production code; refuse their subscribers or close it with a
ruling. Pair it with [[R520]] (callees beyond one hop).
