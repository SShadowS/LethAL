Found by coord task R-964 (BC.History batch 11, 2026-11-13, `/coord/handoff/R-964/`). Related: [[R960]].

**What happened.** APIV1's 467 tests: 454 not green at baseline (271 refused because `Api Web Service` has no provider, upstream
#7775 row 4; 178 follow-ons; 5 other refusals). The session ran its baseline (417 s), then went through the mutant phase anyway:
100 daemon starts, every one closed at once as a refusal, and 5,442 mutants written `no-coverage` with no mutant run. PlanConfiguration
(30 of 31 red, 7 mutants) did the same on a small scale. The figure is right (nothing is covered), but the mutant phase cost time and
daemon starts for nothing, and the report does not say plainly that the baseline left nothing to measure.

**Wanted.** After the baseline, if no mutant has a covering test that passed (or the green share is under a stated floor), end the
session with a named outcome ("baseline leaves nothing to measure: N of M tests not green, by cause") instead of the mutant phase,
and keep the report's verdicts as they are. Not decided: the floor, and whether it is a refusal or a warning.

**Closed by ruling (orchestrator, 2026-11-13, coord R-967): the premise was wrong.** APIV1's own log: `phase: mutants, estimate: 0
covered mutant(s)` and `phase: mutants done in 0.0s`. The mutant phase already skips when nothing is covered, and the report's first
lines say so. The 481 s were deploy (47 s) and the baseline (417 s); the 100 daemon starts were IN the baseline: every codeunit refused
with `store error platform table 'Api Web Service' ... has no provider` closed its serve daemon, and the next started fresh. That cost
is a keep-list question (R945's method), filed as R981.
