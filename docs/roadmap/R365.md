**Found by R-214's pre-commitment (2026-09-30, ruling T1-a).** A member declared once per arm of a
declaration-level `#if` (node `preproc_conditional`) exists twice in the tree, so the operand types
of an operator that reads the semantic layer do not resolve in it, and the operator declines.

**Rule** (R-214's presence check, `$Q/presence.ts`): smallest directive node `preproc_conditional`
and an operator whose `requiresSemantic` is non-empty.

**Lost.** Repro `packages/runner/tests/fixtures/r214/p-r214` under `[]`: the L11 `swap-additive`.
Corpora (compiled-build rows master lacks, plus the Tier-1 rows they displace): bcf.0 5; dc.0 29
(+2 displaced); sysapp.0 5; baseapp.0 745 (+284 displaced); every S1 set 0.

**Out of R-214's scope.** R-214 drops compiled-out sites and lifts statement-level arms; it does
not change how the semantic layer resolves names. Fixing this is part of R364's typing work.

**Closed 2026-10-05 as a ruling: its own shape is fixed.** R-405a's `liveMembers` reads a member
declared once per arm by arm. The R-364 census (`/coord/handoff/R-364/measurement.md`, per
`app.json` project: BaseApp's 203, CDO at 5f2a71d, fixtures and examples, under `[]` and under each
project's own symbols) shows it: repro `r364/t3-dup-member` has zero mismatches under both sets, and
an alc probe gets typed mutants in both arms. The residue under this rule is 20 sites in 8 files
and 1 wrong-tier row, BaseApp under `[]` only, and it is other shapes: 9 conditional global labels
(R369's class), 8 a split object header (R305), 2 plus the wrong row R343, 1 not determined. 0 in
CDO, the fixtures and every symbol set.
