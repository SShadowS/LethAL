**Filed 2026-11-05 by lethal-preproc, from coord task R-813.** Related: [[R658]], [[R598]].

**What.** The wrapper for `itest:hang` takes its coord lease in lane `bugs`, defaults its worktree to
`/work/lethal-wt/r658`, and writes "lane bugs" into the out-of-use marker. Run from another lane, it would lease in
another lane's name, which R598 forbids. So R-783 and R-813 ran `itest:hang` directly, under their own lease.

**What that loses.** The wrapper's safety net. On a STOP or an armed BASELINE leg it holds the lease, heartbeats and
writes the out-of-use marker. R-813's driver (`/coord/handoff/R-813/gate.sh`) copied the hold, not the marker.

**To do.** Take the lane by `--lane`, as `bc-linux-setup.ts` does, and refuse with none. Take the worktree from where the
script runs. Name the lane in the marker. Update `scripts/r658-hang-gate.test.ts`.
