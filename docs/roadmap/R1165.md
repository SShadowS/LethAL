Found by coord PR-281 (lethal-code, 2026-11-21). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-281/result.md` and `ab/compare.txt`. Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-pr281-costliest-first.md`.

**What PR-281 built:** the v3 parallel queue can hand out whole procedure scopes costliest-first within each window (under the
split), with the same cost model `cutWindows` uses. It is opt-in, `LETHAL_V3_UNIT_ORDER=costliest`, because the default
stays `manifest` (the orchestrator's ruling). Two side effects of a reordered queue were closed in the same change: the
daemon-churn count now resets on a FINISHED mutant, and the session-first warm-up takes its mode from the manifest-first
mutant.

**What the A/B showed:** PR-274's 22-file DO slice, 8 lanes, A = manifest, B = costliest.
- **Verdict safety: proven.** All five arms (A1 void under the N rule, re-run as A3) gave identical verdicts and
  `killingTest`s, with no churn and the same warm-up.
- **The pre-committed prediction was missed:** mean B / mean A = 1.13 (≤ 0.85 required). A: 2,354 and 2,366 s. B: 2,513 and
  2,844 s.
- **The cause is the box:** both B arms ran under heavier outside load. Per-test time was 39-47 ms in B against 25-28 ms in A
  (1.65×), and CPU pressure 34-36% against 16-27%. That load is mostly alrunner's work, outside the heavy-slot gate.
- **Lower in B:** the idle tail in lane-s (5,015 against 6,489, -23%).
- **Not claimed:** a per-test-time correction gives B about 0.69 × A, but it was not pre-committed.

**To do:** a new A/B that pre-commits load-robust criteria:
- the idle tail in lane-s;
- the wall normalised by per-test time;
- more interleaved rounds (for example A, B, A, B, A, B), so outside load cannot land on one arm.
If it shows the gain, make `costliest` the default. Raise `MAX_V3_WORKERS` past 8 (PR-274's follow-up 2) only after that.
