**The residual.** [[R204]] closed as narrowed: a stop that lands after a test's last statement can
still score `timeout-killed` for a test that passed, through W1 (the `between` write rolled back under
the stop), W2 (inside the platform, before its result commits) or a CPU-only tail of up to about
3.9 s. R577's reproduction makes a false kill need both mutated runs in the window. No client-side
signal can see these windows (`/coord/handoff/R-204/r204-trace.md`).

**What this item holds.**
1. **Part B, a LethAL Control change** (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-R-204b-stop-path-outcomes.md`):
   after a stop, read the platform's committed `Test Method Line` result under ReadCommitted; a
   committed pass for the stopped method refuses the `timeout` (scores `error`). It closes W1; it
   cannot see W2. Needs a control-app bump, so after the release.
2. **The measurement that sizes the residual,** on bc1-bc3 with an al-probe under a lease, no control
   change needed: how long BC takes to cancel CPU-only AL work after `StopSession` (E8 measured about
   3.9 s on Cronus283, which is parked), and the run-to-run spread of a slow test near its budget,
   which decides how often two mutated runs both land in the window.
