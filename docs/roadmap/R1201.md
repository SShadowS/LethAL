**Found by** PR-302's BC check (M13065 is killed under v3 and passes on BC) and measured by PR-396 (lethal-code, 2026-11-23).
Evidence: spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-pr396-v3-stale-buffer-guard.md`, `/coord/handoff/PR-396/step1-result.md`.

- **What breaks:** al-runner raises "cannot be saved because some information on the page is not up-to-date" where BC
  does not (upstream #8721; the fix is upstream #8801). A mutant's test that fails on that text is a false kill.
- **The guard (PR-396):** `staleBufferGuard` in `al-runner-v3-backend.ts` turns such a mutant row into a non-kill
  `runner-test-error` with `V3_STALE_BUFFER_NOTE`. Baseline rows are left as they are. Across t1b, c3, c4 and y3 the text
  is on two kills only: M13065 (a false kill on BC) and M13625 (it survives on BC, step 1). The PR-340 carry walk
  applies the guard too, so such a row fails the walk. The resume fingerprint does not change, so `--resume` of a store
  written before PR-396 keeps those two kills.
- **Remove the guard when** a gated `current` carries #8801 and M13065 passes under v3. Then delete `staleBufferGuard`,
  its two exports and the PR-396 tests, and close this item.
- **Related:** PR-302, PR-396, upstream #8721 and #8801.
