**Filed 2026-10-23 by coord task R-693** (lethal-code). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/R-693/measure.md`.

**What broke, master-wide since 2026-10-23.** The contract fact `timeout-is-cpu` ran a long burn alone and a
short burn (55M steps) pinned to core 0 beside one busy loop, and needed the short one to PASS at a 2 s limit.
Its counts were fixed on 2026-10-21, when 55M steps cost 1.5 s of CPU. They now cost 1.6-3.8 s, so on a loaded
core the short burn crosses the limit and is stopped. v3 itself did not change: its limit is CPU time on alpha.2
and edge alike. A contract fact that diverges refuses the build, so every v3 leg C and the daily update gate read
`gate-fail` until this landed.

**Fix (r14b).** No sizing, because CPU speed here moves too much to predict (a first design that calibrated
the burn on three pinned runs also flipped, in measured runs). The probe runs ONE huge burn (2,000,000,000 steps,
which never ends in any budget here) at a 2 s limit, twice, both pinned to core 0: call A alone must be stopped
("Test exceeded 2s timeout."); call B beside seven busy loops must ALSO be stopped, and its durationMs must be over
3000 ms (1.5 times the limit) AND over twice A's durationMs (a wall-time limit with slack stops both calls
together). The burn gets about 1/8 of the core in B, so a CPU-time stop comes at several
times the limit in wall time, while a wall-time stop would come at about 2000 ms. Each call has a 60 s process
timeout. A killed A reads `diverged` (no limit stopped it); a killed B reads `unmeasurable`. A missing row is
`unmeasurable`; B not stopped, stopped at 3000 ms or less, or stopped at no more than twice A's durationMs, is `diverged`. See `docs/gates/alrunner-v3.md`
(history, 2026-10-23, r14b).

**Closing.** Done in the commit above: the new `timeout-is-cpu` block in `al-runner-v3-contract.ts`, with unit
tests per direction and a red-check log. Three back-to-back leg-C runs on the pinned build read
`timeout-is-cpu = matches` (A about 2.0 s, B about 16 s). The live proof for the gate is two full
`itest:alrunner-v3` runs on the final head and leg C on edge.
