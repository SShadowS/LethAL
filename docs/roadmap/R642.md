**Filed 2026-10-22 by lethal-preproc for coord task R-642** (orchestrator, after the owner raised the kraken container
from 24 GB to 32 GB, memory.max 34359738368).

**What.** The runbook now allows THREE heavy jobs container-wide, of which at most TWO may be BaseApp (BC.History)
corpus jobs. A third BaseApp job OOM-killed the old 24 GB container, and 32 GB adds room for a third heavy job of
another kind, but not a third BaseApp one with margin. `scripts/corpus-snapshot.ts`'s guard still enforces the old
rule:
- two flock slots (`/coord/locks/heavy.1`, `heavy.2`);
- a `ps` scan that refuses when the other held slots plus the heavy jobs that take no slot reach 2;
- messages that say "at most two heavy jobs container-wide".

So a third corpus job is refused although the container now has room for it.

**To close.**
- Three slots, plus a separate BaseApp cap of two. A BaseApp dump counts against both caps.
- The refusal messages name which cap refused.
- The 10 GB free-memory back-off is kept.
- Red-checked in BOTH directions, because it relaxes a guard: a third non-BaseApp heavy job is admitted; a third
  BaseApp job is refused; a fourth heavy job of any kind is refused.
- The v3 update script keeps its own copy of the limit, which lethal-bugs changes in R-639 (`heavy >= 3` skips).
