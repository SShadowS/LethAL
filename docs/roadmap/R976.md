Found by lane-code, 2026-11-13 (coord R-965, the R975 build). Related: [[R975]], [[R029]], [[R926]].

R975 decides a trigger mutant from per-test LINE coverage using the manifest's `reachLine`. That field is set only for a statement-grain
mutant whose statement is not a nested block. A mutant that replaces a whole trigger body (`lethal.empty-block`) has no `reachLine`, so
FALLBACK 1 still gives it every test that touched the object.

Measured twice. Invented app (`/coord/handoff/R-965/proof.md`): the `empty-block` of an `OnAction` no test invokes is still `survived`
(attribution `object`) after R975, while its statements are `no-coverage`. Continia DO (`/coord/handoff/R-965/blast.md`): of 204 survivors,
158 are decided by the rule (109 become no-coverage) and 46 have no `reachLine` and stay survived.

Fix direction: place a block-grain mutant by the line of the first statement of its body in the same frame (`reachLinesOf`), or by the
trigger's line span, and decide it as R975 does (any hit inside the span covers it). Needs a pre-commitment for the moved figures.
