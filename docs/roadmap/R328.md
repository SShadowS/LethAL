**Filed by RUST-03's S4.2b gate (2026-09-29).** Evidence: AMENDMENT 1 (lead F, the S4.2b gate) and
AMENDMENT 10 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-rust-03-precommitment.md`.

**What was measured.** Lead F is JSC heap capacity minus heap size, read after a forced full GC at
the p5 marker of a marked W8 run (W8 is the whole-BaseApp spec-level identity capture; p5 is the
manifest-row loop in `writeInstrumentedProject`). RUST-03 pre-committed F at or below 2,000 MB once
its spec-memory fixes landed. On the final tree (`5e8a537d`): heap capacity 5,267 MB, heap size
3,037 MB, so F = 2,230 MB, MISSED. The other p5 figures on that run: phase peak 10,663 MB, post-GC
RSS 6,454 MB (so the p5 transient, lead E, is 4,209 MB), external memory 1,658 MB, 25,518,099
objects; whole-run peak 10,664 MB.

**History.** F was 3,597 MB at S0 (AMENDMENT 1), fell to 1,728 MB on S4.2c's marked run, and read
2,157 and 2,205 MB on S4.2d's marked runs. The reading is noisy between sessions; the miss stands on
the single pre-committed run.

**Why it matters, and why it is not urgent.** F is memory the process holds but is not using. W8 is
under its 16,384 MB ceiling (median 11,798 MB), so this is a RAM item, not a crash risk.

**What would close it.** An attribution of what sets JSC's retained capacity here (churn in spec
generation and p5; the live-set size, see [[R326]]), and either a fix with its own prediction, or a
ruling that F is JSC's normal headroom for this live set and not worth chasing.
