**Filed 2026-10-08 by the [[R500]] build** (plan r3 `/coord/reviews/R-500-plan/plan.r3.md`, shape 3, exclusion (b)).
A plausible hang that is NOT refused.

**What.** R500 shape 3 protects only a filter call on the item's own record made in its OnPreDataItem. A
self-inserting item whose bound is set elsewhere, in a procedure OnPreDataItem calls, in OnPreReport, or as a
`DataItemTableView`, keeps that bound unprotected: a mutant that removes or widens it is emitted. The item's other
mutants (a guard before the insert, the key value written) are not refused either.

**To close.** Count self-inserting items whose bound lives outside OnPreDataItem on every corpus, read them, and
refuse or close it with a ruling.

## Done

Measured by R-522 (`/coord/handoff/R-522/measure.md`, section R530): 43 self-inserting items (BaseApp 42, Quality
Management 1); 21 have an OnPreDataItem filter (protected by shape 3), 22 do not. Two mechanisms are real hazards:

- **Custom Report Layout (Copy Company).** OnAfterGetRecord writes `Code` and `Company Name`, then `Insert(true)`; the
  table's OnInsert regenerates the primary key higher and the walk is linked on `Company Name`. A `remove-assignment`
  on the Company Name write inserts rows inside the link, after the cursor: an endless loop.
- **Copy Cost Budget and Copy Production Forecast.** The new rows sort after the cursor inside the walked range, and
  only the guard (`Break` when `Entry No.` is above `LastEntryNo`) stops the walk.

**The rule (W+), `inSelfInsertItem` in `loop-hazard.ts`, asked at the dispatch check for every operator.** A
self-inserting report data item (`selfInserts`; not an `Integer` or `Date` item) refuses: (a) every site in its own
triggers and the same-object procedures they reach (`itemScopes`); (b) the writers, outside that scope and anywhere in
the report object, of any name an exit guard in the scope reads: an assignment to it, or ANY call that passes the name
as an argument (fail closed: a callee that is not in the project, such as `GetLastEntry(LastEntryNo, ...)`, is still
refused); (c) an outside filter call on such an item (`altersOpenItemFilter` now treats a self-inserting item like an
open one). The exit set is shared with `isEarlyExit` (`endsScope`).

**Side effect.** `itemScopes` follows every call shape `openReachable` follows (`CurrReport.P()` and a bare name in an
expression, not only `P()`). That also widens `selfInserts`' own insert detection, so shape 3 (`altersSelfInsertFilter`)
may refuse a few more OnPreDataItem filters than before.

**Tests** (`packages/builtin-tier1/tests/r500-shapes.test.ts`, describe "R530: sites in a self-inserting item, its
feeds and outside filters"): refused T1 to T6, still emitted E1 to E5. Red sets, each by reverting one clause:
scope clause: T1, T2, T3; hiddenCallee closure (back to `bareCallee`): T3; `selfInserts` gate dropped: E1, E2, E3;
feed by assignment: T4; feed by call: T5; feed names widened to every name: E4; M6: T6. Logs:
`/coord/handoff/R-522/red/`.

**Accepted residuals.** A reportextension `modify()` trigger that makes a base item self-insert; a `with` receiver;
an insert made in another object ([[R529]], closed by ruling). **Cost:** at measurement W was 3,203 sites on BaseApp
(about 0.43% of the specs), the feeds about 51 more and M6 about 0: about 0.44%. **Unmeasured on the final code.**
Most of W lies in the Date Compress family (2,831 sites), whose other sites shape 3 did not cover. No fixture is
added (R164): a hang-capable site stays out of every gate but `itest:hang`; the refusals are proven offline only,
and the new predicate refuses 0 sites over all `fixtures/*` (`/coord/handoff/R-522/precommit.md`).
