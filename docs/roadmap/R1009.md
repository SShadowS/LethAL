Found by coord task R-998 (2026-11-14), from R-984's DO window t1 (`/coord/handoff/R-984/result.md`, `t1.run.stderr.txt`).
Related: [[R945]], [[R1000]], [[R1004]], [[R964]].

**What.** In t1, 7 of the 35 daemon closes in mutant requests were on a refusal that starts `a stale Issued Reminder
Header buffer from its own write, after a lock other tha`. That is as far as LethAL's close summary prints it. No store
holds the full text: the rows of a request whose daemon closed are re-sent and never stored. al-runner's own binary holds
only pieces of the template. The BC.History shapes of this family (`after a rollback other than...`, `after a failed
guarded Codeunit.Run...`) are #7964's and are different texts.

**Why it matters.** R-998 keeps three refusal families on the serve daemon after measuring each clean. This one cannot
join without its full text and a trigger to measure, so DO keeps paying a warm-up for each of these closes (t1: about
100 s each).

**To do.**
- Make the close reason carry the whole refusal text: in the stats summary, or as a progress event per close, so a
  window's log has it.
- Then reduce the shape to an invented repro, measure it with R-967's kit, and either keep it or file it upstream.
