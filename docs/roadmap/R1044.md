**Why this family.** Under R1036's count over every store (`causes.txt`), the open issues ahead of #7924 have a family already
(#7775 platform tables, #7854 permission, #8013, #8154, #8002 number sequences, #8028 no-client, #7837 table connection), are
unrelated causes (#7819, #8036), or need TestPage (#7850, R69). #7924's 18 ledger keys are one path: a .NET exception raised inside
AL, refused as "the text AL would see is not pinned". They stand 265 times.

**What was built.**
- **The family:** `scripts/refusal-family.ts` family `dotnet-exception` (target OnPrem), 31 cells:
  - 14 sites with invented arguments: 12 .NET members the ledger names (UriBuilder constructor/Port/Scheme, ZipArchive,
    XmlTextWriter.WriteEndDocument, DataRowCollection/DataColumnCollection/DataRow.Item, JObject.Add, X509Certificate2,
    RSACryptoServiceProvider.Decrypt, an Aes decryptor's TransformFinalBlock), CultureInfo (its key's CultureNotFoundException
    was not reproduced: the name chosen raises nothing, on al-runner or on BC), and Uri (no #7924 key: a member al-runner pins, as
    a comparison);
  - not covered: the OpenSSL form of the RSA keys (`Interop+Crypto+OpenSslCryptographicException`, RSAWrapper.Decrypt 43 and
    RSACryptoServiceProvider.Decrypt 38 sightings). The Decrypt cell raised the managed CryptographicException, so BC's text
    for the OpenSSL form is not measured;
  - each in two shapes: `guarded` (asserterror alone: the outcome cannot depend on the text) and `text` (GetLastErrorText carried
    out through Error, so BC's run records BC's text);
  - 3 controls with arguments that raise nothing.
  - Left out: the BC-internal QR-code and PDF converters (an environment's TypeInitializationException) and XmlDocument.Validate.
- **classOf:** one class for every "DotNet <member> raised the .NET exception <type>: ..." text, with or without its tail.
