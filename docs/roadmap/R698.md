**Filed 2026-10-23 by lethal-code during R-685**, from its deep plan review (`/coord/handoff/R-685/review-plan.md`,
B2). A false "old" verdict: the unsafe direction.

**What.** The parser reads `Codeunit::My.Ns.CU` as a member chain on `Codeunit::My`. In
`packages/runner/src/testpage-scan.ts`, `argInfo` records it as a member on a value, so `argHolds` returns undefined
(no fallback), and `idRefsIn` keeps only `My` (no fold). The quoted form `Codeunit::"CU"` folds or falls back
correctly.

**Measured offline** (probe `/coord/handoff/R-685/probe/p2.ts`, master c7686f12; invented objects): stored
(P4) and handed to an external call (P5), an edit to the qualified codeunit moves no digest and neither takes the
fallback.

**Who hits it.** The shape is real in BC.History (its TestFramework). No instance was found inside a test app of
BC.History, CDO, DC or DO (every grep hit there was a quoted name containing a dot).

**To close.** In `argInfo` and `idRefsIn`, when an object reference is the receiver of a member chain, take the
whole chain as one reference, `<kind>::<qualified name>`; `foldIdTargets` resolves a codeunit by `unitsNamed` (the
last segment). Red tests for the stored and the handed-out shapes.
