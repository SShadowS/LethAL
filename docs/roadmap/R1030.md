**Why.** PR-18's trace of DO: each mutant entry runs its whole covering codeunit, about 47 tests per mutant. On R-984 t3, 13,889 of
the 27,537 tests those entries ran (50%) came at or before the mutant's last covering test, with 28% of their baseline own time.

**What.** `mutantExcludes` (`packages/runner/src/al-runner-v3-backend.ts`) adds to a mutant entry's `excludeTests` every test
after the mutant's last covering test in the codeunit's baseline order, on top of R998's run. The covering sets come from the
prefetch plans (`coveringOf`). Not cut: NONE groups (warm-ups, kill-pair confirms), tests run alone, one-shot calls, a codeunit
whose order is not fully known. Guards: #8059 (a tail test whose name starts a test that must run stays in), an order check (a cut
entry whose rows come back out of baseline order is `missing`), and widening (a ref the cut left out runs live, never `missing`).
The serve-entries stats line gives, once per (mutant, codeunit): `mutant entries N of W test(s), cut after the last covering test
C, tests left out X, widened, order broken`.

**Verified offline.** Six tests, each red-checked (the cut, a kill by the last covering test, #8059, `suiteMissing` both ways, the
order check, widening), plus the once-per-entry count. Opus review: nothing blocking; its fixes are in (the denominator, widening
scope).

**To close.** The DO window with L1 (lethal-bugs runs it): 0 verdict or killing-test differences against t1, t2b and t3 (a BLOCK
otherwise); at least 45% of whole-codeunit tests left out; `order broken` and `widened` 0; prefetch plus live wall at most 10.2 s
per scored covered mutant.
