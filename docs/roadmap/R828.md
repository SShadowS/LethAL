[[R826]], GitHub #28.

**What.** A mutant on a statement's condition resolves to the whole statement, so its root holds the body. Everything nested in that body joins the
chain, and each branch repeats the root's text: the same fold as [[R827]]. This is not only `if`: loops and `case` arms do it too.

**Measured across DC and DO, R827's addendum 5 (R-836, 2026-11-06).**

|---|---|---|

| Files with a chain of 100 or more | 65 | 24 |
| Worst file, `CDOEMailTemplateImportExport`, 944 guards | 46.1 MB | 44.3 MB |

That file's largest chain is a `for` statement with 454 members, which the outermost-loop rule keeps. The typical file shrinks about as measured; the
worst hardly at all.

**So this item's first premise was wrong.** It said "chains of up to 66" and "only if a measurement says it still matters". Those figures held only for 8
DO codeunits of 1,065 to 1,602 lines (`/coord/handoff/R-825/r827-impact.md`: chains of 26 to 66, 146 to 916 KB). Across the corpora it matters.

that crash is still unmeasured: [[R826]].

**O1 built 2026-11-06 on `lethal/r828` (not merged).** A statement with its own mutants copies itself plain once per own mutant; mutants nested in it
are their own components in the one original branch, as zero-width opener and closer insertions (no overlap). A loop, or a root holding a directive, keeps
its flat chain; a mutant in a loop inside a plain root joins the outermost loop within that root. Real emitted size over the 38 files: 165,683 KB to
108,930 KB (model: 108,934 KB, worst file error 0.18%); worst file 43,360 KB, longest chain still 461 (a `for`). Reach grain: 0 moves over 825 fixture
mutants and 259,856 corpus mutants (a synthetic `asserterror` probe does move one `unplaced` to `statement`). Figures: `/coord/handoff/R-828/o1/`.

**O1 build-review fixes (R-851).** In `planFile` the closers now go first in the edit map, inner first, ahead of every opener and chain (a root ending in `;` ends where the next statement starts, and the closer had printed after it: wrong verdict, or `rewrite.overlap`); no tie needs an opener first. A chain or closer whose `end` would touch the source's next character gets a space (`Foo(C + 1)end` was `endend`, AL0111), never a newline. Tests: the GLUED class in `r827-residual.test.ts` and three in `compile.test.ts`.
**Open, on master too:** a mutation text glued to the source after it (`Foo(C + 1)else` with `begin end` for the call) still emits `endelse`; the corpora hold 0 glued statements after a root ending in `;` (parse count, `/coord/handoff/R-828/o1/glued-count.txt`).

**The order.**

1. **O1 alone, after R-836 lands.** No per-pass cost. It does NOT meet this item's headline: the worst file stays near 43 MB.
2. **A BC measurement, on the orchestrator's word and the owner's direct approval, under a lease, with its limit pre-committed on master first.** The first pre-commitment was WITHDRAWN by its review (addendum 1 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-r828-loop-compare-measurement-precommitment.md`): raising the budget floors leaves a false kill and breaks the group path above about F = 1.5. A new one replaces it: no constant moves, and `reproduceKill` re-runs a timeout kill at a larger budget.
3. **O3, only if inside that new limit.** Boolean locals, one read per mutant: a new selector call needs a control bump, and `Active` is not a pure read. O2 alone and the temp-Boolean O4 are out.

**Why O3 waits: it can give a wrong verdict.** A plain compare per pass is not free: [[R246]] measured one latch compare at +63% on a tight loop, and O3 adds one per mutant per
pass. The baseline pays it too, but a mutant that loops far more than baseline could pass its budget, be stopped, and have its confirm pass: a false `timeout-killed`, which is a
BLOCK. No gate holds that race today, because `itest:hang`'s terminating mutants run under 150 ms.

The lead came from lethal-preproc: the worst file's largest chain is a `for` with 0 members of its own, which exists only through R827's outermost-loop rule (R246).
Proof for O1: `r827-residual.test.ts`, per mutant. It cannot prove O3 as it stands: the plan names three holes.

Today's chains give no wrong verdict: this is emitted size, which ties to [[R826]].
