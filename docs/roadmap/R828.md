**What.** A mutant on a statement's condition resolves to the whole statement, so its root holds the body. Everything nested in that body joins the
chain, and each branch repeats the root's text: the same fold as [[R827]]. This is not only `if`: loops and `case` arms do it too.

**Measured across DC and DO, R827's addendum 5 (R-836, 2026-11-06).**

| | master | with R827 |
|---|---|---|
| Longest chain | 496 | 461 |
| Files with a chain of 100 or more | 65 | 24 |
| Worst file, `CDOEMailTemplateImportExport`, 944 guards | 46.1 MB | 44.3 MB |

That file's largest chain is a `for` statement with 454 members, which the outermost-loop rule keeps. The typical file shrinks about as measured; the
worst hardly at all.

**So this item's first premise was wrong.** It said "chains of up to 66" and "only if a measurement says it still matters". Those figures held only for 8
DO codeunits of 1,065 to 1,602 lines (`/coord/handoff/R-825/r827-impact.md`: chains of 26 to 66, 146 to 916 KB). Across the corpora it matters.

**#28.** R827 does not by itself remove the suspect in #28 for such a file. No "fixed" wording on #28 until this item is decided. Whether chain length caused
that crash is still unmeasured: [[R826]].
