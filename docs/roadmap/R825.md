**Filed 2026-11-05 from GitHub #44.** Related: [[R824]] (same commit).

**What was wrong.** The README said "Fifteen operators, in two tiers" and, under Limits, "Twelve operators today, in two
groups: six generic ... six AL-specific". The registry holds 26 (16 in Tier 1, 10 in Tier 2). The tables were already generated; the prose was not.

**Fixed.** `scripts/operator-tables.ts` has two inline markers, `summary` and `limits`, whose text it writes from the
registry's counts. `scripts/operator-tables.test.ts` fails on drift, as it does for the tables. The Limits sentence no
longer carries hand-written per-tier counts; its examples of what each tier covers were checked against the registry and still hold.
