**What.** `ExplainGap` names the gap, its survivors and its counts, but suggests nothing about what
kind of fix would close it (a missing assertion, a missing test, an unreachable branch). `explain.ts`
carries a deliberate rule against passing judgement on the target's code: the ranking that orders
survivors by evidence exists precisely so the tool states what was measured, not what to do about it.
A per-gap fix-kind suggestion runs straight into that rule and needs a ruling before anyone writes
one.

**Who hits it.** Anyone using `explain` to triage gaps hoping for a "write an assertion here" or
"add a test here" pointer: they get evidence, not a suggestion.

**Evidence.** `explain.ts`'s admissibility comment.

**Why left open here.** Needs an owner ruling on whether a suggested fix kind can coexist with the
file's "never says what test to write" rule, or requires a distinct, clearly-labelled output the
rule does not cover. Out of scope for C02-09.

**Found by.** C02-09's own review of GitHub issue #11 (point 4), filed as a follow-up rather than
folded into this task's scope.

**Checked 2026-10-06 (R-277b): still blocked on the owner ruling, no code.** The rule is enforced,
not just stated: `explain.test.ts` ("no string the projection SHIPS tells a reader what test to
write") bans phrases such as "write a test" and "add an assertion" from every shipped string, and
the admissibility test allows only report, registry and pinned strings. A fix kind would have to be
either an exception to that test or a separate, clearly-labelled output it does not cover. That is
the ruling this item asks for; nothing to build before it.

**Owner ruling, 2026-10-06: a separate, clearly labelled, opt-in output.** The existing gap evidence
keeps the "never says what test to write" rule unchanged, and its banned-phrase and admissibility
tests stay as they are. A suggested fix kind per gap (for example a missing assertion, a missing
test, or an unreachable branch) may be added only as a distinct section that is off by default
(behind a flag), labelled as a suggestion rather than a measurement, and kept out of the strings
those tests police. The item is now buildable on that basis.

**Done 2026-10-06 (R-273b).** `lethal explain --suggest` adds a separate `suggestions` section
(`packages/runner/src/explain-suggest.ts`), composed by the CLI only under the flag; `explain()` and
`ExplainOutput` are unchanged, so the default output and its policed tests are untouched by
construction. Each survivor's kind comes only from its measured reach and coverage attribution
(`survivorEvidenceOf`, shared with explain's survivors): `check-the-result`, `cover-the-branch`,
`cover-the-statement`, `undecided`, `reader-marked`; a gap is its members' kind or `mixed`. A report
without gap ids refuses by name. Explain schema stays 13 (optional root `suggestions`). Pinned in
`packages/runner/tests/explain-suggest.test.ts` and `schemas.test.ts`; 13 of 14 red-checks went red,
and the 14th (a batch-index join condition) was removed as unreachable (`tallyGaps` refuses a gap
spanning two batches). Plan and reviews: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-R-273b-explain-suggest.md`.
