**Found by** coord PR-349 (lethal-code, 2026-11-22), re-measuring R1099 (closed), from PR-285's and PR-320's measurements
(lethal-preproc, for alrunner's PERF-01 and PERF-34).

**What it removes:** the company login al-runner raises at every test codeunit session's company open. On current builds
that cost is inside `testsMs`, not outside it (R1099).
- **PR-285's profile:** the company open is 40.9% of the time with the replay off, and 12.2% with it on. Summed test time
  is 40 s on, against main's 72 s.
- **PR-320, four interleaved rounds on DO's 30-request sequence:**
  - instructions -16.5% against main;
  - task-clock -15.1%;
  - client wall -20.0%.
  - The rows are identical in all 20 runs, and the check census compared 110 replays with 0 differences.

**What it would be worth on a full DO run:** about a fifth of the mutant phase, if PR-320's -20% carries over. On c2 the
summed tests time was 48,895 s of 56,248 s of serve wall (`/coord/handoff/PR-349/floor.txt`). Not measured on a full run.

**The owner:** upstream al-runner (PERF-01 step 3, then PERF-34's row-digest key). LethAL's part, once a gated current
carries it:
- set `ALRUNNER_LOGIN_REPLAY=1` on mutant-run daemons, as PR-340 sets `AL_STRICT_SEQUENTIAL`;
- or nothing, if upstream makes it the default;
- then a pre-committed full-DO A/B, replay on vs off, with verdicts and `killingTest` equal per mutant as the hard claim.

**Correction (coord PR-420, 2026-11-24):** current does NOT carry the replay.
- An earlier claim said "the replay is on upstream main and edge". It reached PR-402's spec and the lanes' messages, and it
  was wrong.
- Measured by `strings` on the binaries:
  - `current` (edge e5176a9fd) and upstream main 88ea54c67 carry only `ALRUNNER_LOGIN_REPLAY_CHECK`. That is the
    measure-only check: upstream's `login_replay.rs` at e5176a9fd says "measures only, replays nothing".
  - There is no `ALRUNNER_LOGIN_REPLAY` switch and no `login start: … replayed` counts line.
  - The switch exists only in the perf builds (PR-320's 5aeaec420 has both), on the PERF-34 line (draft PR #8688, open).
- So the trigger above has not fired. PR-402's flag stays refused, and PR-420 (step 2, the leg-C fact) is parked until it
  does.
- **The fact's design is ready** (`/coord/handoff/PR-420/probe/`):
  - upstream's own login stand-in, with LethAL's `active-mutant.txt` read on the login path;
  - the orders NONE, M1, NONE and M1, M2, NONE;
  - on current, an `=1` + `_CHECK=1` daemon and an `=0` daemon gave identical rows (M1 and M2 kill, NONE passes);
  - the check printed "1 compared, 0 differ" and "3 compared, 0 differ". The replay key includes the entry's resource texts,
    so each mutant has its own key.
