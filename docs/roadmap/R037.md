**Coverage subsets are narrower than the gate's confidence implies.** During R32, seven of the ten survivors had been run against exactly one test, two against seven, one against two. Broadening to all 19 changed no verdict, so the selection was correct here — but the margin is thin, and the pre-`0a463fd` failure looked identical from the outside (one test, wrong one). Worth a periodic all-tests sweep of the survivors as a standing check, since a survivor is exactly where a too-narrow subset hides.
