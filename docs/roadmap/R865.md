**What.** The hang rules look at loops. `if Level < 10 then Self(Level + 1)`, with the guard forced true or the depth argument blanked or swapped, recurses without bound, and nothing refuses it. Found by the opus plan review of R-862, in the code, on the prototype. Parse counts of self-calling arms for `condition-true`: DO 2, DC 4.

**Shipped too.** The review found one such shape under the shipped `swap-additive`. Not counted across operators.

**Why it matters.** R826 records a stack overflow killing the BC service tier. How AL recursion ends on BC is not measured. So this may be a loud `error`, or a downed server: only a probe says.

**To close.** The owner chose the NARROW rule of coord R-871 (branch `lethal/r871`), not a refusal of recursion in every operator: `recursionDepthHangs` refuses `swap-additive` and `delete-additive-*` on the depth argument of a direct self-call (`Name(...)` or `this.Name(...)`) whose parameter a guard reads. Counted by parse on 2.71 million specs, the wider wording would have refused 2,866. R-871 names what its rule does not refuse: a value that stops shrinking through an assignment, a guard that calls again in the same state, mutual recursion, depth counted in globals, recursion through another object, a procedure split by `#if`, a procedure written twice across `#if/#else`, and counters that guard only against cycles valid data cannot have. Evidence: `/coord/handoff/R-871/findings.md`. The opt-in operators of R868 refuse their own self-call sites separately. R858 is the same family, for loops.

**The probe was dropped, 2026-11-08, by the owner's ruling,** before any run: `2026-11-08-r865-recursion-probe-precommitment.md`. So R-871's rule does not wait for an answer, and what unbounded AL recursion does on BC stays unmeasured.
