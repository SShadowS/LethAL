Found by coord task R-972 (bugs), from lethal-code's R-942 serve window 1 (DO stopped by its watchdog at a 17 GB tree, 16 min into the
daemon baseline, 0 of 1,979 rows kept). Related: [[R987]] (the daemon's prefetch, a different holder), [[R970]] (one-shot processes in
parallel, a different case), [[R948]], [[R955]].

**Measured** (`/coord/handoff/R-972/measure-do.sh`, `do-base.procs.tsv`: every process of the run's tree every 2 s; master 4fcfaa92,
edge-736fc7677, R-942's DO Cloud x DO Test inputs and `v3.do-serve.json`, 43,583 deployed, alone under `--peak 20`). Peaks over the
30 min run: LethAL's own `bun cli.ts run` process **20.6 GB**; the serve daemon 4.5 GB; one-shot cross-check processes 3.5 GB; the
tree 24.5 GB, when the script's own safety stop (24 GiB) sent SIGTERM at 03:05:21Z. LethAL's process does not grow steadily: it
spikes about once every 2-3 minutes and falls back, and both grow over the run: spikes 5.8, 8.5, 9.1, 9.4, 12.3, 13, 15, 19, 20.6 GB;
the level between them 1.7 GB at the start, about 5 GB at the end.

**Why (measured, coord task R-974, `/coord/handoff/R-974/dbg.*`).** Every daemon answer's size logged with LethAL's RSS at the moment
it was read: 69 answers, 67 to 72 MB each, flat over the run (so the daemon does not accumulate coverage: nothing to file upstream),
while LethAL's RSS rose from 8 to 20.2 GiB. Each answer's coverage lists every statement of the called codeunit's reach, nearly all
unhit, and the baseline fills kept each codeunit's whole answer until the baseline ended (`answers` / `shots` in `fillDaemonBaseline`
and the isolated fill), as did a one-shot baseline call (`lane.rowsByCodeunit`, until the first activation).

**Built.** `keptCoverage` (`al-runner-v3-sleep.ts`): a coverage document kept past its request holds every file entry (the join and
label checks read the file list, unhit files included), every hit statement, and in the top-level coverage also the statements on
Sleep-indexed lines (§6 case 2 asks whether such a line is listed, hit or not); every reader of a kept answer reads the same thing
(attribution and the cross-check read hits only; listed per a build review that walked every reader). Applied at every store of both
fills and at the one-shot call's store. `LineReader` now scans each chunk once and joins a line's pieces when it ends (appending every
chunk to one string and searching it again flattened a 67 MB line per chunk). Tests: the Sleep readers agree on a kept and a full
document (a listed unhit Sleep line, an unlisted one, a hit owned and unowned), file entries and Sleep lines kept, the daemon fill and
the one-shot call hand on coverage with no unhit statement and identical verdicts, the line reader over split chunks and multi-byte
characters; each red-checked. Not changed: R866's isolated one-shot fill (daemon baseline off) still keeps raw coverage text per test.

**Measured on the build** (`/coord/handoff/R-974/fix12.*`, the same DO run on 7fb1cc07, edge-736fc7677, admitted at `--peak 12`):
the daemon baseline ran to its end (5,280 s, 1,603 of 1,979 green) and into the mutant phase (27,392 of 43,583 mutants, then stopped
by hand to free the slot); LethAL's own process peaked at 3.9 GiB (unfixed: 20.2 GiB at the 24 GiB stop, 30 min in, baseline not done),
the whole tree at 9.55 GiB. Baseline rows against R-942's one-shot window 1d (`dod.w1`, isolation `test`): 1,956 of 1,979 identical;
the other 23 are all `CDO Mail Msg Builder Tests`, error there and pass here: window 1d ran on edge 2bd87774f, which refused them
(`DotNet new Newtonsoft.Json.JsonTextWriter: assembly 'Newtonsoft.Json' could not be loaded`), and 736fc7677 runs them. No row moved
for any other reason.
