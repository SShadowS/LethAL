Found by the opus plan review and build review of the operator batch (coord R-855). Read, not measured on a corpus. Related: [[R837]], [[R829]], [[R830]].

`delete-logical-*` and `delete-additive-*` refuse a site that controls a call the loop's progress needs (`deletedOperandHangs` in `loop-hazard.ts`). Operators that shipped before them do not ask that question, so these claim the same sites today:

- `repeat if A or B then Advance(); until Done`: `negate-conditional` on the condition.
- `if A and B then continue; Advance();`: `negate-conditional`, which turns a rare skip into a common one.
- `case I + J of 1: Advance(); end;` and `for K := 1 to I + J do Advance();`: `swap-additive` on the selector or the bound.
- `AddTo(Pos, I + 1)`: `swap-additive` on the step.

A hang in a run ends through the timeout and quarantine path, so this is cost and a stranded run, not a wrong verdict. The site-level check is ready to reuse. The open question is how many sites it would refuse: a corpus count comes first, and each refused site loses a good mutant, because most of these loops still end.

Not covered by that check, for any operator: progress behind a callee ([[R837]]), and a loop that ends only through an `Error` inside a callee.

**Closed by ruling, 2026-11-07 (orchestrator).** A probe extended the check to both operators. It would refuse 678 specs: fixtures 0, CDO 38, DO 43, DC 98, BC.History 499 (`negate-conditional` 610, `swap-additive` 68), and 0 on `examples/`. A synthetic caught all 6 shapes. A sample of 30 read by parse: 1 hang, 2 maybe, 27 no hang. Every mechanical narrowing tried left 0 of the 678 and dropped the one hang; a step-argument-only rule refuses 2 sites. It would move ordinals (DC 14, BC.History 58), so it needs a bump, for what the sample shows is mostly loss.

**The limit.** These sites stay claimed. A hang here costs a timeout on BC 28 and BC 29 alike, since #49's fix landed. In the README's Limits. Evidence: `/coord/handoff/R858/plan.md`.
