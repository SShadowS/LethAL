Found by coord task R-940 (2026-11-12, BC.History batch 7 on al-runner v3 edge-2bd87774f; `/coord/handoff/R-940/password.run.*`).
Related: [[R047]] (the floor), [[R912]] (the time budget), [[R945]].

**What happened.** Password's 22 baseline tests all pass in 0.3 s together. Under some mutants of its strength checks,
`Password Dialog Test`'s and the strength tests' calls ran for minutes: one test, `TestVerifyStrongPasswordSequences`, took 1,823 s
over 49 mutant calls, many near 180 s. The serve daemon closed 18 times on `timeout` (and 19 times on a cascade refusal), 38 daemons
in all. The one `--time-budget 3600` window ended with 143 of 191 mutants decided; 48 were left out of the report (not survivors).
The loop decided 139 mutants an hour, where the batch's other modules decided 16,000 to 700,000.

**Why.** The per-mutant budget is `max(2 x the test's baseline, --mutant-timeout-ms)`, and the floor defaults to 180,000 ms (R047),
chosen for bcdev, where a call carries publish and session latency. On v3 there is no publish, and a call for a test whose baseline is
a few milliseconds still waits up to three minutes before it counts as a timeout.

**To find out, then build.** Measure on Password and on the gate fixtures what a v3-only floor (for example 30 s, or a multiple of the
test's v3 baseline with a smaller floor) changes: how many mutants move from timeout to a verdict or the reverse (a mutant that is
slow but would finish inside 180 s), and the loop time. A floor that changes a verdict the gates pin needs a pre-commitment. The
user can lower it today with `--mutant-timeout-ms`; the question is the v3 default.

**Measured (coord R-945, `/coord/handoff/R-945/study/README.md`).** 41,521 mutant test calls that finished, over 60 v3 stores of
R-925..R-940: mutant time / the same test's baseline has median 0.43, p99 6.34, max 44.5 (Cryptography Management 817 ms -> 36.4 s,
killed). Of `max(F, k x baseline + c)`, none of those calls is cut only from F = 60 s and k = 20 up; a smaller floor cuts real
finishes, as R91 argued. Password's "finishes" past 180 s were hangs: with a 600 s stop the same calls ended past 600 s and the session
never ended (`/coord/handoff/R-945/slow/`); why al-runner reports them `pass` at the stop is the next task.

**Fixed (095fc380; pre-commitment 07e8bbec, amendment c50e89c5).** On al-runner-v3 with no `--mutant-timeout-ms`, after each batch's
baseline the mutant stop is `max(60 s, 20 x the largest GREEN baseline test + 5 s)` (`v3MutantStopMs`), a running maximum over the
session's batches: every mutant budget uses it, and the serve daemon (one `--test-timeout` per daemon) is started with it; a daemon
under another stop is replaced. The baseline keeps its own stop. Named once on stderr (`[lethal] al-runner-v3 mutant stop ...`).
bcdev and an explicit `--mutant-timeout-ms` are unchanged. Red both ways (`/coord/handoff/R-945/redchecks.md`).
- **Password**, same build (edge-2bd87774f) and config as R-940: stop 60 s (largest green baseline 268 ms). All 191 mutants decided in
  the hour (R-940: 143, 48 left out); every mutant decided in both runs has the same verdict; 207 decided an hour (was 139). Still 50
  timeouts at 60 s each: Password's hangs (above) dominate its loop.
- **The full v3 gate** passed on 095fc380, every leg; no figure moved.
