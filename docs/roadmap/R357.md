`CampaignCompareResult` in `packages/runner/src/campaign-subcommands.ts`) but published no schema, so a
consumer cannot validate the document. Evidence: `schemas/` has no `campaign-compare-v1.schema.json`.

**To close.** Generate `schemas/campaign-compare-v1.schema.json` with `scripts/generate-schemas.ts`
(covered by `--check`), list it in `schemas/README.md`, and test in `schemas.test.ts` that real compare
output validates: a verified match, an UNVERIFIED stage with its required `statement`, and that a
coverage-mode mismatch is a refusal that prints no document.
