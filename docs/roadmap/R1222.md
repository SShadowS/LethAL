**Found by** coord PR-431 (lethal-preproc, 2026-11-25), on a branch that does not touch the engine.
- **What happens:** `packages/runner/tests/spec-after-retention.test.ts` fails in full VERIFY by timing out. The two tests
  are "retained arrays and functions per spec, measured on the heap" and "no after node owns an array, a function or a
  position object". Each is near or over 5 s.
- **Seen in four VERIFY logs on 2026-11-25** (`/tmp/lethal-verify-test-*.log`): 1795553281109, 1795586356648,
  1795602592631, 1795603168060. At least one is another lane's run from before PR-431's branch existed.
  - The times seen were 6.9 s, 8.7 s and 14.2 s, at io pressure "some" of 0.8 to 2.5. That is below R1157's threshold, so
    VERIFY kept the 5 s limit.
  - Alone, nice'd on CPUs 12-21, the file passes, but takes 7.3 to 8.6 s for its 2 tests.
- **Why it matters:** a full VERIFY reads FAIL for a reason unrelated to the diff. Each lane must then show by hand that the
  failure is this one.
- **Possible levers (not chosen here):** give these heap measurements their own timeout, or make them cheaper. R1157's
  pressure rule is the related, earlier fix.
- **Related:** R1157, R917.
