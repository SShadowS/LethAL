**Found by** coord PR-424 (lethal-preproc, 2026-11-25), revisiting R992's f with a week of `heavy-peaks.tsv`.

- **The gap:**
  - `heavy-slot.sh` appends one line per ADMITTED job to `heavy-peaks.tsv` (start, end, slot, class, declared, measured, rc,
    command).
  - `heavy-small-admits.tsv` logs the small-job admissions.
  - Nothing records how long a job waited in the queue, or each refusal's reason and readings: `memory.current`, the held
    declared sum and the counted sum.
  - The queue's "queued as ticket N" line goes only to the caller's own output.
- **Why it matters:** R992's question is how often a job waits while memory has room. That is what decides whether f, or a
  preset, should move. Today it can only be inferred.
- **A fix (small, no admission change):**
  - heavy-slot logs, per job, the time it was queued and the time it was admitted;
  - the guard logs each refusal: time, slot, class, peak, `memory.current`, the declared sum, the counted sum, and which
    check refused.
  - A week of it answers R992's question directly.
- **Related:** R992, R953, R972, R991, PR-179.

**Built (coord PR-425, lethal-preproc, 2026-11-25).**
- **The ledger:** `<lock dir>/heavy-events.tsv` (`EVENTS_LEDGER` in `heavy-slot-class.ts`), one line per event.
- **Columns:** UTC, event, who (`heavy.<n>` or `ticket <seq>`), class, declared peak, the job's pid (its `heavy-slot.sh`),
  used GiB, used + peak + reserve, the held slots' COUNTED peaks (PR-179's max(RSS, 0.8 x declared)), held + peak + reserve,
  and max GiB. A reading the event lacks is `-`.
- **Events:**
  - `queue`, from `heavy-slot.sh` when a `--wait` ticket is taken;
  - from the guard: `admit`, `small` (the R992 allowance), `refuse`, `never`, and `count-admit` / `count-refuse` (no cgroup
    limit).
- **Reading it:**
  - a job's wait is its `queue` line to its `admit` line, by the same pid;
  - R992's symptom is a `refuse` whose used + peak + reserve is at or under max: memory had room, and the declared sum said
    no.
- **Admission-neutral:** a failed write is reported on stderr and ignored. `pr425-heavy-events.test.ts` pins it: the same
  inputs give the same exit code with an unwritable ledger, both for an admission and for a refusal. Red-checked
  (`/coord/handoff/PR-425/redcheck.md`).
- **Next:** after a week of it, the serve and baseline preset question (R992) goes back to the owner with the wait figures.
