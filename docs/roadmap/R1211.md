**Found by** coord PR-424 (lethal-preproc, 2026-11-25), revisiting R992's f with a week of `heavy-peaks.tsv`.

- **The gap:**
  - `heavy-slot.sh` appends one line per ADMITTED job to `heavy-peaks.tsv` (start, end, slot, class, declared, measured, rc,
    command).
  - `heavy-small-admits.tsv` logs the small-job admissions.
  - Nothing records how long a job waited in the queue, or each refusal's reason and readings: `memory.current`, the held
    declared sum and the counted sum.
  - The queue's "queued as ticket N" line goes only to the caller's own output.
- **Why it matters:** R992's question is how often a job waits while memory has room. That is what decides whether f, or a
  preset, should move. Today it can only be inferred.
- **A fix (small, no admission change):**
  - heavy-slot logs, per job, the time it was queued and the time it was admitted;
  - the guard logs each refusal: time, slot, class, peak, `memory.current`, the declared sum, the counted sum, and which
    check refused.
  - A week of it answers R992's question directly.
