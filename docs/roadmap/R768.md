**Filed 2026-10-25 (coord task R-762, item B).** Pre-commitment:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-r762-b-retire-sleep-rule-precommitment.md`.

**Change.** The backend decides `sleepRuleRetired` once per `status()`: true only when `findV3Pin` (version line AND
binary sha256) records leg C `sleep-is-noop` = `gone` AND the session's own probe (fresh or R765-cached) also reads
it `gone` (`sleepRuleRetiredFor`, `al-runner-v3-pins.ts`). Then: no §6 on a covering pass under a mutant, no
`--coverage-json` on a mutant call, no coverage window and no second echo in serve-entries. Kept: the baseline
bracket's coverage, B2's virtual-clock guard, and the Sleep index (its sizes stay figures). Unpinned builds keep the rule.

**Gate.** Leg X (d) on a pinned-gone build: M0003 and M0004 survive (2 / 3 / 0), no raw-hit checks; X (e) and
(f1)-(f3) print "not applicable" and do not run. `GATE_WARNINGS` is unchanged (it is asserted per session that runs).

**Residual.** A covering pass is scored `survived` even when the mutant lengthens a Sleep that stays under the v3
stop; BC would wait it out and pass too. A Sleep that BC would time out at its own, longer, deadline is not measured.

Tests: "R-762 B" in `packages/runner/tests/al-runner-v3-backend.test.ts` and `al-runner-v3-entries.test.ts`.
