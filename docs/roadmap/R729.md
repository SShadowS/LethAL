**Closed 2026-10-24 (f75d3874, R-729).** Both shapes are fixed in the dispatch emit and plan that every operator's arm
goes through (`packages/schemata/src`), not per operator.

**Shape 1 (AL0111), root cause.** The original has `if <cond> then;` (an inner `if` whose `then` is empty) as the
whole body of an outer `if` or a `case` branch. Its one `;` is the empty body and also ends both statements. When a
mutant sits inside that inner `if`, its reach marker is placed by wrapping the inner statement in a slot as
`begin <marker> <S> end` (`branchText`, `packages/schemata/src/dispatch-emit.ts`). `S` includes the `;`, and the wrap
dropped it, so the next sibling (or the next case label) followed `end` with no `;`. Read on the emitted DC target at
`CDCLookupGLDocumentNo.Codeunit.al` line 129 (and 142): `begin <marker> if not (<cond>) then; end`, then the next
statement. Fix: when `S`'s own text ends in `;`, the wrap reproduces it after `end`, the same consumed-terminator rule
that `emitDispatch` and the root wrap already used.

**Shape 2 (AL0110), root cause.** The remove-call arm leaves an emptied `then` slot, and `emptiedSlotFiller`
(`packages/schemata/src/dispatch-plan.ts`) chooses `begin end` (not `;`) only when the next token is `else`. Its
look-ahead skipped white space only. The DC site has a `//` comment between the call and the `else`
(`CDCSalesRegister.Codeunit.al`, original lines 370 to 372), so the `else` was hidden, the filler was `;`, and alc read
`then ; // c` before `else` as an orphaned `else`. The same blind spot made a case arm with a comment before its `;`
emit a doubled `;`. Fix: the look-ahead (`skipTrivia`) steps over `//` and `/* */` comments and `#pragma`/`#region`
lines.

**Why the R402 sweep missed them.** `scripts/fixtures/r402` was built to find where a preprocessor directive can sit
in a statement (R402's subject), and every shape is a directive placement. None of its 42 shapes has an empty
statement (`then;`) as the sole body of an `if` or case branch, and none has a comment between a single-call `then`
body and its `else` (the c4 and c4b shapes have `#if` there, not a comment). R161's unit tests for the `else` filler
had no comment either. The sweep could not report a shape its list did not contain. It now has `e1`
(`if X then;` as an outer `if`'s body), `e2` (the same as a case branch) and `e3` (a call with a `//` and a `/* */`
comment before `else`): 45 shapes, 180 cases, 136 artifacts compiled by alc, 0 problems (shape counts corrected
by the R-755 audit from `git ls-tree` at f75d3874^ and f75d3874; they read 33 and 36). Each of e1, e2 and e3 failed
the artifact compile before the fix (AL0111, AL0111, AL0110) and passes after.

**Real-run behaviour today (master before this fix), measured by reading the runner.** A real `lethal run` over DC/Cloud
would NOT have refused the 30 mutants by name, and would not have aborted at once. It would have:
1. found alc rejecting the batch's artifact (`AlcCompileError`, the only error class bisection reads);
2. compiled the UNMUTATED target once (R461, `plainBuild`), which on DC compiles when the control add-in script files
   exist, and would have stopped with `UnmutatedBuildFailedError` first if they do not (R315's AL0327);
3. bisected (`bisectAndNote`, `bisectFailingMutant`): 1 compile of the full set, about log2(n) halving compiles (n is
   the batch's mutants, 17 for the single 117971-mutant batch of an unbounded run), 1 compile of the culprit alone, and
   1 of the complement. Roughly 20 DC-sized compiles for one batch, each a full alc run;
4. because 30 independent sites break the compile, the complement still fails, so the outcome is "environmental":
   "not attributable to any SINGLE mutant ... or MORE THAN ONE mutant breaks the compile", and every mutant of the
   batch (`execute`) is recorded `error` with that note, then the next batch runs. With `--max-guards-per-batch` the
   damage is per batch (file granularity); without it, the one batch is the whole run: 117971 `error` verdicts and no
   kill data.

The cost was a whole run's worth of mutants scored `error`, after about 20 wasted compiles. This was read from the code,
not run: the bisection was not executed on DC.
