**Filed 2026-10-24 by R-702 (lethal-preproc)**, evidence `/coord/handoff/R-702/measure-gap1.md` (the 10-loop sample)
and `measure.md`. A plausible hang that is NOT refused. [[R702]]'s gap 1 (callee movers and inserters) was closed by
ruling; this is the one sampled loop that could really hang.

**What.** DC `CDC Convert Templ. to eDoc.`, procedure `CopyCreateNeweDocTemplate`: a Next walk calls `CDC
Template.Clone`, which inserts a copy into the walked table. The copy lands ahead of the cursor; a later `Modify` and
an identifier assignment move it out of the walk's filter. A mutant that deletes that `Modify` or that assignment
keeps the copy in the filter, so the walk can meet its own copies forever.

**The hazard is a deleted STATEMENT in the callee's mover, not a deleted exit**, so [[R696]]'s exit refusal does not
apply even with the callee followed. Whoever picks this up: check whether R696's mover-write refusal (the writes
before a mover, `r696Writes`) should simply follow one hop into the callee, and measure that rule's cost by parse
first. Never run the shape live outside `itest:hang` (R164).

**Done 2026-10-24 by R-717 (lethal-preproc)**, evidence `/coord/handoff/R-717/measure.md` (`cost/`, `cost2/`,
`cost-built/`), scripts `scripts/r717/`, tests `r717-filter-fixups.test.ts`.

- **Read by parse, the hazard is NOT in the callee.** `Clone` inserts a copy with the SAME filter values. The fix-up
  that moves it out of the walk's filter is in the CALLER's body: a write of the filtered `XML Ident. Template No.` on a
  second variable, then its `Modify`. R696 missed it because a second variable's Modify is a mover only after a KEY
  write.
- **The suggested rule was measured and rejected.** Following R696's mover-write refusal one hop costs BaseApp 955
  (0.13%), DC 87 and DO 11, and refusing the whole callee costs BaseApp 2,369, DC 232 and DO 19. Neither covers the
  case.
- **Built (ruling 2026-10-24): `r717Fixups`.** The trigger is a Next walk that inserts into its own table: in its
  body, through a one-hop callee (`r696CalleeInserts`), or through a bare procedure of the same object
  (`r717BareInserts`). In such a walk, a second variable's fix-up is refused: a Modify after a write of a field the walk
  filters on, or a ModifyAll of such a field. Also refused: its writes, the walk's pre-loop filters (deleting one has
  the same effect) with their guards, and the feeds of all of these. No exit is refused for a fix-up alone. Every field
  counts when R is not a local, or when R is passed to a call before the walk (a helper may filter it).
- **Cost by parse** (`cost-built2/`, after the build review): DC 11 specs, all in the Clone file; BaseApp 19, in 4
  files; DO and CDO 0.
- **Limits:** a fix-up made inside a callee, and an insert through a callee called on a temporary receiver.
