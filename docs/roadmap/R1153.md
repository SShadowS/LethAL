Found by coord PR-217 (lethal-bugs, 2026-11-19), in the keep-list audit after PERF-10's restart storm. Evidence:
`/coord/handoff/PR-217/keep-audit.md` (section 2), real al-runner output in `/coord/handoff/PR-213/repros.json` and
`/coord/handoff/PR-175/repros.json` (`... another indent than 4 (not pinned)`).

**What is wrong.** `pr56Entry` (al-runner-server.ts) builds `^NotRun: out-of-scope: <text>$`. For these 12 `PR56_KEPT` rows, al-runner
(already at edge-4ebfe7913, `current`) ends the text with ` (not pinned)`, which the rows lack, so they never match and every such
refusal closes the daemon, the opposite of what PR-56 meant: `yaml-list-indent`, `yaml-null-or-number`, `yaml-blank-line-nested`,
`yaml-flow-quoted-key`, `dotnet-copilot-platform-authorization`, `gotokey-statement-no-row`, `asdecimal-text-control`,
`page-no-record-onopenpage`, `xmlport-import-header`, `part-visible-other-shape`, `geturl-company-characters`,
`geturl-object-type-or-arguments`. The PR-54 census normalised the suffix away, the rows copied the normalised key, and the pin
(`tests/pr56-measured-refusals.json`) holds the same normalised texts, so the test passed for the wrong reason. It is fail-safe
(a closed daemon is today's behaviour), and DO meets none of the 12 (y3's 25 refusal closes are other texts).

**To do:**
- (a) For each of the 12: a kept-vs-fresh DO (or corpus) check that exercises its REAL text, then widen that row to the real
  text. Never all at once on the census alone (principle 9).
- (b) Every keep entry's test runs against a real al-runner output line (from a repro run or a captured daemon row), not a
  normalised census key. PR-244 (code) adds the run-time near-miss warning, which should fire on all 12.
