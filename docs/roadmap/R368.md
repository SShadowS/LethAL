**Found by R-214's pre-commitment (2026-09-30, ruling T1-g).** A call statement whose head (the
callee and its first arguments) is inside `#if ... #endif` and whose remaining arguments follow the
`#endif` parses as `preproc_split_call_statement`. Master emits no `void-method-call` for it. The
compiled build has the whole call when the arm is active.

**Rule:** smallest directive node `preproc_split_call_statement` and `lethal.void-method-call`.

**Lost.** Corpora: dc.0 2 (`.dependencies/DC/Codeunit/CDCContiniaConfigMgt.Codeunit.al` L890 and
L1265); all others 0. No repro yet: a hand-written one is the first step of a fix.

**Out of R-214's scope.** A split call is not a statement-level `#if`. Under DC's S1 the same file
is unbuildable (R370), because the call loses its callee.

**Closed 2026-10-05 as a ruling: not found outside DC.** The R-364 census
(`/coord/handoff/R-364/measurement.md`, per `app.json` project: BaseApp's 203, CDO at 5f2a71d,
fixtures and examples, under `[]` and under each project's own symbols) found 0 lost sites in
every corpus and set. R-214's 2 sites were in DC, which this census did not measure. Nothing wrong
is emitted; a site is missing. The census's alc probe (shape P4) now repros it: the split call
loses its `void-method-call` and `swap-call-arguments` under both sets.
