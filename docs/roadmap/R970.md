Found by lethal-code, 2026-11-13 (coord R-942 window 1c: Continia DO, transport one-shot, isolation auto, master e8bf6c18; evidence
`/coord/handoff/R-942/doc.w1.*`, `/coord/handoff/R-942/watchdog.log`). Related: [[R961]], [[R896]], [[R866]], [[R953]].

**What happened.** The baseline completed (1,580 of 1,979 green in 6,450 s). R896's `auto` then re-baselined the cascade codeunits one
al-runner process per test, `ISOLATED_BASELINE_PROCESSES` (4) at a time. The run's process tree grew to 20 GB while the container's
headroom fell to 2 GiB (other lanes' jobs shared the 32 GiB), and the lane's external watchdog sent SIGTERM; R955 wrote the report
("TIME BUDGET REACHED IN THE AUTO ISOLATION RE-BASELINE: 1979 of 1979", `stoppedBySignal`). No mutant was reached.

**Why it matters.** R-948's in-process memory guard admits a call only at its start: four processes that are already running can grow
past the bound unseen. A cgroup OOM kill chooses its victim, so this came within 2 GiB of killing another lane's job. Only the external
watchdog stopped it.

**Wanted.** A periodic check during the parallel fills (one-shot `fillIsolatedBaseline` and the daemon fills): read cgroup headroom
every few seconds and, over the bound, stop starting work and stop the run by name through R955's path (the in-flight processes end at
their stop); and/or a lower parallelism for the per-test fill, declared with `heavy-slot.sh --peak` (R-952), so admission accounts for
it. Test with an injected memory reader that rises during the fill.

**Meanwhile.** DO's next window runs with `isolation: "test"` (no auto re-baseline; R-942 amendment 3).
