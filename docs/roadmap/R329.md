**Filed by RUST-03 S4.3 (2026-09-29).** Evidence: AMENDMENT 1 and AMENDMENTs 5 and 10 of
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-rust-03-precommitment.md`.

**What was measured.** W4 is `lethal run --project <BaseApp> --dry-run`, timed with
`scripts/measure-peak.ts`, median of 3 runs, native parser:
- S0, at `e5bed71` (the native switch applied as a scratch diff): 389.99 s.
- S4.2a, at `167f540c`: 463.23 s (before and after that fix alike).
- Final tree, `5e8a537d`: 493.72 s.

Output is byte-identical at every point (788,619 lines, sha256 `f31530b0...`), and peak memory is
flat (5,202 to 5,001 MB median), so this is wall time only. The slowdown was already there before any
S4 fix touched W4's path, so it came in between `e5bed71` and `167f540c`: the master merges into the
RUST-03 branch, the switch commit itself (`9f7cb5f0`) against the scratch diff S0 measured, or a
slower machine on the later days. None of these was measured on its own.

**Where a bisect starts.** The three timed points are the S0 run (AMENDMENT 1, `e5bed71` plus the
S0 scratch switch diff, 389.99 s), the S4.2a runs (AMENDMENT 5, `167f540c`, 463.23 s) and the S4.3
runs (AMENDMENT 10, `5e8a537d`, 493.72 s). `e5bed71..167f540c` is 159 commits. The merges in it that
change product code on W4's path are RUST-03 S1 (`6294768b`), S3, the switch (`4d3cd177`, carrying
`9f7cb5f0`), R-316 (`fd000a71`, split-header procedures as members), R-309 (`a295e52a`) and R-303
run 002 (`f7a15782`). `167f540c..5e8a537d` is 32 commits, with the merges `d10f518a` (RUST-03 S4.1,
S4.2a, S4.2c), `99de14aa` (R-319, al-runner only) and `ffaeb566` (R-320, CI only).

**Why it is not urgent.** Speed is the owner's third priority, after no crash and lower RAM, and W4
is well under its 16,384 MB ceiling.

**What would close it.** Time W4 on one day, one run at a time, at `e5bed71` plus the S0 scratch diff,
at `9f7cb5f0`, at `167f540c` and at HEAD, to find which range added the time; then profile that
range, or record that it was machine drift.
