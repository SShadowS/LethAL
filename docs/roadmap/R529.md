**Filed 2026-10-08 by the [[R500]] build** (plan r3 `/coord/reviews/R-500-plan/plan.r3.md`, shape 3, exclusion (a)).
A plausible hang that is NOT refused.

**What.** R500 shape 3 (a stated LIMIT, `altersSelfInsertFilter` in `loop-hazard.ts`) refuses the OnPreDataItem
filter calls of a data item that inserts into the table it walks (Date Compress keeps its walk off new entries
with `SetRange("Entry No.", 0, LastEntryNo)`). "Inserts" is read only in the item's triggers and the same-object
procedures they reach. An item whose insert happens in a codeunit it calls is not recognised, so a mutant that
deletes or widens that filter is still emitted and can walk the rows it is adding forever.

## Closed by ruling

R-522 measurement, 2026-10-23 (`/coord/handoff/R-522/measure.md`, section R529). 18 items insert from another
object, all in BaseApp (CDO, DC, DO: 0). All 18 were read and none consumes: inserts before the walk (Segment,
the cash flow AI buffer, both certificates of supply), after it (Transfer Budget to Actual), and rows the item's
filter excludes (Move IC to Partner Comp, 5 items, filter on Table ID; Refresh Planning Demand, Planning Level).
Covered today by the OnPreDataItem filter rule (shape 3) where the item has one. Stated limit: 9 of the 18 insert
into a TEMPORARY record through a callee whose parameter the detection reads as non-temporary (a blind spot of the
detection, not a hazard found). Its in-object sibling is [[R530]].

R-696 later found that this census missed Resend Attachments. [[R696]] covers it through its shape A mover clause (a
Validate of the SORTING field, then `Modify(true)` on a second variable of the item's table). The two-hop insert route
that item also has remains outside both rules.

**To close (original plan, closed by the ruling above).** Follow the item's one-hop callees (as [[R500]] shape 2 does) when looking for inserts, measure, and
land it or close it with a ruling.
