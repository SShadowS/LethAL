**Measured 2026-09-03.** `scripts/r198-group-runner-probe/drive.ps1` E8 (second run) and
`drive-stop-race.ps1` (10 rounds): a 45 s bounded hang in a test method, ended from a second
session by `StopSession`. Twelve times the held request came back **HTTP 408** with "The session was
stopped by an AL StopSession call", which `isAlStopResponse` recognises and
`run-mutant-transport.ts` scores `timeout`. Once it came back **HTTP 400** with "Cannot establish a
connection to the SQL Server/Database", 3.9 s after the stop, and the session was just as dead
(its progress row never advanced, its inserted row was rolled back). Which error the stopped
thread hits first is a race inside the NST.

**Today's consequence.** `run-mutant-transport.ts` scores `timeout` only for `stopFired && 408 &&
isAlStopResponse`; any other non-2xx after the stop falls to `in-flight-unknown`. The orchestrator
reconciles: the stop tombstoned the op, so rule 1 says `completed` and retries ONCE as a fresh
attempt, which re-runs the hang, burns another budget, and ends in `result-lost` or a quarantine.
`itest:hang` pins the 408 path and has never seen the other.

**What would fix it.** The stop hook's answer is already in hand (`stopped: true` means
`TryStopHungRun` matched our attempt and opSeq, tombstoned the op and called `StopSession`). A
confirmed stop followed by ANY non-2xx on the held request inside the grace window is the same
event as the 408, and should score the same, with BC's words recorded either way. The R198 redraft
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-03-r198-run-mutant-loop.md`) states that rule for the loop's
per-method stop; it applies to today's single-method stop unchanged, and should land there too.
The residual risk, a genuine SQL outage in the same 30 s window as our own confirmed stop, leaves
the op tombstoned and the session dead either way. Not measured: whether the 400 is tied to a
freshly published app (the one occurrence was 10 s after a publish).
