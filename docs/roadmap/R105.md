**Two session-level bookkeeping structures in `runSession` are write-only and confirmed dead, kept only to avoid reopening a frozen contract.** `outcomes: SessionOutcome[]` (built by `record()`, `orchestrator.ts`) and `permissionRefusedTests`/`runnerDisagreementTests` (the two `Set<string>`s threaded through `RunMutantsOnBackendArgs`) were the event-stream refactor's (spec 2026-08-05 §A, `7449a89`) intended casualties — `buildReport` reads `collectedEvents` now, never `outcomes[]`, and the two report fields these Sets used to feed (`permissionsRefused`/`runnerDisagreement`) are folded from `mutant-scored.permissionRefusedTest`/`.runnerDisagreementTest` instead. Left in place deliberately: `record()`'s `outcomes` parameter is a Task 3 contract `events-orchestrator.test.ts` drives directly, and `RunMutantsOnBackendArgs`'s two Sets would need touching both its interface and its two call sites (sequential + worker fan-out) to remove — real surface area for zero behavioural gain. Confirmed genuinely dead (not merely believed so) during the whole-branch review: no consumer reaches either on any path to `buildReport`. Evidence: `packages/runner/src/orchestrator.ts` (the `outcomes`/`permissionRefusedTests`/`runnerDisagreementTests` declarations in `runSession`, each now documented in place as dead), `.superpowers/sdd/2026-08-05-event-stream/task-4-report.md` §"Residual dead code".
