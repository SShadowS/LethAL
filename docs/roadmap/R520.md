**Filed 2026-10-08 by the [[R500]] build** (plan r3 `/coord/reviews/R-500-plan/plan.r3.md`, shape 2; survey
`/coord/handoff/R-500/survey.md`, shape 2). A plausible hang that is NOT refused.

**What.** R500 refuses a procedure in another project object that open report-item code calls, and the
same-object procedures it reaches (`inOneHopCallee` in `loop-hazard.ts`). A call from that callee into a THIRD
object is not followed. The r1 census followed calls up to six hops: 37,137 BC.History census sites against 15,930
at one hop. A value the item's exit depends on can be computed two objects away (an iterator codeunit that
delegates its `Next` to a record procedure, for example).

**Why not now.** Cost: every extra hop widens the refused set towards most of BaseApp, and the r1 sample of
other-object sites found 0 hangs in 21 reads; the hangs found were one hop away.

**To close.** Count the second-hop sites with production code, read a sample by hand, then refuse a measured hop
count or close it with a ruling.

## Closed 2026-10-10 by ruling (coord task R-521)

Measured in `/coord/handoff/R-521/measure.md`, "R520: the second hop", on fresh master `4b71102a` keys. From every
procedure the one-hop rule reaches in another object, every call it follows, plus the same-object closure, minus
what master already refuses: **21,025 deployed added (0.945% of BC.History, 2.67% of BaseApp, 49% of PEPPOL)**.
DC, DO, CDO and the fixtures have no open report-item code: 0. A general sample of 12 second-hop calls across 2
projects (BaseApp 11, PEPPOL 1) found **0 hangs** (with R-500's r1 sample, 0 in 33).

**Ruling.** The general second hop is not built: a large price for a theoretical risk, as R-532 ruled. The one real
second-hop hang found (`Export Item Budget to Excel` -> `Item Budget Management`.NextRecord -> `PeriodPageManagement`.
NextDate) needs only a narrow rule, filed as [[R569]]: follow the second hop only from a callee whose result feeds an
exit.

**Re-open if** a hang is found that only a GENERAL second hop would catch, that is, one that [[R569]]'s exit-fed rule
would not follow.
