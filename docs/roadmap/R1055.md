Filed and closed by coord PR-41 (2026-11-15). Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-pr41-v3-timeout-no-reproduction.md`
(addenda 1 and 2). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-41/trace.md`, `gate-v3.log`.

**What.** On the al-runner v3 serve transport, a mutant whose prefetch was cut at a timeout on test T1 of a codeunit, and killed by
another test T2 of it (run alone, R895), had its R577 reproduction sent as a live call of the whole codeunit: it stopped at T1's
hang again (a timeout and a daemon restart), and then T2 ran alone a second time (trace M11468: P + A + L + A2).

**Fix.** The backend records, per mutant, the tests its calls were cut at (cleared per prefetch window and per layout). A further
run of that mutant's test `ref` is an alone call when the mutant has a cut test in `ref`'s codeunit that is not `ref`. A cut at
`ref` itself stays a live whole-codeunit call, so the reproduction runs in the kill's own context. NONE, other kills and bcdev are
unchanged; no verdict policy, schema or report change. The skip of the reproduction for a CPU-time stop (withdrawn here, B1/B2;
the source answered upstream #8168) is the follow-up PR-43.
