**Priority.** Filed 2026-10-08 by the R516 + R515 build, from the R-516 plan (r2, section D3b) and
its adversarial review (I1, I4). A false-kill risk on al-runner `--server` and resource-with-server
only. Found by reading the code; NOT measured live: no `itest:alrunner` leg produces a timeout.
[[R516]] closed the matching false kill for bcdev and al-runner one-shot, and says it does not cover
this path.

**What.** The `--server` daemon is spawned with no `AL_RUNNER_TEST_TIMEOUT_SEC`
(`defaultServerSpawn` in `packages/runner/src/al-runner-backend.ts` inherits the process
environment), so al-runner's `TestExecutor` stops a test at its own `DefaultTestTimeoutSeconds` of
60 s, timed on the test BODY (al-runner source `AlRunner/TestExecutor.cs`, read at 43f7617, the
container's current build). LethAL never sends it the budget. Meanwhile every per-test
`durationMs` on that path is the whole SUITE's wall clock (`runViaServer` gives each test
`suite.wallMs`), so every budget and every unmutated confirm is judged on the suite, compile
included. `ensureServerSuite` sets its own deadline at `max(budget, 10 min)`.

The sequence (the review's, exactly): T's body is 58 s and the rest of the suite plus the compile is
30 s. The baseline is 88 s, so the budget is 180 s (the default floor). Under an unrelated mutant T
takes 61 s and al-runner times it out at its 60 s default. The confirm runs the suite unmutated in
88 s; 2 x 88 <= 180, so R516's rule scores it `timeout-killed`: a false kill.

**A second sequence, through [[R515]]'s re-budget (R516 build review, I1).** The suite takes 100 s,
so the budget is 200 s. M1 does not touch T, but T times out at the daemon's 60 s; the confirm takes
105 s, 2 x 105 > 200, so M1 is not killed, and R515 would re-budget T to 210 s. M2 times out at the
same 60 s; its confirm takes 103 s, 2 x 103 <= 210: a false `timeout-killed`. Raising the budget
moved no stop. Closed in the R516 build by skipping the re-budget where the backend's
`inRunStopIsBudget` is false (al-runner under `serverMode`): see [[R515]]. The first sequence above
is NOT closed by that, since it needs no re-budget.

**Evidence.** The names above: `defaultServerSpawn` (no env), `runViaServer` (`suite.wallMs` as
every test's duration), `ensureServerSuite` (deadline `max(budget, 10 min)`), and
`DefaultTestTimeoutSeconds = 60` in `TestExecutor.cs` (read via `/work/src/al-runner-src` at
43f7617; the env var is read in the same file, else the default). Not measured.

**Proposed close.** Spawn the daemon with `AL_RUNNER_TEST_TIMEOUT_SEC = ceil(F / 1000)`, where F is
the session's `--mutant-timeout-ms` floor (fixed for the daemon's life), and judge a server confirm
as `2 x measuredDurationMs <= F`, using R272's per-test figure rather than the suite's wall clock.
Probe first, live: does the daemon honour the variable, and does each row carry the test's own
duration.

**Follow-up in this item (ex-Q2 of the R-516 plan, review I4).** On al-runner ONE-SHOT, judge the
confirm on `measuredDurationMs ?? durationMs` instead of the wall clock. That closes R516's one-shot
lost-kill band (a genuine hang is reported `timeout-unconfirmed` whenever compile plus body takes
more than half the budget; e.g. a 65 s compile, R222's 553-file app, loses every hang kill under a
budget below 130 s) and [[R514]]'s M3. It needs the same probe for one-shot's per-test figure.

**To close.** The probe's answers recorded; the daemon spawned with the floor; the server confirm
judged on the per-test figure; an offline test of the review's sequence (a false kill today, not
after) red-checked both ways; the re-budget sequence above still not a kill, and, if the fix makes
the daemon's stop the budget, `inRunStopIsBudget` turned true under `serverMode` only with an offline
test that a re-budgeted run really gets the larger stop; `itest:alrunner` unchanged on every leg.
