**Filed 2026-10-21 by lethal-code**, from the orchestrator's R-598 merge verify. A follow-up to [[R593]].

**What happened.** At 23:05Z the merge verify for R-598 ran while two heavy jobs were running: R-586's legs and a
CDO slice. The load average was about 9.6. The parallel full verify (R593, P = min(8, cores/4)) had two timeouts at
5000 ms:
- `publish-ceiling.test.ts`, "reports the tier's measured bracket";
- `resume.test.ts`, "an old store gains both tables".

Both pass alone, and a full re-run passed 8949 / 0. R593's equivalence set was measured in a quiet window, with no
local heavy job, so it could not see this. This is the R482 starvation case under real load.

**The rule.** Lower the parallelism; never raise a timeout. (R593's plan says the same.)

**To close.**
1. Make `verify.ts` choose P from the current load: for example, scale P down with the load average, or with the
   count of running heavy jobs (corpus-snapshot's `isHeavy` scan), down to 1.
2. Print the chosen P and its reason in the receipt.
3. Measure it: re-run the two named tests' files at the chosen P beside two heavy jobs, at least 10 times, with zero
   timeouts.
4. Add a unit test for the P choice in each direction (high load gives a lower P; a quiet machine keeps 8),
   red-checked.

**Measured since (R-622, 2026-10-22).** Load alone did not reproduce it.
- 20 full verifies at P=8 (`/coord/handoff/R-622/repro.md`): 5 in a quiet window (load 8 to 13) and 15 beside
  a 24-thread CPU burner (load 17 to 49). All 20 passed, with no timeout.
- 500 runs of `real-home-guard.test.ts` alone, 8 and 16 at once, at load about 45: all passed.
- So P=8 never failed in any of these runs. The burner used only CPU; the heavy jobs of 23:05Z also used
  memory and disk.

**A second failure, at 05:33Z on 2026-10-22, at LOW load.** A full verify on master at P=8 failed with 10
fails, 9 of them "timed out after 5000 ms". All 9 were tests that open a SQLite database on disk (store
migrations, resume, bundle pruning, `store-query`). The load average was 6.0. Logs:
`/coord/handoff/R-622/r616/orchestrator-evidence/`. No load rule would have prevented it: at load 6 the planned
rule still gives P=6.

**The suspicion: several full verifies at once, from several lanes, on one disk.** It could not be checked,
because verify kept its log only when it failed, so the other runs of that minute left no trace.

**Another possible cause at 05:33Z.** R-622's own watchdog red checks were running then: `verify.test.ts`
spawning inner `bun test` runs with 25 s busy-loop files and a test that prints 20 MB. That is not a full
verify, but it does load the machine and the disk.

**The ledger (R-622).** Every verify now appends a START and an END line to `/coord/locks/verify-ledger.tsv`
(`LETHAL_VERIFY_LEDGER` overrides it) and prints how many other verifies overlapped it. **Decide from the
ledger at the next timeout**: if other verifies overlapped, limit how many verifies run at once; if none did,
look elsewhere (the disk, or the job beside it).

## Test DBs on tmpfs (R-616)

**The measurement** (`/coord/handoff/R-616/measure.md`). The timeouts are `fsync` waits on the shared disk. Under
other jobs' disk writes one `fsync` took up to 5 s. Two verifies at once never failed alone (0 of 8 here, 0 of 20
in R-622). With 3 disk writers, every verify failed, at P=8 and at P=4, and even a single verify did. On /dev/shm
(a tmpfs, a RAM disk) the same DB tests stayed under 80 ms under the same load. So the fix is where the scratch DBs
live, not how many workers run. The shared worker budget was dropped (orchestrator ruling, plan r2).

**What changed** (plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-r616-test-dbs-on-tmpfs.md`, section r2):
- `packages/runner/tests/helpers/test-db.ts`: `chooseDbDir` picks the tmpfs root when it is a directory, is
  writable and has at least 16 MB free (`MIN_FREE_MB`); otherwise disk, and each fallback is named. The root is ONE
  setting, `LETHAL_TEST_TMPFS_ROOT` (default /dev/shm), so moving to another tmpfs is a one-line change.
  `LETHAL_TEST_DB_ON_DISK=1` forces disk. `testDbPath` gives a fresh DB path and throws by name
  (`TestDbDirMissingError`) when the preload did not load. `sweepDeadDbDirs` removes the folders of dead test
  processes (a watchdog kill skips `afterAll`), and keeps live ones.
- `scripts/test-preload.ts`: one DB folder per test process, removed in `afterAll`. R358's leak check no longer
  sees DB folders; `afterAll` and the sweep remove them.
- The scratch DBs of store, resume, orchestrator, cli (including the `runThenArtifact` store, review B1),
  cli-envtool, publish-ceiling, runner-provenance, r474-context and scripts' store-query tests use `testDbPath`.
  Project folders and artifacts stay on disk. No test needed a real disk path. Production `lethal.sqlite` is
  untouched and stays on disk.
- `scripts/verify.ts`: a `test DBs:` line counts where EACH worker put its DBs (each process reports itself), and
  `pressure at start:` / `pressure at end:` give `/proc/pressure/io` and `/cpu` (avg10/avg60/avg300), skipped and
  named where unreadable. The ledger gains the pressure as a 10th column; 9-column lines still parse, and a verify
  from before this change skips 10-column lines until it merges master.

**The proof** (`/coord/handoff/R-616/proof/proof.md`). The timeout no longer reproduced at this load after the
32 GB recreate (page cache is the likely cause, unmeasured): the on-disk control arm had 0 failed tests in both
verifies. The control was 3 writers plus two verifies at P=8, DBs forced onto disk, with io PSI full (the share of
time all tasks stalled on I/O) at 27%. So the pass/fail proof is void, and a margin proof replaced it (orchestrator
ruling): the /dev/shm arm under the same load (io PSI full 26.5%). The fix moves the DB tests from up to 2.9 s to
0.64 s under load, so the margin to the 5 s limit grows from about 2 s to about 4.4 s.
- Slowest / summed time of the 65 fsync-bound tests under load: disk 2689 / 35025 ms and 2896 / 30455 ms;
  /dev/shm 73 / 1296 ms and 644 / 2013 ms.
- Tests over 2500 ms: 12 on disk, 0 on /dev/shm. Every verify in every arm had 0 failed tests.
- Quiet, 2 runs per arm: on disk 31.1 and 31.3 s, on /dev/shm 30.7 and 30.6 s. The 65 tests' sum fell from
  5.3 s to 1.0 s.
- A full verify's DBs peak at 856 KB on /dev/shm (1572 KB for two at once).

**The whole temp folder on tmpfs (r3 measurement, not built).** A full verify with the WHOLE temp folder on
/dev/shm peaked at 213 to 260 MB but FAILED 19 tests: /dev/shm is mounted `noexec`, and some tests run scripts they
write into the temp folder (`/coord/handoff/R-616/r3-peak.txt`, `r3-verify.log`). That option (with a 512 MB floor)
needs an exec tmpfs from the owner, for example `--tmpfs /work/tmpfs:rw,exec,size=2g`. It would also cover the 4
non-SQLite hook timeouts (mkdtemp, rm), which this change cannot reach.

**What stays open (review I3).** The 05:33Z outliers have no explanation: R373 took 7116 ms on a `:memory:` store,
and h-multiline-before 5000 ms. Moving DBs cannot reach them. The pressure figures in the receipt and the ledger
are there so the next such timeout can be read against the disk's state.
