**Scanner census, 2026-09-27.** The static TestPage scanner (`packages/runner/src/testpage-scan.ts`)
first aborted inside web-tree-sitter on all of `BC.History/BaseApp` because it kept every parse tree
alive: the wasm heap grew to its 2,048 MB ceiling at file 9,007 of 9,620. Fixed in `e01e88c` (each
tree is deleted once its facts are read; the heap stays at 32 MB). The re-run then completes but
reports 127 loud errors on BaseApp, all one shape: a test procedure inside an `#if not CLEANnn` region
that the grammar attaches to the codeunit's global `var` section, so the scanner never sees it. Open
until ruled. Evidence: `docs/measurements/2026-09-27-r236c-testpage-census.md`. The same retained-tree
class also broke the operator-site census on BaseApp (R292).

**Round 2, 2026-09-27.** The `var`-attached `#if` shape is a grammar defect, filed upstream as
tree-sitter-al #29 (<https://github.com/SShadowS/tree-sitter-al/issues/29>): on the vendored 4.4.1 the
procedure and its attribute sit under `var_section > var_body > preproc_conditional_var` with no error
node, while the AL compiler's own parser makes them codeunit members. The scanner now collects
procedures from every branch of such a region (`c7c5ea3`, kept until #29 is fixed), and BaseApp's 127
loud errors are gone. The codeunit lookup was also indexed: a linear scan per call site was 349 of
BaseApp's 415 s. Re-run numbers are in the census doc.
