**Found 2026-09-26, while building GH-24's reach grain.** `lethal.empty-block` lists
`repeat_statement` among the parents whose body block it empties, and the GH-24 plan's placement
rule P1 has a case for a block directly under a `repeat_statement`. MEASURED on the vendored
`tree-sitter-al.wasm`: a repeat's body is a `statement_block` in every shape tried
(`repeat begin ... end until`, the same without the inner `;`, with a `;` after `end`, and plain
`repeat A; B; until`), and a `begin ... end` written inside it is a `code_block` whose parent is
that `statement_block`. So no block has a `repeat_statement` parent, the entry claims nothing, and
the P1 repeat case is unreachable. The shape is pinned in `packages/schemata/tests/compile.test.ts`
("a repeat body block is enclosing, because the grammar puts it in the repeat's statement list").

**What this contradicts.** R180's census table gives 334 blocks whose parent is `repeat_statement`
on `do-rel2/Cloud`, and R179 says `empty-block` claims 110 `repeat` bodies there. Either those were
counted under an older grammar, or they counted a different node. It is R180's own failure shape (a
guard naming a plausible parent kind the grammar does not emit) one construct over.

**What it costs.** Every `repeat` body block goes unmutated by `empty-block`. It is not a wrong
verdict, it is a population that silently has no mutant. GH-24 is unaffected: a hand-built spec on
such a block resolves to the whole `repeat` and is `enclosing` grain, which compiles and never
throws.

**To close:** re-run the parent-kind census on `do-rel2/Cloud` with today's grammar. If repeat
bodies are wanted, target the `statement_block` under `repeat_statement` (or the `code_block`
inside it) and decide what the reach grain of that site is.

**Closed (012f91c8, R-244; orchestrator ruling: option A).** The census was re-run on today's
grammar over the BaseApp history, CDO and every fixture (`/coord/handoff/R-244/measurement.md`):
12,363 `repeat` loops, and NONE written `repeat begin ... end until`, which a plain-text search on
BaseApp confirms. So the entry could not have claimed anything even on a grammar where it worked;
R179's 110 and R180's 334 do not reproduce, and were most likely counted under an older grammar.
The entry is deleted, with a comment in its place saying why repeat bodies are not claimed, and a
test pins that none is, plain or `begin ... end`, whether its `until` advances a cursor or not. No
site moves, so `IDENTITY_SCHEME` stays at 16. RED-CHECKED: listing `statement_block` as a parent
fails the test (the two inner `begin ... end` blocks are claimed), and so does claiming the
repeat's own `statement_block` (all four bodies). Deleting the entry itself has no red-check: it
was dead, which is the finding. GH-24's P1 repeat case stays unreachable and is already pinned as
`enclosing`. Whether to mutate the cursor-advanced repeat bodies is filed as R467.
