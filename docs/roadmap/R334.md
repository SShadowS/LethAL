**Low priority. Filed by R-302 run 003 (2026-09-29, review minor M5).** The recovery for
[[R330]] and [[R331]]'s fail-safe, which is under-generation in the safe direction.

**What was lost.** Run 003 made every member the symbol table does not index "unknown": a plain
procedure wrapped whole in `#if`, a split member swallowed by the global `var` section ([[R327]]),
and the names a member or trigger declares inside its own `#if` region. Those names get no type, so
no typed operator claims a site that reads them.

That removed 62 mutants:

- 19 in DC/Cloud, now 102584 raw and 97126 deployed;
- 43 across all of `BC.History/BaseApp`.

The plan's addendum 3 pins them. Every one sat inside a `#if`-wrapped member and was typed from
another member's declarations (a same-named DIRECT overload) or from a global.

These 62 are NOT known to be invalid. Many of them probably compiled, because the other overload
often has the same parameter types, so the borrowed type was right. They were removed because
nothing could PROVE their type, not because they were shown to fail. Nobody compiled DC or BaseApp
to tell the valid ones from the invalid ones. Recovering the valid ones is this item's purpose.

**Fix direction.** Index a `#if`-wrapped member as a symbol of its own, and resolve its names by
[[R302]]'s every-arm rule:

- a name resolves only when every build that has the member declares it with the same type;
- anything else is ambiguous.

Then each such member types by its OWN declarations, and the valid sites return. The same applies
to `#if`-wrapped objects, which are also unindexed today.

**Deferred (R-308, 2026-10-23); upper bounds only.** Counted by parse, as identifier references, not typed operator sites; nothing was compiled:

| corpus | #if-wrapped members | unindexed | refs in unindexed | distinct files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BaseApp | 2,310 | 214 | 3,046 | 28 |
| other BC.History | 604 | 72 | 499 | 9 |
| DC | 227 | 74 | 1,021 | 10 |

Limits: nested BaseApp roots double-count members; triggers and object-level gaps are not counted (xmlport, query and `#if`-split headers: 138 in BaseApp, 173 elsewhere). The recoverable sites are unknown until a typed generation run measures them. The change loosens a refusal, so it needs a deep review and a false-kill hunt. Deferred for those reasons.

Pre-commit the corpus effect first, because this ADDS sites, and compile a sample of them under
every symbol subset.

## Re-measured 2026-10-24 (coord task R-334, lethal-code): mostly recovered by R405 (a), not R340

On master c0bc6ef6, against the c03d5356 corpus dump. Nothing was compiled. The scripts and raw output are in
`/coord/handoff/R-334/census/`.

**R340 did not do it.** R340 types a TRIGGER's header names; these are procedures. **[[R405]] (a) did**
(`bd5ce769`, 2026-10-04). With the build's arm map, the symbol table indexes a member inside a member-level `#if` whose
arm the build compiles, and that member types by its own declarations. That is this item's fix direction, for the
build's arm instead of every arm. Nothing else is needed for those members, because a member in a compiled-out arm
carries no mutant.

**The 62 pinned rows (addendum 3 of `2026-09-28-R-302-split-semantics.md`).** Each was matched by file, member and
operator against the dump, with the member's declarations read from today's source. Sources have moved since
2026-09-29, so line numbers were not used.
- 49 rows sit in a member that is still `#if`-wrapped today. For 47 of them, the wrapped declaration emits that
  operator again.
- The other 2 (`CalculatePlanFromWorksheet`, `CreateEmailBodyFromReportSelections`) are in the residual shape below.
- In the last 13 rows the member is no longer wrapped in today's source (8 in `ServPEPPOLManagement`, and 5 in 3
  other files), so they no longer measure this item.

**The residual, by parse** (`residual.txt`):
- **235 members are swallowed by the global var section** (BaseApp 161, other BC.History 52, DC 22; 29 files). The
  parse path is `var_section > preproc_conditional_var > procedure`. An object-level `var` section followed by a
  `#if` region of whole members parses with that region INSIDE the var section, so the symbol table never sees those
  members. It is [[R327]]'s grammar defect, which R327 measured only for split-header members ("0 swallowed"), in
  whole-member form. [[R369]] notes that the tree can stretch a var-section `#if` over whole members.
- **49 are in wrapped BaseApp xmlports** (48 in a `preproc_conditional_object`, 1 under a member `#if`), and **10
  are in objects whose header is split by `#if`** (other BC.History). These are object-level gaps outside the R-302
  class, as R-308 already noted.
- **Untyped operators still mutate the swallowed members.** DC's 22 carry 86 mutants (`empty-block`,
  `remove-assignment`, `void-method-call`). Only the typed sites are lost, and the references above are an upper
  bound on those.

**Still deferred.** Recovering the 235 would mean reading members out of a `preproc_conditional_var`. The better fix
is upstream: tree-sitter-al should end the var section at the `#if`, as R327's draft asks. Either way it loosens a
refusal, so it needs a pre-commitment, a deep review and a false-kill hunt.
