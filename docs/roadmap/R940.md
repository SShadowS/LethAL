Found by lethal-code, 2026-11-12 (coord R-925). Related: [[R912]], [[R915]].

**What happened.** After R915 the estimate sums each covering test's measured baseline time. On seven BC.History apps on v3 serve
(all under per-test `method` isolation), estimate against the mutants phase: Review G/L Entries 312 s vs 796 s (2.55x under),
Essential Business Headlines 1,716 vs 1,456 (1.18x over), Email - SMTP Connector 138 vs 571 (4.14x under), Late Payment Prediction
810 vs 1,536 (1.90x under), Sales and Inventory Forecast 37 vs 216 (5.84x under), Excel Reports 888 vs 654 (1.36x over), Field
Service Integration 72 vs 246 (3.41x under). Left out: the per-entry request cost, the echo entry, kill-pair confirm runs and daemon
warm-ups; and a warm daemon can run a test faster than its one baseline measurement. R912's and R915's text call it a lower bound on
test time, which these figures contradict.

**Invented repro.** A suite whose tests each take 5 ms but whose serve requests cost 100 ms each: the estimate says seconds, the phase
takes minutes.

**Wanted.** Either name it for what it is ("baseline time of the covering tests", no bound claimed) in the detail and in R912/R915, or
add a measured per-entry cost (from the warm-up or the first window) times the entries the windows will send, and say it is an
estimate. A per-entry fit over these seven apps missed by 2x or more on three, so measure before promising a bound.

**Measured and relabelled (coord R-931).** From R-925's and R-926's events and serve close lines (`/coord/handoff/R-931/estimate.ts`,
output `estimate.out`), nine finished al-runner-v3 serve runs (two unfinished ones left out):

- The phase is mostly the prefetch requests' wall time. The gap is not a per-entry cost: (phase - estimate) / entries runs from
  below 0 (Headlines, Excel Reports) to 0.71 s (Review G/L Entries), so no constant fits, and no input LethAL has at the estimate
  would make it a bound.
- It errs both ways: over, because a kill stops at its first failing test while the estimate sums every covering test, and a warm
  daemon can beat the one baseline measurement; under, because per-request, echo, warm-up and kill-confirm costs are not in it and
  vary by app (Late Payment Prediction's 18 daemons, R939).
- So it is relabelled, and nothing else changes (the windows still cut on it; not widened on a guess). The detail now reads
  `estimate: N covered mutant(s), about X of their covering tests' baseline time (not a bound or a forecast of this phase: on
  al-runner v3 serve it took 0.7 to 5.8 times it, R940)` (`ESTIMATE_NOT_A_BOUND`), and R912's "lower bound" sentence is corrected.
