title: "A RecoverOp that THROWS while reconciling a lost EndPublish ack escapes publish() with no latch and no recycle recorded"
section: "correctness-risks"

**Found while fixing R240 (2026-09-30), confirmed by reading.** `reconcileStrandedPublish`
(`packages/runner/src/orchestrator.ts`) handles a lost `EndPublish` ack. When the reconciling
`GetOperationStatus` throws, it calls `leaveStrandedPublish`, which latches the session and records a
`container-needs-recycle`. When the marker is ours and `recoverOp` answers "not recovered", it does
the same. But when `recoverOp` itself THROWS, the error escapes `publish()` directly: the session is
not latched and no recycle is recorded, although the server's publish marker may still be set.

**Why it matters.** The session still aborts, because the error propagates and `finish()` checks the
marker. But the durable record that tells the NEXT session the tier needs a recycle is exactly what
`leaveStrandedPublish` exists to write, and this path skips it. The direction of the other two
branches is "unreconciled means latch and record"; this one is "unreconciled means throw and hope".

**To close.** Treat a throwing `recoverOp` like a throwing `GetOperationStatus`: catch it and call
`leaveStrandedPublish` with both causes named. Test with the fake lease client: a `recoverOp` that
throws latches the session and records `container-needs-recycle`; a `recoverOp` that recovers
records nothing; red-check both.
