**Found by** PR-436 (lethal-code, 2026-11-25). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-436/findings.md`, `scaled.py`. The figures
are lane-seconds of c5 (PR-422), which ran under load.

- **What was measured:**
  - c5's scored mutants need about 106k entries: one per mutant and covering test codeunit.
  - Its requests carried more. `serve-request-sized` counts 123,941; `serve-request-timed` counts about 157k (prefetch
    126k + live 31k). The two counts disagree and must be reconciled first.
  - Each extra entry pays al-runner's fixed per-entry cost (R1219, about 260 ms) plus its rows (about 265 ms on
    average). That bounds this at about 18k-51k extra entries × ~0.53 s ≈ 9,000-27,000 lane-s, or 9-27% of the
    mutants phase.
- **Candidate causes, all LethAL-side:**
  - re-sends after a cut: a timeout row (R895), a refusal and the daemon close that follows, R612's cut;
  - kill-pair confirms (c5: 6,178);
  - live re-runs of entries whose prefetched rows could not be used;
  - carry walks.
- **Next (coord PR-438):** break the extra entries down by cause. Build only the largest cause, if it is at least 3%
  and no verdict can move.
- **Broken down by PR-438 (lethal-code, 2026-11-25; `/coord/handoff/PR-438/findings.md`):**
  - **The two counts reconcile.** The timed count includes echo entries and the sized count does not (about 33,400
    echoes). c5's own counter gives 106,137 mutant entries, the modelled need.
  - **Causes, priced by a fit over 3,654 requests** (`611 ms × test entry + 294 ms × echo`, R² 0.75):
    - echo entries (one per mutant group per request): ~33,000, about 9,700 lane-s, ~10%;
    - kill-pair entries (R653's confirm: the mutant re-run plus its NONE twin, 6,178 pairs): ~8-11%;
    - other re-sends (cut tails, cut-before-refused, refusal, timeout): ~3% together, each cause under 3%.
  - **Nothing built.**
    - Each echo checks that its group ran with the mutant it sent. Dropping echoes LethAL-side would let a misapplied
      resource go unseen.
    - Kill pairs are a false-kill guard.
    - The other re-sends are each under 3%.
  - **A route for echoes, and its two conditions (corrected by PR-441's spec-adversary).** al-runner reports the
    resources each entry ran with (`entrySummary[].resourcesHash`, asked through the orchestrator,
    `/coord/handoff/PR-438/alrunner-ask.md`). That field alone is NOT stricter than the echo.
    - The echo (`emitSelectorEcho`) compares `Selector.Active` with the selector's CACHED SingleInstance reading. So it
      also proves that nothing carried over from the previous entry, which a hash of al-runner's input cannot see.
    - Echoes may be dropped only when BOTH conditions hold:
      1. A per-build leg-C fact reads `matches`: consecutive entries with different resources each read their own
         selector value (no SingleInstance carry across entries).
      2. Each entry's hash is compared with LethAL's own sha of the resource bytes it sent for that entry, and a missing
         or empty field fails loudly.
