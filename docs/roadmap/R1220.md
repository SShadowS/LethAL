**Found by** PR-436 (lethal-code, 2026-11-25). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-436/findings.md`, `scaled.py`. The figures
are lane-seconds of c5 (PR-422), which ran under load.

- **What was measured:**
  - c5's scored mutants need about 106k entries: one per mutant and covering test codeunit.
  - Its requests carried more. `serve-request-sized` counts 123,941; `serve-request-timed` counts about 157k (prefetch
    126k + live 31k). The two counts disagree and must be reconciled first.
  - Each extra entry pays al-runner's fixed per-entry cost (R1219, about 260 ms) plus its rows (about 265 ms on
    average). That bounds this at about 18k-51k extra entries × ~0.53 s ≈ 9,000-27,000 lane-s, or 9-27% of the
    mutants phase.
- **Candidate causes, all LethAL-side:**
  - re-sends after a cut: a timeout row (R895), a refusal and the daemon close that follows, R612's cut;
  - kill-pair confirms (c5: 6,178);
  - live re-runs of entries whose prefetched rows could not be used;
  - carry walks.
- **Next (coord PR-438):** break the extra entries down by cause. Build only the largest cause, if it is at least 3%
  and no verdict can move.
