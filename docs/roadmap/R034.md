**A reused environment that is `Stopped` is not handled.** `env-tool-session` resolves it, finds a dead endpoint, and aborts. Observed live 2026-07-26: the gate environment had idled to `Stopped` and needed a manual `env start` plus a ~420 s wait. Starting someone else's environment unasked is worse than refusing, so the fix is to refuse *naming the status* — and possibly an opt-in `--start-env`. Predicted by the env-tool spec's own risk list before it happened.
