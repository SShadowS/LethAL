Found by coord PR-223 (lethal-bugs, 2026-11-19), for the alrunner tentacle. Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-223/result.md`.

**What was measured (the FORK's version).** The fork's runTests field `stopAtFirstFailure` (#8496, branch
perf-13-stop-at-first-failure 8694c1aad; now closed as a duplicate) stops a mutant's entries at its first failing test. Measured
on 90 DO mutants (one all-green codeunit of at most 25 tests each), one serve daemon per variant, y2's flags:
- **verdicts: 0 moved.** before, `"group"` and `"request"` each gave 39 killed and 51 survived, all equal to y2;
- `"group"` (`stopGroup` = the mutant id on each of its entries, 5 mutants per request): test time -20%, warm wall -23%, 79 of
  519 test rows skipped;
- `"request"` (one mutant per request) is slower than batching, because of the per-request setup (#8205/#8206), not the stop.
The saving is a LOWER BOUND: 57.5% of y2's killed mutants have larger covering sets (median 10 tests, max 364).

**Upstream's version differs by name and may differ in shape.** Upstream merged #8484: `stopAfterFirstFailure`, as a runTests
field, a `--stop-after-first-failure` flag and a serve option. When the first gated `current` carries it, re-measure before any use
(PR-223's shape: the same 90-mutant sample, before vs after, per mutant):
- the scope: per group (per mutant), per request, or per daemon. Only a per-mutant scope is usable for batched mutant requests;
- the skipped rows: the fork reported them as `notRun` with **status `"error"`**. Check upstream's shape.

**The trap (pin it first, whatever upstream's shape).** A skipped test must never count as a failure, a killing test, or an
`error` verdict. LethAL reads `notRun`, never the status. **A red test pins this before any use:** a fake answer with one fail and
then skipped rows -> killed by the failing test, the skipped rows named as skipped, no error verdict.

**Then build:** send the stop on mutant requests only, never on baseline, warm-up or recheck requests, which need every test's
outcome. Judge it by a full DO run against the then-latest fresh run, per mutant on the full site key, as a pre-committed speed
change.

**Upstream sweep (PR-240, 2026-11-19):** PR 8484 merged 11-19 (f7b156fb4), on upstream main, not in `current`
(4ebfe7913); the trigger stands. Its scope is the request ("later tests of its codeunit and every later entry of the
request", each codeunit on its own), not the fork's per-group `stopGroup`.
