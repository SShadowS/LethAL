**Found by** coord PR-409 (lethal-code, 2026-11-24), offline from c4's events, report and store (`/coord/handoff/PR-379/c4`;
full DO, c4's head b1a0e6717, main-bf3a3e863). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-409/findings.md`, `restarts.py`,
`walk-flags.py`.

- **What happens:**
  - c4 ran 129 carry walks (R1173's sequential re-walk of a labelled survivor), 12,197 s in all.
  - All 129 failed at request 1, on the same rule: a covering test "passed but al-runner flagged" it, and PR-340's B1
    leftover makes a flagged pass no sound pass.
  - The walk still sends all three requests (mutant, NONE, mutant) before `judgeCarryWalk` judges them.
  - 115 of the walks then closed the lane's daemon (`closeLane(lane, "unkept-row")`), each followed by a full-DO warm-up:
    6,407 s of warm-up wall, 4,363 s of it compile.
  - 0 kills.
  - The lanes' busy time was 56,873 s, so about 33% of it went to walks that could not give a verdict.
- **Why it is predictable:** in all 129 failed walks, the test that failed the walk was already flagged in c4's baseline
  snapshot, which flags 1,735 of DO's 1,979 baseline tests.
- **The candidate fix, LethAL-owned, needs a pre-commitment:**
  - Skip a carry walk whose kept codeunits hold a test the baseline flagged, or judge request 1 alone before sending
    requests 2 and 3.
  - Either way a failed walk never moves a verdict, so the claim to measure is the prediction itself: that a
    baseline-flagged test is flagged in the walk too. If it is not, a possible kill would be lost.
  - The check: a full-DO pair with the walk skipped against c4 per mutant, with verdicts and killingTest equal.
  - Also record why a carry walk closes the daemon. Its close event carries no family or text today, so which row was
    unkept is not in the data.
- **Not built here.** It needs its own DO check before landing.
