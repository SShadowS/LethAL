**Found by** coord PR-427 (2026-11-26): the E-Documents baseline of a private ISV app on bc4 (BC 29.0.54011.57673, LethAL
ac413797d, two `--only` files, 853 deployed, 266 baseline tests). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-427/edocs/` (private).

**What was measured** (the run's events):
- **The control app stopped answering at 8.92 h.** From 8.92 h into the run, `LethALControl_RenewLease` gave no answer
  within 30 s, twice in a row, 9 times (`lease-renew-unanswered`). LethAL treats that as "not lease loss" and kept
  scoring.
- **72 mutants (M0082-M0164) were scored after that point,** until 11.46 h.
- **Mutant M0164 is not a hang.** Its first group call (81 methods) ended at the 330 s hard cap after 33 methods, and its
  retry got no answer before headers. The run quarantined with "test in-flight-unknown". On al-runner v3 (edge
  e5176a9fd, the same scope), M0164 SURVIVED in 3.4 s, and no mutant of the scope was `timeout-killed`.
- **So the cap is the degraded tier, not the mutant.** The hung-session stop cannot act when the control app does not
  answer, and the doctor then finds an orphaned run lease that only a service-tier restart clears.
- **The late verdicts look different:**
  - against al-runner's verdicts on the same covered mutants, 7 of 60 BC kills before 8.92 h are al-runner survivors;
  - **22 of 72 after 8.92 h**, three times the rate;
  - a degraded tier failing tests reads as kills, and those are false kills.

**Why it matters:**
- `--resume` reuses every scored verdict, so the 72 late ones would enter the baseline unexamined.
- A run cannot tell a slow tier from a dead one. It needs a rule for when unanswered renews stop the scoring, rather than
  only being warned about.

**The fix to decide (not built):**
- after N consecutive `lease-renew-unanswered`, stop scheduling (as the time budget does) and mark the mutants scored
  since the first unanswered renew as suspect, re-run on resume;
- or quarantine at the first such streak.
- Either way, `--resume` must not silently keep verdicts from the window after the first unanswered renew.
- For PR-427: whether to re-score the 72 is the orchestrator's call. Nothing is excluded or deleted until then.
