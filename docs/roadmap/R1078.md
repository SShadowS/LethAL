nothing. The row it compared was a baseline fail. The guard stays as it is (ruling recorded in R1072).

**Part 2, the census.** A new generator family, `t9-refusals` in `scripts/refusal-family.ts` (15 invented cells, ids
51001-51015), produces each text t9 met in its mutant phase. The texts are exact, because al-runner's text is the class.
PR-54's strict `keep-census.ts` then measured 10 classes, all CLEAN. Each had `engineKept` on every kept reader, and every
refuser refused under serve. Every target text could be driven. The ZipArchive text needed an empty stream,
`Report.RunRequestPage` a `Commit()` after the kit's writes, and `Report.SaveAs` the Xml format.

**Kept** (`PR56_KEPT` in `al-runner-server.ts`; pinned by `al-runner-serve-pr74.test.ts` against
`tests/pr74-measured-refusals.json`):
- new entries:
  - `setfilter-value-expected` (`..` and `@`);
  - `evaluate-decimal-comparison` (`>100`, `>1000`, `<1000` only);
  - `report-parameters-no-xml-declaration` (RunRequestPage and SaveAs only);
  - `dotnet-zip-central-directory-corrupt`, exact;
  - `dotnet-json-writer-no-token-to-close`, exact;
