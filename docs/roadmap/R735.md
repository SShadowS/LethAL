**Filed by R-731** (2026-10-24). [[R734]] isolates breaking mutants by halving, one culprit at a time, under a compile
budget (`MAX_ISOLATION_COMPILES = 64`). With k culprits among n mutants that costs about k * (log2 n + 2) compiles,
so 30 culprits on DC is about 540, far over the budget. Past the budget the rest stays `error`, which is correct but
loses the verdicts.

**Idea.** alc already says where it failed. Parse its diagnostics (move `parseAlcErrors` from
`scripts/r315-dc-compile.ts` into the runner), map each error line up to the nearest `.Active('M...')` guard in the
emitted file, and try those mutants as the suspects first. A suspect is still confirmed by compiling it alone, so a
wrong guess costs a compile and never blames an innocent mutant. Estimate: k = 30 in about 36 compiles.

**Done when** a batch with 30 breaking mutants is isolated inside the budget, an offline test pins that the suspects
come from the diagnostics and that each is confirmed by a singleton compile, and a diagnostic that maps to no guard
falls back to the halving search of [[R734]].
