  or too small for a Decimal.".
- **Filed:** https://github.com/aldevtools/BusinessCentral.AL.Runner/issues/9267. The invented repro is refused on
  edge-e5176a9fd and on main-e806d7ebc (`repro-*.out`).
- **Control after the review** (`control/`, run on both builds and on bc1 under a second lease, cleaned up, doctor ok,
  released): the refusal comes from the arithmetic, not from the literal.
  - `7922816251426433759354395033.5 * 100`: BC raises the overflow text; al-runner refuses it.
  - The largest Decimal plus itself: the same on both sides.
  - Both are #9267's class; the issue body names only the multiplication.
  - The literal alone, formatted: both raise "The value of Decimal parameter is outside of the permitted range ...".
    The number inside the text differs (BC `...337,500,000,000,000`, al-runner `...337,593,543,950,335`). That is
    error text in a culture format, so it is not filed.
