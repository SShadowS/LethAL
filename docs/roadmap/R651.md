**Filed 2026-10-22 by lethal-code**, from R-635's run3 (`/coord/handoff/R-635/live/run3/results.md`).

**What.** The CDO slice's serve-entries legs passed, but they remain 2-3x slower than bcdev:

| Leg | Itest total | Not covered by the timings |
|---|---|---|
| E3a (secondEcho on) | 164.0 s | about 145 s |
| E3b (secondEcho off) | 200.3 s | about 170 s |
| bcdev | 53-80 s | |

- The report's `timings` hold only generate, deploy, baseline and mutants. `mutantsMs` is a SUM over mutants
  (562 s and 657 s), not wall time. So the remaining ~145 s and ~170 s cannot be split.
- The candidates are the union warm-up, the one prefetch request, the 102 live requests (51 kill confirms and 51
  reproductions), the 37 §5 coverage requests, and the 74 echo requests (37 with secondEcho off).
- Without that split, the next speed step cannot be chosen by measurement. The choices are: prefetching the confirm
  and the reproduction, batching the §5 windows per chunk, or dropping the second echo.

**To close.**
- Add per-phase wall timers to `AlRunnerV3Backend` (warm-up, prefetch, live, coverage window, echo), reported through
  `entriesStats()` and the itest's `entries` line.
- Re-measure E3a and E3b on the CDO slice, with per-mutant equality held as in R-635.
- File the speed step the numbers point at, with its own pre-commitment.
