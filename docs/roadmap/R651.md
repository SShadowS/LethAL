| Leg | Itest total | Not covered by the timings |
|---|---|---|
| E3a (secondEcho on) | 164.0 s | about 145 s |

- The report's `timings` hold only generate, deploy, baseline and mutants. `mutantsMs` is a SUM over mutants
  (562 s and 657 s), not wall time. So the remaining ~145 s and ~170 s cannot be split.
- The candidates are the union warm-up, the one prefetch request, the 102 live requests (51 kill confirms and 51
  reproductions), the 37 §5 coverage requests, and the 74 echo requests (37 with secondEcho off).
- Without that split, the next speed step cannot be chosen by measurement. The choices are: prefetching the confirm
  and the reproduction, batching the §5 windows per chunk, or dropping the second echo.

**Done (e1cd0cd8, 2026-10-22).** The serve-entries backend now times each phase:
- `entriesStats()` holds `warmupMs`, `prefetchMs`, `liveMs`, `windowMs` (which contains `coverageMs`
  and `echoMs`), and `boundMs` with `boundChecks` (the `assertBound` checks in the mutant loop).
- These are TIMING ONLY: no verdict reads them. A test runs the same scenario under a still clock
  and under a fast-moving fake clock and gets the same verdicts.
- The itest prints them in each leg's `split ms:` line, beside the orchestrator's deploy, baseline
  and mutant-loop walls (`phase-left`). It also prints `other = loop - (warm-up + prefetch + live +

**Reading the report's durations.** On serve-entries a verdict's `durationMs` is the wall time of
the REQUEST that produced its row, not the mutant's own cost. All 425 prefetched rows carry the one
prefetch's wall time. So `timings.mutantsMs` (a sum over mutants) exceeds the loop, and the
per-mutant median and p95 are not cost figures. Read cost only from the split line.
