Filed and closed by coord PR-43 (2026-11-15), the follow-up of [[R1055]] (PR-41). Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-pr43-v3-cpu-stop-skip.md` (addenda 1 and 2). B2 settled from al-runner's source (upstream
#8168, comment).

**What.** R577 re-ran every v3 timeout kill under the mutant to confirm it, a second serve timeout (60-180 s in DO) plus a daemon
restart (~32 s) per hang. al-runner's limit is the test thread's CPU time, so machine load cannot cause the stop.

**The rule.** At the cold position-1 confirm only, the reproduction is skipped when all hold:
- the killing row has `clock: "cpu"`;
- its `durationMs` is below 10 x N x 1000 (al-runner's wall backstop and no-clock fallback print the same row; the backstop cannot
  fire earlier);
- the unmutated NONE confirm of that test passed with its own time (else the call's wall, since al-runner omits a zero
  `durationMs`) at most N/4.
Anything else reproduces as before; warm groups and bcdev always do. Resume and the every-kill-reproduced checks accept the
`v3-cpu-stop` evidence.

**Left open.** No v3 baseline holds a timeout kill (hang sites stay in `itest:hang`, R164), so the gate cannot exercise the skip
live. A future v3 hang leg would also need the itest's killer-rows-per-kill figures to allow one row for such a kill. If al-runner
gives the backstop a distinct `clock` value (asked on #8168), the 10 x N rule can go.
