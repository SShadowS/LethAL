Found by coord task R-927 (2026-11-11), Email - SMTP Connector's v3 sample on serve-entries (`/coord/handoff/R-927/smtpconn.sample.*`).
Related: [[R915]] (the same cause, fixed for the estimate and the window cut only), [[R914]] (the TIMING total).

**What.** [[R915]] found that on al-runner a test verdict's `durationMs` is the whole call's time, not the test's own, and made the
estimate and the window cut read `measuredDurationMs ?? durationMs`. A mutant's own `durationMs` in the report (the summed test time
it was scored by) and the TIMING line's "mutants' summed test time" still sum `durationMs`. So each covering test adds the whole
call's time:
- 14 of the sample's 87 mutants report `durationMs` 174,390 ms: 15 covering tests at 11,626 ms each, the one call's time. The whole
  session took 157.7 s and its mutant phase 21.7 s.
- The TIMING line reads "the mutants' summed test time, 3804.0s, is more than that because their tests overlapped". With one serve
  daemon, the 3,804 s is the call times repeated, not overlap.

Verdicts are not affected. A reader who sorts mutants by cost, or reads the summed time as work done, is misled by up to the number
of covering tests.

**Invented repro.** R915's: a suite of 10 tests of 5 ms each in one al-runner call of 60 s. A mutant covered by all 10 reports
600,000 ms.

**To build.** Report a mutant's summed time from `measuredDurationMs ?? durationMs`, as the estimate does, or say in the report that
on al-runner it is call time; reword the TIMING line so it does not claim overlap where it cannot know. Red both ways.

**Fixed (coord R-928).** The mutant's summed time (`spent`, the orchestrator's covering-test loop) reads `measuredDurationMs ??
durationMs`, as the estimate does, and al-runner's rows now keep the runner's own figure on a `fail` as well as a `pass` (kills
were the worst case: Retention Policy's kills read up to 444 s, mostly 79 to 218 s, the call's time). The baseline event still
carries an own time on a pass only (its contract), now by an explicit outcome check. A timed-out, refused or errored row still adds
its call's time; the TIMING line now names both causes ("tests overlap (prefetch, lanes), and on al-runner a test that timed out,
was refused or errored counts its whole call's time") instead of overlap alone. bcdev sets `measuredDurationMs` equal to `durationMs`, so its figures
do not move. The other readers checked: the estimate and the R912 windows already read the own time (R915); the timeout budgets
keep the call's `durationMs` by design; the progress median, the report's per-mutant distribution and `mutantsMs`, and a resumed
mutant's `priorDurationMs` all read this one sum and are display only; the R517 confirm judge reads `measuredDurationMs` only
inside its `pass` branch. The per-test rows in the store keep the call time. Tests, each red both ways: `resume.test.ts` (a
survivor's and a kill's time are their covering tests' own times), `r272-covering-tests.test.ts` (a fail row keeps the own
figure), `orchestrator.test.ts` (the baseline event carries it on a pass only) and `timings.test.ts` (the wording).
