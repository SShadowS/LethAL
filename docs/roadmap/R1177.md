**Found by** coord PR-338 (lethal-code, 2026-11-22), from c2's process samples (`current` main-ccd0f823d, full DO, about
69 MB of instrumented .al). Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-338/rss.txt`.

**The numbers:**
- **A serve daemon's RSS at a request's start** (median GiB), by its request number:
  - 2.59 for requests 0-24, 2.67 for 25-49, 2.71 for 50-74;
  - 3.13 for 75-99, then 3.21-3.24 flat to request 400.
- **The longest daemons** (up to 5,501 s) stay at 3.2-3.3 GiB.
- **Within a request,** the transient on top is 0.18 GiB median and 0.8 p90.

**Why it matters:** the lane gate charges a lane its peak, so the retained 0.6 GiB is about one lane of 8 at `--peak 22`
(R1176).

**The question for alrunner:** what does a daemon retain between requests (compiled entries, test-codeunit caches, kept
workspaces)? Can it be capped, or released at a request boundary? LethAL's own fallback is R1176's recycling.
