**Found 2026-10-05 while building R464** (plan
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-R-464-implicit-record-resolver.md`, r3/r4 "cost" and close-out).

**What.** `validate-to-assign`'s bare form (`Validate(F, V)` with no receiver) writes the assignment
with a synthesized receiver prefix, and since R464 only where that prefix is PROVEN to bind the
call's record (`bareReceiverText` in `packages/engine/src/semantic/receiver.ts`). Where it is not
provable, the site is refused: 35 measured sites.
- 29 the tool mutated before: Intrastat 23 and SAF-T 3 (bare `Validate` in a tableextension of a
  table outside the project, so the extended table's fields are unknown), BaseApp 3
  (`IntrastatSetup.Table`: the table is not indexed; `SalesHeader.Table` x2: a `Rec :` declaration
  shape is visible in scope).
- 6 new claims not taken: CDO 2 (`CDO Update Customer Setup` report, dataitem over the dependency
  table `Customer`), Intrastat 4 (`with` over a dependency-table subject).

**Do not just emit a bare `F := V`** (sol's review of R-464 r3): an assignment's left side binds
differently from Validate's field designator. A parameter, a local, or a table/tableextension
global named like the field captures the bare assignment (`procedure P(Amount: Decimal)` then
`Amount := 1` writes the parameter); a page global alone does not (R294). And Validate's binding
of a shadowed first argument was never measured (R136).

**To close.** Prove the field name's ASSIGNMENT binding at the site instead of the receiver
name's (explicit `with` first, the most promising), measure the shadowing controls with `alc` and
at runtime, then re-measure how many of the 35 come back. All 35 recovering is not established.
