**Filed 2026-11-23 by lethal-bugs (coord task PR-357), on the orchestrator's word.** Evidence: `/coord/handoff/PR-357/c3/`
and `/coord/handoff/PR-357/c3off/` (`events.ndjson`, `box.tsv`, `time.txt`, `stderr.txt`). Both runs are the full DO
suite on master ec78cff18, al-runner `current` main-bf3a3e863, 5 of 8 lanes, pinned to CPUs 0-11; c3off differs only by
`LETHAL_V3_CARRY_WALK=off`.

**What was measured.**

| run | `phase-left teardown` (atMs) | `recheck-baseline-finished` (atMs) | gap | recheck baseline (tests, elapsedMs) | then |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c3 | 14,050,363 | 14,924,585 | **874 s** | 593 tests, 386,089 ms | 369 `mutant-rechecked` in 48 ms, `session-finished` |
| c3off | 13,568,142 | 14,580,660 | **1,012 s** | 593 tests, 438,134 ms | 369 `mutant-rechecked` in 52 ms, `session-finished` |

- **No event between teardown and `recheck-baseline-finished`.** A user watching progress sees the run "finished" (the
  teardown phase is left, in 0 ms) and then nothing for 15-17 min. The stderr summary and `report.json` come only at the
  end.
- **The client works through it.** In c3off at 06:46Z (3 min into the gap): about 3.2 cores busy (316 CPU ticks in 10 s),
  RSS 2.2 GiB, 27 threads. In `box.tsv` the process tree falls from 15.8 GiB to 3.3 GiB at 06:45Z (the main pass's daemons
  closed), then grows back to 15.5 GiB at 06:55Z (the recheck's daemons). No stack or perf sample was taken.
- **Only part of the gap is the recheck baseline.** 386 s of 874 s (c3) and 438 s of 1,012 s (c3off). The other ~490-575 s
  is not attributed. It is likely the recheck's daemon starts (each compiles the instrumented build, about 35 s on DO, R1183),
  but this is not measured.
- **Not a carry-walk-off effect.** Both runs show the gap, with the same test count. c3off's is 138 s longer. Other jobs
  loaded CPUs 12-21 then (load1 up to 299 at 06:51Z, `cpu12_21_busy` 0.96).
- **The speed figures already include it.** The run's own `elapsedMs` (`session-finished`) counts the gap, but no phase's
  `elapsedMs` does: the phases sum to 14,041 s of c3's 14,922.6 s.

**What to look at (not lethal-bugs' to fix; the orchestrator hands it on).**
1. Make the recheck visible: a phase of its own (`recheck`) or at least a start event and progress during its baseline, so
   that "teardown left" is not read as the end of the run.
2. Attribute the unexplained ~500 s (daemon starts, the candidate set's build, anything else) before deciding whether to
   cut it.
