# PR-287 and PR-284 pre-commitments (lethal-bugs, 2026-11-21), written before either runs

## PR-287: the 174 DO Company-table mutants scored on BC (bc4) against al-runner's CRONUS verdicts (PR-283)
- **What runs:** LethAL's bcdev backend on DO (the PR-67 config, BC 29 W1 on bc4 with Continia 30, the test app 30.0.0.1 from
  `do-test-new`), scoped with `--only` the 10 files plus `--lines` at the 174 mutants' 127 lines.
  - That is 277 mutants by the dry run: the 174 plus their neighbours, which are ignored in the comparison.
  - It always runs with `--stop-hung-sessions`.
- **Order (one command, `/coord/handoff/PR-287/go.sh`):**
  1. **The v3 hang check on the same scope** (`current`, PR-67/v3.json). It must show **0 timeout-killed**, or nothing runs on bc4.
  2. Lease bc4 (only if free), doctor.
  3. The **safe arm:** the 23 covering test codeunits other than 68986 and 69001.
  4. The **risky arm:** 68986 and 69001, the safety list's codeunits (Commit, company create/delete), the owner's one batch
     (q-20261121T095043-72299f6f).
  5. Doctor after each arm. A failed doctor, a run that returns 401 on every call, a hang, or "begin transaction world not called"
     stops the script with the lease KEPT. Then ONE bc-restart ask goes through kraken. The lease is released only after a healthy
     doctor.
- **The comparison, per mutant, for the 174 only:**
  - BC's verdict is the bcdev run's verdict from the arm that ran the mutant's covering tests. A mutant covered by both arms is
    killed if either arm kills it, and survived only if neither kills it and both measured it.
  - al-runner's verdict is PR-283's CRONUS column (/coord/handoff/PR-283/scored.json: 156 killed, 18 error).
  - Reported:
    - the 2×N table (al-runner vs BC);
    - every disagreement by mutant;
    - specially, any mutant al-runner calls killed that BC calls survived (a false kill on al-runner), and any al-runner error
      that BC scores.
- **No prediction of BC's verdicts is made.** BC is the oracle here: this is a measurement, not a gate. A false kill on al-runner
  is a finding, filed upstream under the owner's rule.

## PR-284: PERF-33, main ccd0f823d vs perf-combined-2 e3a1bae7c (PR-261/PR-265's shape)
- **Binaries:** both built with upstream's recipe under /coord/handoff/PR-284/target-{before,after}-{plain,fp}:
  - before = `+perf33before (ccd0f823d)`, plain sha256 0a6e0885…;
  - after = `+perf33after (e3a1bae7c)`.
  Each round logs its binary's version and sha256. A wrong binary stops the comparison.
- **Runs (one command, `/coord/handoff/PR-284/rounds.sh`):** interleaved rounds before, after, before, after (PR-223's 90 mutants,
  one daemon each, a fresh cache, perf stat instructions:u, heavy-slot --peak 6, CPUs 12-21). Then a 60 s warm frame-pointer
  profile of each. A round with a suspend inside is rerun.
- **Verdicts first:** every mutant's verdict and killing test must be IDENTICAL before vs after. Any difference is reported by
  mutant BEFORE any timing, and the timing is then reported as conditional on it.
- **Then:**
  - instructions:u and warm testsMs and wall per request, per arm, the mean of 2 rounds;
  - the profile shares PERF-33 names (inclusive, of all samples and of tests): MemStore::count, flow_op, alt_next, format_inner
    under alt_next, Mat::walk, Mat::passing, media_of_id, Providers::next, MemStore::next.
- **No timing prediction:** alrunner's ask is a measurement. A slower "after" is reported, not blocked.
